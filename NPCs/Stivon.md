*Helena's son, Estorian guard*

## Summary

Helena's son, a guard on Estoria's gate, and Kaz's old barracks-mate. A gambler and a flirt who can't stand Kaz anywhere near his sister. He ran the guard's code black meeting after the Titan, believes Athalea conjured it, and broke Kaz's nose with a shield for kissing Stivona, and the next day held a spear to his throat for round two.

## What the Party Knows

- **On the gate** when the party arrived ([[Sessions/Session 10 - By Not Being There|Session 10 - By Not Being There]]). Wants the ten gold Kaz owes him for medical bills (Kaz fell on him with Betsy in a drill); shot Kaz in the left testicle with a longbow
- Flirted with [[The Party/Athalea|Athalea]], invited her to the play, and offered his place (really his mother's; he lives in the barracks). A degenerate gambler and womanizer by Kaz's account; runs dice nights (*"No money, no honey"*). Hates that Kaz is around his sister
- Athalea's date for the play; promised Kaz extra guards in [[NPCs/Anora|Anora]]'s dungeon ([[Sessions/Session 11 - With Friends Like These|Session 11 - With Friends Like These]]); ran to the palace with [[NPCs/Stivona|Stivona]] when the centaur ultimatum came out — code black needs the king first
- **[[Sessions/Session 14 - Ladies and Gentlemen, the Oracle's Chosen|14]]:** had warned the palace guard Kaz might try to *infiltrate*; unscrewed Kaz's spare horn; ran the **code black** meeting (bigger weapons, giant crossbows, the old smithy, *artifacts*)
- Home for his gear, told Athalea that scaring people is an act of war, and about the missing young healer and the old *Whisperer*; thought a god might help [[NPCs/Kyrah|Kyrah]]
- Believes **Athalea conjured the Titan** and wants to replicate it. Counts five Oracle's chosen
- Saw Stivona kiss Kaz. At dawn he hit Kaz with a shield (nat 20, broken nose): *"You kissed my sister."* Chased him through the city in his underwear and ended up screaming at the palace guards
