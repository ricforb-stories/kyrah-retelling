*The woman in the split tree, one of Darius's order*

## Summary

A woman of Darius's order, nailed into a tree split down the middle beside the burned farm, left there as a message. Darius last saw her as a little girl, ten years ago, wearing armor like his own. *"She's my family."* The party burned the shrine with her in it, and Darius offered it to Sydon.

## What the Party Knows

- Kyrah found her: a big tree split in two, a woman nailed into the split, blood running down; snakes growing from the ground among the bluish flowers; a bird nailed to her chest; in her hand a horn very like Kaz's ([[Sessions/Session 02 - You, or Your Crew|Session 02 - You, or Your Crew]])
- Carved into her face, in cipher: *"Swear an oath here, or everyone you love will suffer the same."*
- [[The Party/Darius|Darius]] knew her: a girl of his order, last seen ten years before, before the ritual. She should never have been left unarmored, even in death. *"She's my family."*
- Thunder rolled over the ocean when they asked whom the shrine was for. They burned it as it stood, wrapped in cloth from [[NPCs/Philon's Family|Philon's Family]]'s house; Darius offered the burning to Sydon, who asked *"You, or your crew?"*
