*Palace guard, Stivon and Stivona's mother*

## Summary

A palace guard and the mother of Stivon and Stivona, who keeps her house by the rules of hospitality. She took Athalea and Darius in as guests, told them about the siege and the Order of Sydon's demands, and now has an oak tree growing through her kitchen. Kyrah lies unconscious in her house. She runs the king's war council. After the kiss she beat Kaz to his last breath, dismissed him from the guard and forbade him her daughter, and she has closed the palace to the party until each of them explains themselves after the meeting with the Order of Sydon.

## What the Party Knows

- **[[Sessions/Session 10 - By Not Being There|Session 10 - By Not Being There]]:** escorted the party from the palace to the royal stables and to her house, where [[The Party/Athalea|Athalea]] and [[The Party/Darius|Darius]] are staying and the stag was cooked for the poor; pleased that her son found guests who keep the rules of hospitality; gifted the stag skull. Asked Darius if he had a date for the play — *"You're going with my daughter"*
- **What she told them:** Estoria has been under a not-officially-a-siege for almost half a month; the southern gate from Mytros still lets a few merchants through; plenty of wine, no food. The six cyclopes came with the siege, destroying houses and taking supplies; either someone directs them or something bigger drove them out. She once met a cyclops that killed almost her whole group — they ran. Months ago the **Order of Sydon** came to the palace and it went badly; Estoria hasn't sacrificed enough for its size, has no temple of Sydon (only the temple of the Five and a temple of the dragons), and isn't devoted to either Titan twin; the demands keep growing. *The walls have eyes and ears*
- **[[Sessions/Session 11 - With Friends Like These|Session 11 - With Friends Like These]]:** pinned Darius in her kitchen as Stivona's date and saw through his lie (*"Your helmet is twitching"*); an oak burst through her roof as he fled; furious, then talked down by Athalea (Persuasion 25); *"Nature will take its course"*
- **[[Sessions/Session 13 - Don't Shoot the Snake|Session 13 - Don't Shoot the Snake]]:** Athalea brought the unconscious Kyrah to her house. Stivona says her mother *set her up with a medusa*
- **[[Sessions/Session 14 - Ladies and Gentlemen, the Oracle's Chosen|14]]:** Away all night; Athalea put Kyrah in her house. In the morning she was at the palace, in a room with ordinary guards, in a **war council**; shouted through the door she'd be free in half an hour. Kaz found her by smell
- **[[Sessions/Session 15 - A Lot of Beating for One Kiss|15]]:** Runs the king's **war council** in the palace. Told Athalea the Order of Sydon has spies in the city and Estoria has spies in the Order; the Order's signs call for **the great sacrifice** and for **getting rid of the Oracle's chosen**. Invited the Oracle's chosen to the **meeting with the Order's delegates in two days**. Her map shows an **evacuation** of the city. The king is not receiving (*"Kings get tired"*); she doesn't believe Kyrah is the goddess (*"I trust my sources"*) but carried Athalea's message in (Persuasion 25): *"He heard your message very well."* Sent them to the Whisperer and asked them to look into the missing young people. On Stivona: she set up dates *to get her out of the house*; Stivona wants *to leave Estoria and build her own greatness*; she and Stivon are the same age. Choked Darius unconscious in the dungeon, then beat Kaz to his last hit point in the war room: refused her blessing, **dismissed him from the guard until the end of his crusade**, banned him from her daughter on pain of exile: *"I will not let my daughter end up like your mother."* Unrolled Darius naked from a hide, **revoked the party's invitation to the palace**, and wants explanations from each of them after the meeting. Her house is still open to them
