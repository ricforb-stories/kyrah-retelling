*Dwarf copper miners*

## Summary

Two dwarf copper miners from the Dragon's Tooth tavern, drunk under the table by Darius. They warned that the Mithral mines are cursed, pointed the party to Volkan's Forge, and offered to show them the copper mines at four in the morning.

## What the Party Knows

- [[The Party/Darius|Darius]] drank them under the table on Dragon's Breath ([[Sessions/Session 11 - With Friends Like These|Session 11 - With Friends Like These]]): *"Mighty body you've got there. Never get drunk? That's a curse if I know one."* They went home arguing over who was escorting whom, and took Darius with them
- **The Mithral mines are cursed**; their people fled them long ago and now break their backs in the copper mines for nothing. They wouldn't advise going
- For anything about the forge, ask at **Volkan's Forge**, the city's weaponsmith — they know everything
- They'll show the party the copper mines: **meet at the gate, four in the morning, sharp**
- *"Do not toast like that in this city"* — people were staring
