*Pythor's general*

## Summary

Pythor's cold, quiet general, who met the party at the gates and brought them to the palace whether they liked it or not. He speaks for the king with the Order of Sydon's seven captains.

- Pythor doesn't speak to "terrorists", so Gallios deals with the Order of Sydon's **seven captains**, due at the palace two days after the party arrived
