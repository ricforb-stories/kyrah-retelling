*Battered hunters who had seen the white boar*

## Summary

Badly hurt hunters in the tavern, better armored than anyone else there, who had seen the cursed white boar run straight through their camp. They told Athalea everything they knew, paid her with a silvery feathered trinket for healing their friend, and cooked the party a farewell breakfast of stew.

## What the Party Knows

- **The boar** ([[Sessions/Session 01 - Speak, Memory|Session 01 - Speak, Memory]]): huge and ghostly white, black bloody eyes, about the size of a highland boar; studded with hunters' spears and still fast; no blood on it, a faint glow; agitated, roaming frantically; drawn to rotten fruit, especially. It ran straight through their camp a few miles south, some days before
- Between them a broken arm, a broken leg and a bad flesh wound; [[The Party/Athalea|Athalea]] treated their friend and lectured them on cleaning needles with alcohol
- Paid her with a **silvery trinket with feathers and a goddess's symbol**, found over the summer, which they thought had belonged to a healer
- Farewell breakfast of stew *"from the bottom of their simple hearts"*; they still didn't see why anyone would pour good drink on a cut ([[Sessions/Session 02 - You, or Your Crew|Session 02 - You, or Your Crew]])
