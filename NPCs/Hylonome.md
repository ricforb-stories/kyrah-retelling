*The centaur huntress, leader of the patrol*

## Summary

Leader of the centaur patrol and the voice of the herd at Estoria's gates; she carries a sheaf of spears, wears animal skulls, and can become a giant scorpion. She demanded the cursed one and a champion, killed Darius at the Vigil, and after the figure walked over the city agreed to fight Estoria's champion in two days, at dawn.

## What the Party Knows

- **Met [[The Party/Ellion|Ellion]]** ([[Sessions/Session 11 - With Friends Like These|Session 11 - With Friends Like These]]): a volley at his feet; *"Prove you are."* She became a scorpion, he a giant wolf spider, and the bows came down. *"What do you do far from home, child of Thylea?"* Did not yet accept his wine
- **The cursed one:** told he travels with a cursed one — *"Bring him to us. We shall purge him from the land. Once you do, we shall see it as a gift."*
- **The champion:** not interested in *blasphemous* Estoria's champions; wants proof the city has one. **Terms: before sundown that night, bring the cursed one and the champion — or be out of the city by sundown. "We will find him ourselves."**
- **The Vigil** ([[Sessions/Session 12 - She Is Listening|Session 12 - She Is Listening]]): met Ellion at the north gate at sundown with the herd behind her. Tested Darius in the Naming (*"What name do you give me, champion?"*); when he answered with the ritual she gave her name, **Hylonome**, and stepped back; mocked the Grey Plain (*"is the Mother's will against us now?"*). Liked Ellion's approach; called Kaz *the one cursed by our father* and *cursed human*; demanded chalices, then Kaz's horn. Looked at the lightning-scorched ground and dropped it. At the Proof, became a giant scorpion and stung Darius twice through the chest. *"I assume that's it for the champion of the Great Mother."*
- **The herd:** 1,041 centaurs at the gate, all armed, no children (Athalea's count)
- **After the Proof** ([[Sessions/Session 13 - Don't Shoot the Snake|Session 13 - Don't Shoot the Snake]]): watched Darius rise (*"That is the champion. I still see no curse"*) and then show his snakes; knelt with the herd when the figure came. Accepted: *"We shall fight your champion. We shall meet you in the city in two days, at dawn."* Walked off with her hand on Ellion's shoulder; stopped when he let slip the princess and asked why he thought anyone would take her; believed his story of the Oracle's vision, and took his word that there should be no fight. Sent a centaur to escort him home to the Old Wood
- **The cyclopes** have joined the ranks behind the herd
