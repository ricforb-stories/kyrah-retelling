*The hag of the grotto*

## Summary

The hag who held Versi captive in the grotto under Sydon's temple and sacrificed the acolytes, with skin bubbling green and blue. Her single word, *"Die"*, nearly killed Kaz. Mid-fight she turned to stone and Kaz shattered her; a torn parchment in her remains gave her name.

## What the Party Knows

- **First seen** ([[Sessions/Session 06 - The Door That Didn't Ask for Consent|Session 06 - The Door That Didn't Ask for Consent]]) by Athalea from the ceiling: a blue-skinned woman laughing without a sound, driving a knife into the last of the captured acolytes, with two fish-folk and Versi held in living water. No boat
- **The fight** ([[Sessions/Session 07 - The Ransom of a Horn|Session 07 - The Ransom of a Horn]]): she pointed a crooked finger and whispered *"Die"* — Kaz dropped and his heart kept beating; on Ellion it failed (his soul was promised to his Mother). Clawed Ellion. Directed the water weird, which was bound to **a vial of water she carried** (Kyrah worked it out)
- Out of nowhere she had to make a Constitution save, failed badly, and turned to stone from the floor up. Kaz broke the statue; the pieces became flesh as they fell. In the remains: the vial (Athalea crushed it; the weird stopped hunting) and **a torn parchment that named her**
- Kyrah's verdict in verse: *"She said one word and people just died. 'Die,' she said. Efficient."*
