*Lutheria's revelers at the roadside camp*

## Summary

A drunken maenad and her three goatling children on the road to Estoria, roasting a dead dwarf as they went. She danced, and everyone fought the pull to join her; the goatlings fought with insults that drew blood; she became a bear. She would not die until Darius's bell tolled for her. Athalea told the party they had been people once, cursed into this by Lutheria. The party buried them all with full rites.

## What the Party Knows

- **The dance:** everyone had to resist joining it every round (Charisma saves); dancers were clawed at the eyes and wrists. Ellion first, then Darius, and [[NPCs/Sir Ferdinand|Sir Ferdinand]] on her hind legs
- **The goatlings** fought with insults that drew real blood; one cried *"I hear you growling, mother!"* and she became a **brown bear**. Athalea told one that its mother had lain with a goat on two legs because she couldn't pull a goat on four
- [[The Party/Ellion|Ellion]], as a giant badger, killed two goatlings and **sacrificed them to Thylea** mid-fight, which blessed the party
- **Athalea's whisper** — ***"Those are mine"*** — was the most terrifying thing the maenad had ever heard, and she ran. Athalea said it twice more, to her face, claiming the party
- **Kaz's struggle cuddle:** he grappled the bear off his dying friends and held her most of the fight. She spoke into his mind (she spoke to everyone but Athalea): she'd have let them go after her fun and her meal, but with her children dead they would all die — *unless* she got a substitute for them. Back in woman form in his arms, she undressed; he stabbed her and tried to dress her. *"You're not my type."* *"That's all right. I can play with all of you after you die."*
- **She would not die:** Ellion saw that everything harmed her; she *walked a thin line between life and death*. **Athalea explained Lutheria's gift** to the pleasure-fey — at death's door, always a chance to stay (History 21)
- **Darius's *toll the dead* finally killed her.** Kaz tied the body up anyway
- **Her territory** was marked with mushroom patterns and trinkets in the trees — dolls and body parts. Six more goatlings came out laughing as the party left
- **What they were:** Athalea told them — people, killed and cursed (or blessed) against their will into this; and Lutheria resents losing her creations. Kyrah: *"A human. Not anymore."* They buried the maenad, her three children and the dwarf with full rites; Ellion laid mistletoe on the two he had sacrificed
- **On her:** a carved drinking horn with grapes, a pretty goblet, and a vial Athalea knows the contents of
