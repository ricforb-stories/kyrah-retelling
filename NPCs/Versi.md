*The Oracle, a naiad*

## Summary

The Oracle: a naiad with the gift of prophecy and gold fused into her skin, who rarely speaks because her words can seal fates. The party rescued her from the hag Helica in the grotto under her temple, and she gave them the great prophecy: three labors, a warning of black fire, and a fate for each hero. She is kin to Ellion. Athalea sent her word that Kyrah is failing, and Versi answered: blood of her blood, then sing, then hurry.

## What the Party Knows

- **Her temple** sits in a steaming chasm many leagues from anywhere; [[NPCs/Kyrah|Kyrah]] was raised with her and serves her; [[NPCs/Proteus|Proteus]] is its senior priest
- **Silent two weeks** before the party arrived ([[Sessions/Session 05 - Praise Sydon, Trust the Winds|Session 05 - Praise Sydon, Trust the Winds]]); taken during a raid on her own temple half an hour before they got there
- **Captive** ([[Sessions/Session 06 - The Door That Didn't Ask for Consent|Session 06 - The Door That Didn't Ask for Consent]]): held above the boiling river in living water, weeping, in golden robes, while [[NPCs/Helica|Helica]] sacrificed the acolytes
- **Rescued** ([[Sessions/Session 07 - The Ransom of a Horn|Session 07 - The Ransom of a Horn]]): hysterical throughout; Athalea pulled her out of the water weird twice; the weird struck her (blood from her mouth, thunder outside); dropped into the boiling river when Helica turned to stone; scalded; flown clear on Athalea's back
- **Why she's silent** ([[Sessions/Session 08 - Keep Me Close|Session 08 - Keep Me Close]]): *"The words I say out loud might seal the fates of the one I've spoken to."* Everything else is gesture
- **Her guardians:** the **rock guardian** on the fruit island — "the carpet(s)", carnivorous, answering only to her, which swallowed [[The Party/Darius|Darius]] ([[Sessions/Session 06 - The Door That Didn't Ask for Consent|Session 06 - The Door That Didn't Ask for Consent]]); and the **water weird**, now hers, mirroring her movements and ferrying (and bathing) the party ([[Sessions/Session 08 - Keep Me Close|Session 08 - Keep Me Close]])
- **The prophecy** ([[Sessions/Session 08 - Keep Me Close#The Prophecy|Session 08 - Keep Me Close]]): the three labors — the silver fires of the lost forge, the weapons of the first Dragonlords, the bottomless dragon horn — and the warning: *"My father's anger cannot be quelled, and his sister schemes even now… the sky will rain black fire, and the doom of Thylea will come."* Then a private fate for each hero
- **To Ellion:** they are kin; she gave him his grandmother's silver locket
- Athalea credited the Oracle when farmers asked which deity sent her food ([[Sessions/Session 11 - With Friends Like These|Session 11 - With Friends Like These]]); Athalea promised to recruit for her temple at the Estorian festival
- **[[Sessions/Session 14 - Ladies and Gentlemen, the Oracle's Chosen|14]]:** Athalea sent her a message by the steam mephit: *Kyrah is unconscious after a giant rock Titan walked over Estoria. Help?* No answer yet
- **[[Sessions/Session 15 - A Lot of Beating for One Kiss|15]]:** Answered Athalea by the steam mephit: *"Versi. Says. Blood. Of. Her. Blood. Then. Sing. Then. Hurry. Question mark? No. No question mark."*
