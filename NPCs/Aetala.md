*Keeper of the tavern where the party first met*

## Summary

The keeper of the Highlands tavern where the four heroes first met, summoned by Kyrah. She fed the house, ran the kitchen where Athalea washed the blood off her hands, and watched the Oracle's chosen interrogate each other over their drinks.

## What the Party Knows

- The party met there for the first time, summoned by [[NPCs/Kyrah|Kyrah]] ([[Sessions/Session 01 - Speak, Memory|Session 01 - Speak, Memory]])
- Hunters and farmers drank there the night the party arrived; the wounded hunter was laid on a cot in the back room near her kitchen
- The party slept there and had a farewell breakfast before the hunt ([[Sessions/Session 02 - You, or Your Crew|Session 02 - You, or Your Crew]])
