*Travelling poet, servant of the Oracle*

## Summary

A travelling poet and servant of the Oracle Versi, who gathered the party by letter and travels with them, writing down their story in gloriously terrible verse. She writes resurrection scrolls in her own blood and has brought the party back from death more than once. She has gathered eight fellowships before this one. Since the night of the Vigil she has lain unconscious at Helena's house, slowly fading. The Oracle's answer for her: blood of her blood, then sing, then hurry.

## What the Party Knows

- **The resurrection scrolls:** she writes them in her own blood, because the proper ink from the Mother Tree is too dear; each takes about a month. She had four; three raised the party after Sydon's lightning ([[Sessions/Session 02 - You, or Your Crew|Session 02 - You, or Your Crew]]), the fourth raised [[The Party/Darius|Darius]] ([[Sessions/Session 08 - Keep Me Close|Session 08 - Keep Me Close]]). **None left**; making another now would cost her years ([[Sessions/Session 11 - With Friends Like These|Session 11 - With Friends Like These]])
- **Eight fellowships before this one.** She let slip in [[Sessions/Session 02 - You, or Your Crew|Session 02 - You, or Your Crew]] that she has gathered heroes for this eight times, and every group did something stupid; *"none of the groups died like that before."* Pressed in [[Sessions/Session 03 - The Wisdom of Mr. Flufster|Session 03 - The Wisdom of Mr. Flufster]]: some brought traps, one fell into its own; the rest is *"your story"*
- **Darius's oath of servitude** is sworn to her: anything she asks, at any point in his life. Called once — no children until he breaks the curse ([[Sessions/Session 04 - You Chose the Wrong Goddess|Session 04 - You Chose the Wrong Goddess]]). She also slapped his cheeks and told him never to lie to her about what's in his heart
- **Temple family:** she grew up in Sydon's temple; its clergy were her family, Bria included. She knew Versi's rock guardian (carnivorous, answers only to Versi) and the hidden passage behind the golden statue
- **Opinions:** Pythor's wallowing is disgusting; the gods who want to help will help without the blade; Sydon *"tried to take your destiny away"*; the Oath of Fellowship is worth swearing (proposed [[Sessions/Session 09 - Those Are Mine|Session 09 - Those Are Mine]], deferred); word of the maenad clearing would wreck their fame
- **Knows** of one other person chosen by the Oracle and raised by her ([[Sessions/Session 11 - With Friends Like These|Session 11 - With Friends Like These]]); that the Horn of Balmytria is in Estoria's reliquary and no one who drinks from it stays sane
- The party believes **the goddess Kyrah** — the goddess of music, whom Kaz drank with and gave his horns to — is a different person who shares her name
- **[[Sessions/Session 15 - A Lot of Beating for One Kiss|15]]:** Athalea told Helena that Kyrah is **the goddess of music, Pythor's sister**, and dying; Helena didn't believe it but took it to the king, and it changed nothing. At Volkan's Hall Athalea recited *"Athalea and the Sea"*; Volkan put his hammer down: *"If that's my girl… gods don't catch cold… she broke the Oath. … She knew the price."* No god will help. Versi's answer: *blood of her blood, then sing, then hurry*. Still unconscious at Helena's
