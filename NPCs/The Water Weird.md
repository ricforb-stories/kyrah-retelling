*Versi's living-water guardian*

## Summary

A living serpent of water in the Oracle's grotto, made to guard Versi and answer only to her. The hag turned it against her: it held the Oracle prisoner in a bubble, glowed an angry purple when Athalea snatched her away, and dragged her back into the river. Once the hag was dead it was Versi's again, ferrying Athalea across the river, holding Kaz up mid-jump, and serving as a very public shower.

- Kyrah: an **enchanted guardian made to protect Versi**, answering only to her. It's carnivorous, can move and can jump. *"Don't touch it."* ([[Sessions/Session 06 - The Door That Didn't Ask for Consent|Session 06 - The Door That Didn't Ask for Consent]])
- Held [[NPCs/Versi|Versi]] in a great mass of water in the tunnel beyond the grotto, with the two [[NPCs/The Fish-Folk|fish-folk]] and the hag around her ([[Sessions/Session 07 - The Ransom of a Horn|Session 07 - The Ransom of a Horn]])
- [[The Party/Athalea|Athalea]] flew straight through it and pulled Versi out. Angry that it didn't have its thing anymore, it rolled after the party glowing a deep purple. Ellion: *"I am nature. Hello, my dear water"* — *it is very weird water. Water should not be walking.*
- **It obeyed its mistress, the hag:** crashed into Versi in Athalea's grip, blood spilled from Versi's mouth, thunder boomed outside, and it swallowed her again and leapt into the river
- **Versi's again afterwards** ([[Sessions/Session 08 - Keep Me Close|Session 08 - Keep Me Close]]): ferried Athalea across the river; took Athalea down the river for a real bath; no intelligence of its own, it mirrors Versi's movements
