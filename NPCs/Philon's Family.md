*The burned-out rye farmers the party helped*

## Summary

A family of eight who lost their rye farm to fire and rumbling from every side the morning the party set out. Kaz cleared their caved-in well stone by stone, Athalea put out the house, and the party hauled their seed out of the cellar. The family knelt and took the heroes in as guests of the house, and wanted to build a temple where their journey began.

## What the Party Knows

- Mother, father and six children aged ten to fifteen; lost the house, barns and fields to fire and rumbling from all sides while gathering breakfast. One boy wondered whether Pythor was angry with them ([[Sessions/Session 02 - You, or Your Crew|Session 02 - You, or Your Crew]])
- [[The Party/Xhazag (Kaz)|Kaz]] cleared the caved-in well with his fingertips, Darius "spotting" him and singing; [[The Party/Athalea|Athalea]] put the fire out with *shape water*
- A boy knelt and invited them in as **guests of the house**; Kaz gave them a gold piece by custom and told them to call him Kaz, not "Master Horny"
- They want to build a **temple where the heroes first helped**; Athalea said maybe in a couple of years
- The children followed [[The Party/Darius|Darius]] around with questions (*was he made of metal? how did he wash?*)
- Their cellar held one strange sack of **mountain flower** seeds they never grew; travelers, two men and a woman, had stayed two nights before and told them all about the party and the Oracle's vision
- They gave Athalea a large cloth for the woman in the tree, and were gone by the next night, leaving seed sacks behind
