*The blacksmith of Volkan's Hall*

## Summary

A withered-looking old dwarf at the anvil of Volkan's Hall, a fortified smithy near the center of Estoria hung with bronze armor, weapons and farming tools. Asked if he is a god, he says *yes*, and goes on hammering. He is Kaz's grandfather. When Athalea recited one of Kyrah's verses he put his hammer down for the first time: if their Kyrah is his daughter, she broke the Oath, and no god will help her. He can forge anything, and Kaz may come to him.

## What the Party Knows

- **Before meeting him:** [[NPCs/Pythor|Pythor]]'s hammer was made by Volkan and *"did not serve me"*; Pythor sees him sometimes ([[Sessions/Session 10 - By Not Being There|Session 10 - By Not Being There]]). Pythor described him as one of the Five, in his tiny forge, proud of things that never worked
- [[NPCs/Brolin and Graham|Brolin and Graham]]: for anything about the forge, ask at **Volkan's Forge**, the city's weaponsmith — they know everything ([[Sessions/Session 11 - With Friends Like These|Session 11 - With Friends Like These]])
- **[[Sessions/Session 15 - A Lot of Beating for One Kiss|15]]:** Met at Volkan's Hall with his apprentice [[NPCs/Theo|Theo]]. *"Greetings, travelers. Do you need something forged?"* *"Are you a god?" "Yes."* *"Kyrah. Lovely name. My daughter's name. How many girls are called that right now?"* At Kyrah's verse *"Athalea and the Sea"*: recognition, then the face of someone hearing a badly tuned instrument; put the hammer down. *"If that's my girl… Gods don't catch cold, and they don't get sick. So if it is her, it means she broke the Oath."* The Oath *takes its due from whoever breaks it. That's the law. I helped forge it. She knew the price."* *"None of us will break the Oath, not even for one another. We are not allowed to guide you, or help you."* On the Titan: *"If you get far enough, you will learn."* On helping her: *"This is not the first time."* Can forge anything *given the proper instruments*. Asked Kaz's mother's name. *"He wants something forged? He may come."*
