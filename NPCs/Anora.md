*Princess of Estoria, Kaz's half-sister*

## Summary

Princess of Estoria, Pythor's "pride and joy" and Kaz's half-sister, kept in a gilded cell under the palace. The Order of Sydon demands she be chained to the Rock of Sacrifice for the basilisks by the next full moon, or the centaurs raze the city. Clever and very formal, she shaped the plan for a champion's contest and still wants to meet her champion, Darius.

## What the Party Knows

- **Sydon's demand** ([[Sessions/Session 10 - By Not Being There|Session 10 - By Not Being There]]): through the Order of Sydon, she must be chained to the **Rock of Sacrifice** outside the city before the next full moon (about two weeks) and eaten by the river's **basilisks**, or the centaurs raze Estoria. The Rock is for the most terrible sacrifices, used only a few times, centuries ago. Word set in stone: her, no substitute
- Kept **in the palace dungeons** so she can't run to the centaurs and surrender — she'd sacrifice herself in a blink (Kyrah)
- **Her cell** ([[Sessions/Session 11 - With Friends Like These|Session 11 - With Friends Like These]]): at the far end of a cave-prison under the palace, reached by a secret stair behind Pythor's statue; rugs, paintings, figures, vases, a barrel of wine, a gilded bed; she was tinkering with some contraption
- **Meeting Kaz and Ellion:** called Kaz *my lord* (nobility by blood); *"You are the Camille! How interesting"*; named Ellion a dryad at a glance — her first. Has met other siblings; Kaz is the first to meet her
- **Her thinking:** Sydon has no motive; his servants read signs. So find a priest who reads them differently, or a substitute dear to the city, or a mass ritual — or have **rain fall at the same moment the basilisks fail to come**; asked Ellion if he could bring rain. Human sacrifices are common, but none in Estoria in her lifetime
- **The threat:** she's certain part of the Order's delegation, arriving in two days, will try to carry her off; the caves have other entrances. She wants out of the city; if nothing works, she'll go to the rock herself. She may have an escape plan
- **The oath plan:** from Kaz's idea of swearing to deliver her to the rock on the day, she set the form — a champion to fight the centaurs' champion, so the gods show the truth. Wants to meet Darius first, and wants the festival play acted out for her (by the bard). Drink from the Horn of Balmytria *after* the champion's fight
- *"You sound just like Father."*
- **[[Sessions/Session 12 - She Is Listening|Session 12 - She Is Listening]]:** at sundown Kaz came back alone. Asked for his oath word for word; said their father's logic is that the dungeon is the city's bunker and safest place. The other ways in are a labyrinth she knows; drew Kaz a map nobody could read; doesn't know where the exits lead. *"Don't worry about me. I'm most probably dead in two weeks."* Heard rumors of Kaz being beaten by a girl at the sparring grounds. Sent him back: *"Don't risk being an oathbreaker. Don't risk dying here for me. I finally got to meet you. So go and make sure that we meet again."* Sees their father in him now (*"Is it the beard?"*)
- **[[Sessions/Session 14 - Ladies and Gentlemen, the Oracle's Chosen|14]]:** Met Athalea. Calls Kaz *Camille*: *"The father-given name is the true one."* Told of Kaz's dream: *"So people know I'm here."* Heard the barracks rumor that the Titan was Athalea's illusion. *"How could you say that of our king?"* Knows of no healing in Pythor; *"He was by my side from the very beginning."* The horn *"is part of our relic hoard"*; access depends on how things end for the Oracle's chosen. Still wants to meet Darius
