*Helena's daughter, Estorian guard*

## Summary

Helena's daughter, a guard, and Kaz's old barracks-mate: a very tall, very muscular half-dwarf and the most ruthless fighter Kaz knows. She has loved Kaz for years. She went to the play on his arm, stood on the wall beside Athalea when the Titan walked, and on the night after finally told him how she felt, and kissed him. The next day Kaz announced their wedding without asking her; she arrested Darius at the Dragon's Tooth, and her mother has forbidden the match.

## What the Party Knows

- **Who she is** ([[Sessions/Session 10 - By Not Being There|Session 10 - By Not Being There]]): very tall, very muscular half-dwarf, from a whole family of bodybuilders; the most ruthless person Kaz knows, and in sparring he half-suspected a demigod. Invented "beard lady" herself, drunk, to annoy her brother. Helena's pick as [[The Party/Darius|Darius]]'s date for the play
- **In love with Kaz for years** ([[Sessions/Session 11 - With Friends Like These|Session 11 - With Friends Like These]]): the kindest, most unselfish person she knows, taller than her, handsome, trains like crazy, and her brother hates him. [[The Party/Athalea|Athalea]] dressed her for the play and braided her own feathers into her hair; Kaz took her, in a suit with flowers. She brought poison daggers
- Ran to the palace with [[NPCs/Stivon|Stivon]] when the centaur ultimatum came out
- **At the Vigil** ([[Sessions/Session 13 - Don't Shoot the Snake|Session 13 - Don't Shoot the Snake]]): Athalea grabbed her to stop a guard shooting Darius (*"Don't shoot the snake"*); she whistled and the guards unstrung their bows. She and Athalea were the last two on the wall when the figure walked over the city
- Afterwards she sat drinking with the few guards left, wide-eyed: *"My mom set me up with a medusa."* *"How do you fight a mountain?"* Kaz left **Betsy** with her and asked her to think up bigger weapons; they meet again in an hour
- **[[Sessions/Session 14 - Ladies and Gentlemen, the Oracle's Chosen|14]]:** in the hallway after the code black meeting, told Kaz he was the strongest, kindest and prettiest person she had ever met, all the years she has known him, since they were kids. *"I feel the same."* *"Oh, shut up."* And she kissed him. Her brother saw
- **[[Sessions/Session 15 - A Lot of Beating for One Kiss|15]]:** Kaz left her a letter when he ran in the morning (*"You found my letter?" "Yes."*). Came out of the tower at the Dragon's Tooth just as the guards wanted Darius's helmet off, and **arrested him** herself (*"Athalea said I could shoot him later"*). When Darius congratulated her on marrying Kaz, she went white: nobody had told her. Dragged him to the palace dungeon with a bag on his head and left her **sword** (her initials) in the alley; Kaz has it. Kaz has announced their wedding for tonight; her mother has forbidden it. Kaz says nothing happened beyond the kiss and sleeping in the same room
