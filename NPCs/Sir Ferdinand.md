*The party's donkey*

## Summary

The party's elderly donkey, a gift from Proteus along with the cart; female, though titled Sir, and called Freddy. To anyone who speaks with animals she sounds like a minor noble, and she claims a University of Mytros education. Kaz is sure she is someone in disguise. She now lives happily in Estoria's royal stables.

## What the Party Knows

- **By her own account** ([[Sessions/Session 08 - Keep Me Close|Session 08 - Keep Me Close]]): educated at the **University of Mytros**, where her duty was carrying the bathwater; too old for that work now, so sent to the temple as a gift. [[The Party/Athalea|Athalea]] once lectured on poetry there, and Sir Ferdinand may have attended. Addresses Athalea as *my lady*; asks for verses to suit the journey; found Kyrah's "truly terrible"
- **Kaz vs the donkey:** Kaz is convinced she is someone in disguise (*"you wouldn't suspect the donkey"*; *"if I pretend to be French, I'll speak French"*); she asks why his cow is staring at her, calls him a brute, and puts it down to jealousy of her shining fur. Ellion's counterpoint: she speaks beast tongue natively. Animal Handling found only an old donkey
- **The maenad fight** ([[Sessions/Session 09 - Those Are Mine|Session 09 - Those Are Mine]]): danced on her hind legs; began glowing pink with faerie fire (cause unknown); was dropped and stabilized. Darius tried to cut her harness with a dagger; Kaz declared that anyone who attacked the donkey would be dealt with first; the maenad never cared about her. Afterwards Kaz carried her on his back
- Too slow to pull a whole stag ([[Sessions/Session 10 - By Not Being There|Session 10 - By Not Being There]]); now in the royal stables of Estoria, the only animal in them, very happy with all the hay ([[Sessions/Session 10 - By Not Being There|Session 10 - By Not Being There]], [[Sessions/Session 11 - With Friends Like These|Session 11 - With Friends Like These]])
- **[[Sessions/Session 15 - A Lot of Beating for One Kiss|15]]:** Kaz visited her in the palace stables: the only animal left, very excited, with only hay. She tapped *one hoof for a good day* for about five minutes. Kaz promised carrots
