*Senior priest of Versi's temple*

## Summary

Senior priest of Versi's temple and its last surviving elder after the raid. He explained why the temple needed outsiders to save the Oracle, pointed the party toward the labors, and sent them off with coins, a cart, Sir Ferdinand, a steam-mephit messenger and the temple's blessing.

## What the Party Knows

- **Bound at the altar** during the raid ([[Sessions/Session 05 - Praise Sydon, Trust the Winds|Session 05 - Praise Sydon, Trust the Winds]]); cried out to [[NPCs/Bria|Bria]] — *"Bria, don't! We can still fix this. Let me free!"* — and Kyrah knew him. She freed him and led him out behind the golden statue
- **Darius took a blow for him** ([[Sessions/Session 05 - Praise Sydon, Trust the Winds|Session 05 - Praise Sydon, Trust the Winds]]): when the green dragon's madness turned [[The Party/Xhazag (Kaz)|Kaz]] on whoever was nearest, Proteus, still tied up, was next; [[The Party/Darius|Darius]] threw himself in front of him and went down
- Kept a poker face through Bria's poisoning; when the party looked again, he was kneeling before the statue and Bria was dead ([[Sessions/Session 06 - The Door That Didn't Ask for Consent|Session 06 - The Door That Didn't Ask for Consent]])
- **After the rescue** ([[Sessions/Session 07 - The Ransom of a Horn|Session 07 - The Ransom of a Horn]]): spent his last healing on Ellion, dressed Kaz's wounds (*"You're in the temple of Sydon; it might bless your soul"*), saw to the murdered acolytes, and explained why the raid needed outsiders: no one in Sydon's own temple would act against it, and a god who intervened directly would break the five-hundred-year-old oath. Without the prophecy the party would never find their destinies
- **In [[Sessions/Session 08 - Keep Me Close|Session 08 - Keep Me Close]]:**
  - walked in on Darius showering inside the water weird, and stared
  - argued with Kyrah over who guards Versi — he's meant to go out and preach, she's ordered to follow the party; Athalea took the recruiting on herself at the Estorian festival (Persuasion 24) and he agreed; visitors should write ahead, siren couriers drop the mail
  - told them the labors' practicalities: the undead gatekeeper **Damon**, paid in special coin; the dwarves near Estoria who know the forge; the Horn of Balmytria in Estoria
  - **walked across the boiling stream on stepping stones of chilled water**, coiled steam into a **steam mephit** and bottled it for them as a two-way messenger (if the bottle breaks, you have to kill it); set mephits to build a staircase out of the chasm
  - **gifts:** five dragonlord coins (one, then four more — *"I do not have much trust that you keep this one safe"*), a pouch of coin, potions of healing, provisions, blankets, the cart and [[NPCs/Sir Ferdinand|Sir Ferdinand]]
  - the temple's blessing: *"Seek neither fame nor fortune, but show them what remains of heroism. Make yourself a beacon of light as we enter the time of darkness."*
