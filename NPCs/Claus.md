*A starving man who sold Ellion an emerald necklace*

## Summary

Claus, a thin, starving man who sold Ellion a beautiful emerald necklace from the Necropolis of Telamok. He went there with an archaeology group and was the only one to come back. Since the sale he can eat again. For one gold and rations he will lead the party to the start of the road to Telamok.

## What the Party Knows

- By a fountain in Estoria ([[Sessions/Session 14 - Ladies and Gentlemen, the Oracle's Chosen|Session 14 - Ladies and Gentlemen, the Oracle's Chosen]]) he sold [[The Party/Ellion|Ellion]] the emerald necklace. He asked **one gold piece**; Ellion paid **two**. He said he *found* it in the old city of **Telamok**, in the **Necropolis**
- He needed money, not charity: he refused gold for nothing, and needed to *sell* it. He has someone to take care of him
- He ate the whole pile of Ellion's food in one go, and it was not a nice thing to watch. *"Thank you. And I'm so sorry."*
- **[[Sessions/Session 15 - A Lot of Beating for One Kiss|15]]:** **Name heard:** the Agora's merchants call him **Claus**; he has been trying to sell the necklace for a week, and they pity Ellion for buying it. He went to **Telamok** with an archaeology group hired by historians; none of the others came back. *"There is a mad thing in there, the keeper of the place."* Gave Kaz the road (three days east, the stone arms, the Pass of Death, the singer, the three bridges and the robed figure with its hand held out, the tombs) and advice: *don't take anything, cover your ears, don't touch the hands, tie yourselves together.* He went into one tomb and starved ever since. Now eating at the Dragon's Tooth. For **1 gp** and rations he'll lead them to the start of the path; **meeting at the Dragon's Tooth tomorrow at sundown**; Kaz gave him an advance. Nobody in Estoria will come near him. *"Same to you, Master Kazak."*
