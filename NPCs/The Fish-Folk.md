*Helica's servants in the grotto*

## Summary

Two hunched fish-folk who served the hag in the grotto beneath the temple. They watched over the captive Oracle, hurled spears at Athalea, and muttered to each other in Primordial about getting out before they died.

## What the Party Knows

- With the blue-skinned hag beside the captive Oracle when Athalea scouted the chamber ([[Sessions/Session 06 - The Door That Didn't Ask for Consent|Session 06 - The Door That Didn't Ask for Consent]])
- One hurled a spear at Athalea, then came at her again; Athalea overheard them in Primordial: *we should move if we don't want to die here* ([[Sessions/Session 07 - The Ransom of a Horn|Session 07 - The Ransom of a Horn]])
