*The sour farmers in the tavern*

## Summary

Sour, frightened farmers in the Highlands tavern who had lost their homes and fields to the boar and said out loud what they thought of the Oracle's heroes: not much. Kaz bought them a round and was called a cow. They swore to keep his drink on the shelf forever, and started whispering again the moment the party walked away.

## What the Party Knows

- Drinking *kon* (barley, rose water and herbs); loudly decided *these* were not much of heroes, and nobody was running to save anyone ([[Sessions/Session 01 - Speak, Memory|Session 01 - Speak, Memory]])
- [[The Party/Xhazag (Kaz)|Kaz]] bought a round of five and walked up flagon in hand. One farmer had never talked to a cow before. *"I am not a cow. I am a minotaur."* [[The Party/Darius|Darius]], asked what he was, said he was just a man
- They'd never seen the boar. One lost his **whole house**, the other his **twenty acres**
- They thanked Kaz with all their hearts and would keep his drink **on the shelf forever**, as a gift from the great heroes. The whispering started again as soon as the party left
