*Enlightened squirrel, now a homunculus*

## Summary

A sugar glider whose mind lit up under Darius's guidance and who led the party to the boar, in exchange for a promise of blood. He died in a pocket, and Ellion raised him with quail blood as Master Flamster, a fire-loving homunculus who burns for Lutheria. He roams the woods outside Estoria and wants Ellion to call him master.

## What the Party Knows

- **Found** ([[Sessions/Session 03 - The Wisdom of Mr. Flufster|Session 03 - The Wisdom of Mr. Flufster]]) by Athalea in the air, lying on her hand like a warm blanket — and, as the party insisted to Kyrah, *abducted*, not met. Touched by Darius's *guidance*: *"I see the light. I see the meaning of things."* Named himself (the First, the Second, the Third). Called them simple creatures and their questions stupid
- **His terms:** Darius's touch, over and over; kept dry; and **the blood of his enemies**, enough to cover him — Ellion promised it. Led them in a wide circle around an unseen beast that was eating something, then north to the boar's country
- **Unravelling** ([[Sessions/Session 04 - You Chose the Wrong Goddess|Session 04 - You Chose the Wrong Goddess]]): unguided in Athalea's pocket, he broke and ran; Ellion held him. His price rose to **a basin, filled to the brim**. When *guidance* faded, the only word left in him — and in the birds — was *fire*. Murmured *fire* on the road ([[Sessions/Session 05 - Praise Sydon, Trust the Winds|Session 05 - Praise Sydon, Trust the Winds]])
- **Dead** — found crushed in a pocket after the grotto ([[Sessions/Session 08 - Keep Me Close|Session 08 - Keep Me Close]])
- **Raised** ([[Sessions/Session 08 - Keep Me Close|Session 08 - Keep Me Close]]): they didn't use the bird-skull blood (magical; they guessed dragon). Ellion refused the nesting mockingbirds, killed two quail with magic stones, and bathed the body: *"I'm sorry, my friend, that I did it too late."* What rose was tiny, batlike, speaking Common: *"I'm finally free." "The death is nothing. The victory is everything." "I shall seek my destiny and make it burn." "I'll be in Estoria. Find me."* Kissed Ellion on both cheeks
- **Master Flamster:** blows fire from his hands; *"Did you know stone doesn't burn? But hair does."* Charred a deer, several foxes and a rabbit within hours; *"Your master returns"*; ate most of the cart's food. Wants to be touched — above all by Darius, for his "magic touch", as divine intervention. Burns *"for Lutheria"*; *"everyone is immortal in the Furious domain."* Argued all evening with Ellion (*"You do not know the price of a good sacrifice"*); Kaz proposed a contest for mastery, and Flamster wanted Darius as the prize. Ellion walked away; *"I know this breakup is hard on you."*
- **What he is:** Lutheria's work, per [[The Party/Athalea|Athalea]] — one purpose given to a struggling thing, eating it; homunculi come from her blood-ritual parties. Athalea whispered he should be ended gently; Ellion refused, since Lutheria would take offense
- **The pit** ([[Sessions/Session 13 - Don't Shoot the Snake|Session 13 - Don't Shoot the Snake]]): came when Ellion called him three times, with a tree that burst into fire and fell across the path. *"Last time you said next time we meet, we're fighting."* Steered Ellion's centaur escort into a hunter's pit with a second burning tree, then burned him alive, laughing, until Ellion asked him to stop. Has heard *the whole fey circle* say Ellion killed babies; offers to keep quiet if Ellion accepts him as **master** (bow, carry, feed). His plan for the siege: lure the centaurs in one by one. Stayed at the pit when Ellion left
- **[[Sessions/Session 14 - Ladies and Gentlemen, the Oracle's Chosen|14]]:** gone when Ellion came back to the pit, and didn't answer his call. In Ellion's dream a small bright flame sat at the lip of the pit, patient as a cat
- **[[Sessions/Session 15 - A Lot of Beating for One Kiss|15]]:** Ellion told Kaz he saw Master Flamster near the city the night before, *kind of okay, actually*; and that a mouse once told him its master is gathering an army
