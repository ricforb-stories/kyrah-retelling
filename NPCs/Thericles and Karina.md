*Vintner and his missing daughter*

## Summary

Thericles keeps a vineyard outside Estoria that still thrives in the drought. His fourteen-year-old daughter Karina went into the Old Wood a week ago and never came back, one of many young people vanishing in those woods. Athalea promised to look for her.

## What the Party Knows

- **Thericles** keeps a lonely vineyard on Estoria's eastern outskirts: neat trellises, healthy vines heavy with purple grapes in the middle of the drought — blessed by Thylea herself when the city was founded, he says. Anyone is welcome. Gave Athalea a bunch of grapes ([[Sessions/Session 11 - With Friends Like These|Session 11 - With Friends Like These]])
- **Karina**, his daughter, 14: long dark curly hair, olive skin, a birthmark on her back, green-and-brown foraging clothes, an herb bag. Went into the **Old Wood** about a week ago on an errand and never came back; last seen leaving by the southern gate, heading south-southeast across the river — where the cyclopes came from
- Tavern rumor: teenagers across the region have been disappearing
- [[The Party/Athalea|Athalea]] promised to look for her, and connected it to her prophecy (Demetria, *the darkest parts of the forest*)
- **[[Sessions/Session 14 - Ladies and Gentlemen, the Oracle's Chosen|14]]:** Stivon: young people keep disappearing in the old woods, mostly kids and apprentices; *something is nesting there*; a search party found no traces. Estoria's own **young healer** vanished a week ago; her grandmother, nearly blind and deaf, was training her as the next *Whisperer*
