*God of Battle, King of Estoria*

## Summary

God of Battle, one of the Five Gods, and King of Estoria; father of Kaz, whom he calls Camille, and of Princess Anora. He drinks, fights his own statues, and says the Oath of Peace keeps him from acting. He made Kaz swear not to free Anora, and promised him his hammer if she is saved. He has stopped receiving anyone; he heard Athalea's message about Kyrah, and it changed nothing.

## What the Party Knows

- **Before they met:** a free spirit who wrote Kaz's mother ten poems ([[Sessions/Session 05 - Praise Sydon, Trust the Winds|Session 05 - Praise Sydon, Trust the Winds]]); "not in the best of moods lately", per Kaz's three-step plan ([[Sessions/Session 03 - The Wisdom of Mr. Flufster|Session 03 - The Wisdom of Mr. Flufster]]); Kaz wanted the boar given to him ([[Sessions/Session 04 - You Chose the Wrong Goddess|Session 04 - You Chose the Wrong Goddess]]); never received Kaz in all his years in the Estorian guard
- **The throne room** ([[Sessions/Session 10 - By Not Being There|Session 10 - By Not Being There]]): very drunk, fighting the statues of the Five with a spear (Mytros's winning). Didn't recognize Kaz until told. Calls him **"Camille"**, the name he gave him; forgot Ophea's name five times; met her "in the steppes"; *"I tried. Three times"* to find her; hugged Kaz off the ground. *"How did you raise me?" "By not being there."* Kyrah told him his wallowing was disgusting
- **Anora:** Sydon, through the Order, demands her for the Rock of Sacrifice and the basilisks by the next full moon, or the centaurs raze Estoria. He has spent every ox he can buy on Sydon's sacrifices; keeps her in the dungeons so she can't surrender herself
- **The hammer:** Volkan made it; it never served him; he'll give it to Kaz to reforge into his own weapon if Kaz saves Anora
- Won't speak to "terrorists": his general **[[NPCs/Gallios|Gallios]]** meets the Order's seven captains, due in two days
- Proposed to [[The Party/Athalea|Athalea]], then tried to set her up with Anora; apologized to her for the Five's conquest of her people — *"we came here after the Dragonlords; the rebellion was already going; they needed someone to lead"*
- **On the Five:** they were bound by an Oath of Fellowship (he loved the idea for the party). Volkan in his tiny forge, proud of things that never worked; Kyrah, who wrote terrible verses about every step and tortured them with it; Vallus, who fought with her mind and beat him at every game; Mytros, who wanted a city. No final battle. He sees Volkan sometimes and Kyrah whenever she travels
- **[[Sessions/Session 11 - With Friends Like These|Session 11 - With Friends Like These]]:** made Kaz swear on Ophea not to free Anora, then **turned completely sober in a second** and led them to her cell through a secret stair under his own statue (a spear-tapped sequence). Gave Ellion free passage and a palace room
- **[[Sessions/Session 12 - She Is Listening|Session 12 - She Is Listening]]:** found shouting at his own statue about failure and emptiness. Refused to release Kaz's oath, and couldn't: *"You do not make an oath to me, son. You make it to Thylea herself. The oath will stand."* He took the *now* in Kaz's oath to mean *as long as Kaz lives*. Doing nothing has been *the deal for the last five hundred years*. *"You're just like me. Like father, like son."* Advice: *"Never make an oath to a god."* Called Kaz **son** instead of Camille. *"I love you too, son. Even if I'm gone, I will still be protecting you and your sister. You might not agree with my methods."* Kept his blood: Kaz never asked for it
- **[[Sessions/Session 14 - Ladies and Gentlemen, the Oracle's Chosen|14]]:** throne empty in the morning. Kaz followed the smell of *wine, blood, sweat and no will to live* to a door in the living quarters held by silent royal guards. Stivon thought a god might help Kyrah; Anora knows of no such power in him
- **[[Sessions/Session 15 - A Lot of Beating for One Kiss|15]]:** Not receiving: Helena says he's *tired*. Athalea's message (his sister is dying at Helena's) reached him; *he heard it very well* and it changed nothing. Something crashed loudly behind his door. The nobles resent his absence and the cracks in the statues of the Five he went at with his spear
