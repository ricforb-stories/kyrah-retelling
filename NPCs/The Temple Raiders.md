*The mercenaries who stormed Versi's temple*

## Summary

A band of mercenaries in mismatched armor who stormed the temple at the bottom of the steaming chasm, killed its attendants and held the priests hostage beneath the golden statue of Sydon, with a robed woman, Bria, healing them in Sydon's name. They fought the party with nets and javelins, broke, and scattered. Some fled across the boiling river and up the walls. Someone hired them, and the party still doesn't know who.

## What the Party Knows

- Guards in full armor met them at the chasm floor as steam creatures burst from the geysers; one spotted Kyrah and shouted *"They're here. Kill them"* ([[Sessions/Session 05 - Praise Sydon, Trust the Winds|Session 05 - Praise Sydon, Trust the Winds]])
- **They were strange:** none seemed sure of what they were doing, except one who was very locked in. [[The Party/Darius|Darius]] wondered whether they were under some kind of control. Their blood was ordinary red
- Eight inside, in mismatched armor: a net came down on Kyrah and Darius; Darius escaped it in style and threw it over two of them. One shoved at Kaz with his shield toward the boiling river; one threw a javelin [[The Party/Athalea|Athalea]] caught and threw back
- [[NPCs/Bria|Bria]] healed them with Sydon's blessing; Athalea insulted one guard's mother all fight, then put an arrow between his eyes
- They broke after Athalea promised they'd drown in their own blood in Lutheria's realm. **Some fled across the boiling river and up the walls.** [[The Party/Xhazag (Kaz)|Kaz]] asked to keep one alive for questioning, and knocked another out rather than kill him
- [[NPCs/Proteus|Proteus]] later explained that whoever wanted Versi silenced had to hire outsiders: no one in Sydon's own temple would act against it ([[Sessions/Session 07 - The Ransom of a Horn|Session 07 - The Ransom of a Horn]])
