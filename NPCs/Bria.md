*Robed healer at Sydon's temple*

## Summary

A robed woman of Sydon's temple who healed the mercenaries raiding it, with Sydon's own blessing. Caught, she poisoned herself rather than explain. She was family to Kyrah, and on her body was a small device whose needle always points one way.

## What the Party Knows

- **At the temple** ([[Sessions/Session 05 - Praise Sydon, Trust the Winds|Session 05 - Praise Sydon, Trust the Winds]]): healed a mercenary with *"Sydon blesses you, my dear"* and sent him back into the fight. The bound priest [[NPCs/Proteus|Proteus]] cried *"Bria, don't! We can still fix this"*; she didn't answer. A fleeing mercenary threw a javelin at her. Left alone, she looked at the party, then at the golden statue, drank a vial and fell; the party made her throw it up and bound her
- **Her death** ([[Sessions/Session 06 - The Door That Didn't Ask for Consent|Session 06 - The Door That Didn't Ask for Consent]]): nobody had hurt her — Athalea saw she'd done it herself. A fast poison in the blood; she had given herself to Sydon with a prayer; Darius stepped back rather than defy Sydon in his own temple. Proteus knelt before the statue as she died
- **On her body:** more poison, and **a small device whose needle always points one way** (Storm-Glass (Bria's Device)), however it's turned
- **Kyrah's family:** Kyrah grew up in the temple; its clergy, Bria included, were her family. *"Nobody would hurt Versi. None of this makes sense."*
