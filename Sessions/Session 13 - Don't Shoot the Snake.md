# Session 13 — October 23, 2026 — "Don't Shoot the Snake"

**Level:** 2
**Party Members in Attendance:** [[The Party/Ellion|Ellion]], [[The Party/Darius|Darius]], [[The Party/Xhazag (Kaz)|Xhazag (Kaz)]], [[The Party/Athalea|Athalea]]

## Our Adventure

### Six Seconds

[[The Party/Darius|Darius]] lay dead in front of the north gate, and the herd was laughing.

[[The Party/Athalea|Athalea]] climbed high, about a hundred and twenty feet up, where her light paint and the dark made her invisible from the ground. From there she sent a *message* into the centaur woman's head, in a voice as close as she could make it to the one they had all heard in their dream. She did not name anyone. She did not claim to be anyone's champion. She was an actor, and she did her best.

It did not stop them. The herd began to advance. The party had about six seconds.

### The Champion Rises

In the centaur woman's head, a whisper: *Just watch.*

Darius began to rise. It did not look like any holy magic. Blood was still running from his wounds; he was paler than before, with the green still smeared on his face; and he did not get up the way a man gets up. He came up like a puppet lifted on its strings, and was dropped onto his feet. He made no sound at all.

The herd took a step back.

*"That is the champion,"* said the centaur woman. *"I still see no curse."*

Athalea flew down to Darius, and on her signal he showed them. He waved, and the snakes under the veil lifted their heads at the herd, and Darius gave them his creepy smile to go with it. [[The Party/Ellion|Ellion]] stood with his back to all of it the whole time.

### Don't Shoot the Snake

As Athalea flew back up she looked along the wall (Perception 19), and forty feet to the right, on the far edge, someone had seen the snakes too, and was taking aim. She flew at full speed and landed on the one person up there big and muscular enough to grab: [[NPCs/Stivona|Stivona]].

*"Don't shoot the snake."* *"What do you mean, don't shoot the snake?"* *"His snake is saving the city. Don't shoot the snake or the world will die."* *"I do not get it."* *"Get it faster. If this snake dies, the attack starts. You can shoot the snake later."* (Persuasion 22.)

Stivona whistled. Up and down the wall, very confused guards unstrung their bows.

### She Walks

Then the drums began. They came from everywhere: from the steppe, and from the city behind them. The festival stopped in the middle of a beat. Whatever was happening in the amphitheater stopped. Every torch inside the city and outside the wall went out at once, and then the stars overhead went out with them.

There was a smell, something very familiar: wine, and freshly turned earth. To Athalea it smelled like home.

The whole herd took five, six, seven steps back, at once. Somewhere in the ranks someone started crying and was hushed.

Behind the wall, something began to move. It was outside the city, and it was as tall as a mountain; next to it the gate tower looked like a toy. A figure, walking toward them slowly, with its eyes closed, stepping over the city. Behind it, across the rooftops, trailed a scythe of crystal.

On the wall, the city guard saw it too. They put their weapons down. One man dropped his spear over the parapet. The rest ran for the stairs, and soon there was nobody left on the wall but Athalea and Stivona, staring.

The figure was murmuring something. It did not sound like words. It sounded like stones falling down a mountainside. And the whole herd went down on its forelegs.

Down at the gate, Kaz wanted to know whether he should pray or run. The scythe was the thing the Lady was always said to carry.

Then the figure opened its eyes. They were black from lid to lid, and there were stars in them. The herd began to scream.

A hand came off the figure and began to move down toward them, slowly. Ellion dropped to his knees. There was no point running: if it meant them harm, they could not outrun it, and if it meant them good, they would be running from the good. *"You cannot outrun God,"* said Kaz. *"Que sera sera."* He went for a drink. His commitment to the wine was very impressive.

The darkness covered them whole. For quite some time they could see nothing at all.

Then the hand lifted, and went back up to the figure, and the figure turned and began to walk away in the direction it had come from: the way that leads to the sea. As it went, the torches in the city came back on, one by one.

Where Darius had stood there was nothing. No body. No blood. Only the grass, and the grass was wet.

### Silence While She Walks

There was no sound inside the city, and no sound outside it. The herd stayed down on its knees and did not look up. Athalea, high above, was the only voice anywhere, whispering.

She fed Ellion lines by *message*: *Silence while she walks. Nothing is decided. Nothing can be decided.* The party compared what they had seen. The air had smelled of wine and turned earth; Athalea committed the smell to memory. The creature had two eyes, black, with stars in them, and was more or less humanlike. Did its power feel like the power Ellion drew on for his own magic? It did not.

Kaz asked about the cyclopes Ellion had seen on the horizon. They were moving. They were joining the ranks behind the herd.

The champion was gone, carried off, as far as anyone could tell, by the Lady herself. And the script said the herd should wait two days for the champion to fight their own. It was time to go off script.

### Two Days, at Dawn

The gates were shut, and Kaz was on the wrong side of them. They considered the sewers (there would be a drain somewhere in the wall, but those are usually barred for exactly this reason) and Athalea dropping him a rope ladder.

Before anyone settled it, the whole army stood up in the same motion. The figure was gone from the horizon.

*"We accept your proposition, child of Thylea,"* said the centaur woman. *"We shall fight your champion. We shall meet you in the city in two days, at dawn."* She bowed slightly, turned, and began to walk slowly toward the steppe. There was a whistle, and the herd began to break up into groups, the way it had come.

The terms were the old trouble all over again: a contest in two days, with a champion who was no longer there. Athalea gave Ellion a bardic inspiration, told him to figure it out, since she could not be in two places at once, and flew off to see to [[NPCs/Kyrah|Kyrah]].

### The Child of Thylea Goes Home

The centaur woman put her hand on Ellion's shoulder and began to walk with him, away into the steppe.

His servant, Ellion told them, was not tough enough to survive the steppe and did not deserve their hospitality. He turned to Kaz. *"I release you. Go back to your tribe, or whatever. I don't need your service anymore."* Kaz, for his part, had wanted a date with Stivona and some wine. *"Do you have a last will? I'll tell the trees you love them."*

Then Ellion tried to get them out of the contest, and said too much. At his words the centaur woman stopped walking, and her group, very confused, stopped around her. Nobody had said anything about taking the princess. Why did he think they would?

He had to explain. The Oracle, he said, had seen a vision: that taking the girl would bring bloodshed between the mortal races and the fey. With the oath worn so thin, and a Titan walking the earth again, the gods themselves might rain justice down and drown the tribes in blood. He had come because he was afraid for the fey, and wanted to save their lives. (19, and Athalea's inspiration made it 25.)

She believed him. Ellion agreed to go home to his circle in the Old Wood, two weeks away. One of the centaurs left the group and motioned for him to start moving. He would be Ellion's escort, for safety.

### Kyrah

Athalea came back to Kyrah, whom she had covered up before she left. Kyrah was very pale. Her shoulders barely moved; Athalea could hardly see her breathing. Her pulse was thin, almost impossible to find, and her body was cold, as if very little blood was moving through it. She was unconscious. There were no wounds on her.

Athalea understood that more magic was a bad idea. Whatever had happened to Kyrah was magical, and it had to be Kyrah's own body that recovered from it. So she took her to [[NPCs/Helena|Helena]]'s house, where they had both been invited to stay and where nobody could harm them, to keep her warm and make her hot broth.

### Emotional Support Betsy

Kaz came back in through the gate. The city was still very quiet, and none of his friends were anywhere. He thought out loud. *"Ellion is gone. So is Kyrah. They're probably together. Darius… we don't talk about Darius. My head hurts."*

He went up the wall. A few guards were still up there, and Stivona, sitting down, which was why he hadn't seen them from below. They were drinking nonstop with their eyes wide open, and not speaking.

*"You guys all right?"*

*"My mom set me up with a medusa,"* said Stivona.

*"Tried to. Didn't succeed."* *"What are you doing with a medusa?"* *"I'm not very good at deception."* It had surprised him too. They hadn't covered it at guard school.

*"How do you fight a mountain?"* *"We need bigger weapons. Much bigger."* She could come up with ideas for bigger weaponry; he was going to check on his sister, his father, and his friends. They would meet back in an hour. His own blanket was still over Kyrah, so he promised to find her an emotional support blanket. *"It's going to be all right. It's gone. No longer here. You can't even see it now."* *"It walked through the walls."*

Then Kaz did something he never does. *"I usually don't do this. Ever. I'll leave this here with you. You can hold it for me."* He left Betsy with her, and sprinted for the palace.

### The Pit

Ellion walked with his escort around the city by the riverbank and into the forest. His plan was to ask to stop and sleep. Instead, on the road, he prayed. He called a name three times: *"Master Flamster. I need you right now."*

There was a creak. One of the trees burst into fire and fell across the path, and there was [[NPCs/Mr. Flufster (Master Flamster)|Master Flamster]].

*"Last time you saw me, you said next time we meet, we're fighting. Bring it here."* *"No. I need your help."* Ellion spoke in a tongue the centaur did not know, and the centaur was busy looking at the burning tree and trying to work out how to get past it. How could they get rid of him? *"By fire."* Without killing him? Centaurs are very tough. But Flamster knew a hunter's pit up ahead. He would steer them both onto it; Ellion only had to jump clear before he stepped on it.

Flamster flew up. There was another creak from the other side, and another burning tree fell. *"We should probably take another route,"* said the centaur, and led them round. Ellion knew the pit was fifteen feet ahead, and got ready to jump. Ten feet. Five.

The branches hiding the pit creaked. The centaur fell, and there was a scream of pain from below. It was fifty feet down, onto spikes.

*"Child of Thylea, are you all right?"* Ellion said he would bring help. *"Don't. There is something in the forest. Hide!"* And from above: *"Indeed, there is something in it. Step away from the pit."*

Master Flamster came down into the pit, and began to burn him alive, laughing all the while. Centaurs are tough, so it took a long time.

*"Master Flamster, could you stop, please?"* *"Huh? All right."* It was a few moments too late. What was left was almost charcoal, blackened hide and a ruined face, and still moving. The windpipe was burned out, so there was no more screaming. The centaur looked up, and part of his lip fell away from his face, and he was still mouthing something. The wooden spikes had burned too, and the wounds were cauterized.

Ellion wanted to get him out and heal him. *"You wanted him in the pit. Why do you want to get him out of the pit? Why was I doing the job if you want to undo it?"* *"Because he's also a child of Thylea. It's not right."*

Flamster had already heard the rumors. *"You killed babies."* *"Where did those rumors come from? Those rumors are false."* *"The whole fey circle says you did."* *"Can you not tell anybody about this?"* That could be arranged, if Ellion accepted Flamster as his master: called him master, bowed to him, carried him, fed him. And how did the fey circle know? *"You wandered onto their territory during their party and killed part of their people while others were watching."*

Something dropped into the pit, and the centaur screamed again. *"Don't touch him."* So had Ellion called him here to kill him, to torture him, or to burn him? *"Just get away from him, because he was trying to take me to the Old Wood."* *"Terrible. What is an Old Wood?"* Flamster had a plan for the rest of the siege: tell the centaur's family something terrible happened to him in the woods, they come to save him, and here is another one for you. And another. One by one.

*"I'm going to go save him,"* said Ellion. *"Master."*

*"Bye-bye. See you."*

Ellion ran for the edge of the forest, toward the city, away from the terrible things happening in the woods, to get help. Behind him, the centaur was still alive at the bottom of the pit, and Master Flamster was still there with him.
