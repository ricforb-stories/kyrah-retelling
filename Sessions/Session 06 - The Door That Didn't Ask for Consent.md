# Session 6 — August 14, 2026 — "The Door That Didn't Ask for Consent"

**Level:** 2
**Party Members in Attendance:** [[The Party/Ellion|Ellion]], [[The Party/Darius|Darius]], [[The Party/Xhazag (Kaz)|Xhazag (Kaz)]], [[The Party/Athalea|Athalea]]

## Our Adventure

### Bria

The fight in the temple had barely ended. [[NPCs/Bria|Bria]] lay by the golden statue of Sydon, convulsing, bloody foam at her mouth; the ropes binding the priests were woven with symbols of lightning and waves. Athalea, sixty feet up, noticed what the others had missed in the heat of battle: nobody had actually hurt her. She had done this to herself.

Darius knelt over her. From his years as a mercenary he knew the symptoms at once: a fast poison, spreading through her blood, seconds from killing her. Magic wouldn't help; the blood itself needed cleansing. The party put their heads together. Leeches would be too slow. Athalea suggested salt water — a saline solution, run through her blood to thin the poison. Ellion, asked to help, declined: he's a druid of nature, and humans are invaders.

Then Darius looked up at the statue. This was the temple of Sydon, and she had given herself to Sydon with a prayer as she drank. If they saved her, whom would Sydon be angry with? He took a step back.

He searched her for an antidote and found only more poison, and one other thing: a meticulously built little mechanism whose arrow kept pointing in one direction, however it was turned. He asked the bound priest — the one called [[NPCs/Proteus|Proteus]], who had kept a poker face through all of this — to help her, and went after the others. When the party next looked, Proteus was on his knees before the statue, and Bria had stopped moving.

### The Swords

Meanwhile there had been footsteps, and a short scream, from the passage where [[NPCs/Kyrah|Kyrah]] had led the freed priests. Kaz ran after it, into a corridor lined with nine doors. In the furthest room to the right he found Kyrah backing out, holding two people in plain robes behind her. The third lay on the floor with his throat cut, and no one else was in the room. On the wall hung a trophy of two crossed ceremonial swords. One was missing.

*"The sword!"*

A longsword shot out from under the bed and cut Kaz, and two more burst from the doors beside him, one going for Kaz and one for Kyrah. They made no sound at all. Athalea's mockery did nothing; they had no brains to hurt. Kyrah went down. Kaz, on 4 hit points after everything the day had done to him, took the one piece of cover available: he stepped into the room, lifted the bed to make sure there wasn't another sword under it, closed the door, and held it shut. The swords didn't have hands. The attendants were stuck outside, frozen with fear.

Athalea flew in above them, whispered Kyrah back up — *"It's really not the time to rest when a sword is swinging in your face"* — and shot the sword that had just cut her down with a natural 20, catching it mid-swing and pinning it into a wooden door. Darius took the frozen attendants by the hand and led them out, *"Follow me,"* and they followed him everywhere after that. Kyrah went down again; Athalea stabilized her, tearing strips from her own dress for bandages (Medicine).

Darius fought one sword like a sparring match, parry for parry, spear against blade. Kaz came out of his room and brought his club, Betsy, round in an arc that knocked the sword out of the fight and stopped a hand's breadth from Darius's face — *"Duck!"* — and bent another into an L, a boomerang. Athalea snapped one apart at the hilt with an arrow. Darius, who had healed Kaz with a touch that landed somewhere around the thigh, swept the last one to pieces with his spear.

Kaz fetched Kyrah and shut the door on that room behind him, and jammed a javelin through the handle of the corridor door in case there were more swords.

### Downstairs

The attendants clung to Darius's bare feet as he moved. They told the party what they knew: the attackers had split. Some had stayed up here with them; three, four, five more had taken [[NPCs/Versi|Versi]] down to the lower chambers maybe half an hour ago — two hundred feet down to an underground grotto that connects to the sea. It had all happened so fast. The key to the downstairs door was in the clerics' room, where the swords had come from.

The attendants would look after Kyrah; the best way for her to heal was to rest and pray. Athalea didn't go for the key. She poured water from a flask into the lock and froze it, and the lock burst. Somewhere far below, very faintly, a small bell rang. *"They know we're coming."*

Ellion lit a flame in his hand for those who couldn't see in the dark, and they started down a narrow passage, ten feet high, crouching. Nobody had a plan. Kaz suggested two of them could put on mercenary armor and pretend to have captured the others; it was pointed out that people with good intentions don't burst locks. Kaz, whose nose is sharper than anyone's, smelled salt ahead — and then, faintly, blood behind them. Someone was following.

Athalea clawed her way back up over the others like a bat and found a pile of fur creeping down the stairs: Kyrah, bandaged and bleeding a little, hiding under Kaz's hide, with a slightly moldy orange for company. There was no talking her into resting. On the way down she told them she had grown up here. The clergy were like family to her — and the woman who had died upstairs had been part of it. None of this made sense. Nobody would hurt Versi. Athalea took her arm over her shoulder to help her keep up.

### The Door That Didn't Ask for Consent

At the bottom was a sturdy oak door, and carved into it, where the top of it would be for anyone of Kaz's height, was the angry face of Sydon. Kaz checked it for traps and wires (Perception 13) and found nothing. He muttered a prayer to Sydon, just to be safe, and pushed.

Sydon's mouth opened and bit him. His hand stuck fast to the door. Six piercing and three poison, then another bite, and Kaz collapsed where he stood, still stuck to it, too big for anyone behind him in that narrow passage to see what was happening. *"The door bit me. It hurts. I'm stuck to it. I'm going down."* Kyrah hid under the hide.

Darius healed him, calling on the divine, and drew a card for it: **the Tree** — perseverance in difficult circumstances. Kaz came back with double the healing. And in Ellion's head, the voice returned, with the same cold phrase aimed at someone new:

***"I cannot decide whether he should live or die."***

His only aim, every turn until this was over, was to attack Kaz. His *thunderwave* hit Kaz in the back, and for a moment, as the thunder boomed, the carved face of Sydon on the door seemed to be laughing hysterically. When Kaz turned round there was no purpose at all in Ellion's eyes, just a club, glowing green, raised over his head.

*"Help me kill the door!"*

It was chaos. Holy fire burned into the door; Athalea, who couldn't see past the others, pushed Darius flat with the side of her bow and put an arrow into Sydon's open mouth. Kaz, stuck, prone, one hand still in the wood, hacked at it with his handaxe on a natural 20, pulling himself up the door with each cut and falling back down on one hit point. Ellion conjured a bonfire under him.

Darius tried to protect Kaz from Ellion. He grappled Ellion, dragged him round to swap places so Ellion would have cover between them, and tied him up — the rope went round both their arms, so they ended up tied together, with the loose end shoved in Ellion's mouth — and pulled them both to the floor. Ellion headbutted him from underneath.

Then Kaz, swinging at the door again, burst into **a cloud of six hundred oversized butterflies**, filling thirty feet in every direction. Nobody could see anything. The door bit him once more, and he felt the life drain out of him, and he stood back up anyway, slowed and dazed and on fire. *"I don't know if I'm dreaming. Few days ago I was summoned by the Oracle to do heroic shit, and now a lady is singing weird things, the bird keeps killing things, and the tree guy is sometimes healing me and sometimes hitting me."* Behind them, Kyrah had started singing their epic poem under her breath.

With the last of his strength Kaz slammed the handaxe into the door, pulled himself up, slammed it in again, and on the third try he climbed through as the door broke apart, and fell into the room beyond. From now on, he announced, he will open every door from five feet away with the far end of Betsy.

Ellion came back to himself remembering that he'd wanted something terrible, and nothing clear about what he'd actually done. Darius untied him. Somebody asked Ellion to put out the fire under Kaz.

### The Grotto

Beyond the door was a grotto: liquid blue light shimmering across the rock, torches, the vaporous mist of hot springs, water cascading into an underground river at the back of the cave. The river was steaming hot — Kaz, whose backside was burned, declined to cool it there. On the small stone islands stood braziers, and one was laid with carpets and bowls of fruit. The only way across was a line of pebbles. There was nobody there.

Kyrah slipped away under the hide, crouching along the left shore toward the tunnel, whisper-singing the words of the party's poem. Athalea caught up to her and pulled the hide back. Kyrah was deeply distressed, but not about any of them; she kept glancing ahead and repeating the words. She couldn't stop singing. They had to go on.

Athalea argued for rest. Darius had more holes in him than a person should, and Kaz was on one hit point. *"If we die, no amount of prophecies will help us."* *"If you die, others can come. If the Oracle dies, we are doomed."* Athalea didn't think anyone would kill a prophet; she was too valuable. Kyrah said only that they would prevail — how was up to them. *"You said last time we wouldn't die because you had a plan." "I did not have a plan." "You had the resurrection scrolls."* Do you have any faith in yourself? You'll need a lot of it if you want to continue.

They settled on an hour to patch themselves up.

### The Rug

While they argued, Darius crossed the pebbles to the island with the fruit and looked for anything useful. There was carpet, and there was fruit. He took a grape and ate it.

The rock beneath the carpet closed over him and began to crush him.

Nobody saw. The others were at the far end of the grotto, and Ellion was still in the butterflies. Darius, at two hit points, lost consciousness inside it, and it went on crushing him.

When they looked round, they were missing someone. Where was Darius? Nobody had seen him since the door. Athalea flew over the islands (Perception 21) and saw that the rock on the fruit island was bigger than it had been; the fruit and carpets were gone. Darius had to be inside.

*"Don't touch it,"* said Kyrah. It was an enchanted guardian, made to protect Versi, and it would respond only to her. It is carnivorous. It could move, and it could jump. Could they bargain with it, offer it fruit or meat? It could eat both. The only way to get Darius out was to get Versi.

### Versi

Versi's room was fifty feet ahead, a nook to the left; beyond it the shore narrowed until only the river remained, running out to the sea. There was nowhere to moor a boat.

Athalea flew up to the ceiling and crept along it in the shadows (Stealth 21) to look. Through the opening she saw a beautiful woman in golden robes stretched out above the underground river, held captive by tendrils of something like animated water, weeping. In the chamber behind her stood a hideous blue-skinned woman, her mouth open in laughter that made no sound, plunging a knife into a terrified human in the same robes as the priests upstairs. Beside her were two creatures that looked part fish and part human.

Athalea whispered all of it to Kyrah by *message*. Kyrah repeated it aloud to the others. *"I don't think they have a boat."*

Nobody knew how to deal with water tentacles. Ellion talks to creatures, not water. *"I'm not even a fighter. I do thinking and reciting and writing."*

The blue-skinned woman drove the knife into the last acolyte. With nobody left to sacrifice she looked at the Oracle, still laughing, and they started to move, Versi carried inside her bubble of water.

*"Guys,"* said Athalea. *"If I die, sing the song for me at least."*

Then Kyrah stepped into their path as they came out of the room, pale and bleeding, and collapsed in front of them. They noticed her, and then they noticed the rest of the party, and began to talk among themselves.
