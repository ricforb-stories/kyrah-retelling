# Session 8 — August 29, 2026 — "Keep Me Close"

**Level:** 2
**Party Members in Attendance:** [[The Party/Ellion|Ellion]], [[The Party/Darius|Darius]], [[The Party/Xhazag (Kaz)|Xhazag (Kaz)]], [[The Party/Athalea|Athalea]]

## Our Adventure

### Where Darius Went

[[The Party/Darius|Darius]], still dead at the session's open, later told them where he had been. In a blink he was somewhere wide and empty: a flat dark-gray plain, dead grass, dead trees, no wind, no sound at all, and a long road running toward a white river. On the far bank, the clouds had broken, and rays of sunlight fell on the other shore. He walked toward it for hours, and the river came closer only very slowly.

Then he began to hear footsteps somewhere behind him, and felt a fear so huge that he wanted to know what was there and did not want to look. He went on. At the side of the road stood an old man, who looked at him and held out a basket, like a tax collector, waiting. Darius understood that he had nothing at all to put in it. The old man shrugged and disappeared.

Darius walked on, and now nothing changed. Hour after hour, the river stayed exactly where it was, and the footsteps grew louder. When he finally turned his head, the road behind him was full of thousands upon thousands of souls, all walking the same way he was, toward him.

### Morning in the Grotto

It was hard to tell morning from night in a cave. [[The Party/Athalea|Athalea]], the least battered of the survivors, woke first, to [[NPCs/Kyrah|Kyrah]] singing some of her verses in a terrible falsetto. [[NPCs/Versi|Versi]] was sitting on top of the carpets, mostly ignoring her, now and then flinching and looking off into the distance.

*"Morning, lady. Are you all right?"* Versi tilted her head. Kyrah: *"She's not much of a speaker, you know."* Athalea told her she didn't need to speak; she could nod, or point. Versi pointed to the other shore, where Athalea's things were. Athalea wasn't much of a jumper. So Versi beckoned with her hand, and a very familiar creature rose out of the river: the water weird. It scooped Athalea up in its tentacles, pushed her through its insides for a few very wet seconds, and spat her out on the far bank.

Athalea wanted a bath, a real one, to soak the blood out of her feathers. The temple had buckets and washcloths. So she asked Versi whether the weird could come with her down the river somewhere it wasn't boiling, and it followed. It didn't seem to have any intelligence of its own. When Versi moved, it mirrored her. About fifteen minutes later Kyrah came down the stairs with a basket full of soap leaves.

Athalea tried to make conversation, and pointed out that Versi had been talking before. Versi answered:

> *"The words I say out loud might seal the fates of the one I've spoken to. A word dropped without caution…"*

That was why she said almost nothing. Up close, Athalea saw that she had dark olive skin melting into patches of gold. It might have been armor, but it was fused with her flesh.

Kyrah had been busy writing. The door:

> *The door did not respect personal space. It bit one cleric and would not let go, until the axe made it let go. So the lesson learned: when Sydon carves an angry door — maybe don't lean on it. There should be a sign. I will write a sign. Probably badly.*

Kyrah had also written about the hag (*"Green and blue and crawling, bubbling rot, her skin looks like abandoned food. She said one word and people just died. 'Die,' she said. Efficient. I respect the pride, but also, ma'am — exfoliate"*). And about [[The Party/Xhazag (Kaz)|Kaz]]: *"Kaz gave his horn again, a second time. I didn't even ask."* She had taken him for a cleric; he had driven a knife into his heart, praying to the goddess of music.

### Milk, Tea and Push-ups

Kaz woke on a cold floor with no idea where his things were, having promised himself not to sleep on the ground. Kyrah wanted tea, or failing that, milk. He could provide neither: female cows produce milk, minotaurs don't. *"You also lost your horn. Twice. To the same goddess."* *"I know."* *"Just so you know."* *"I know. Luckily, I kept my spare."* One was right here; the other was already screwed back on. *"So you are half screwed."* *"Based on yesterday, we are fully screwed."* What he could produce in the morning was push-ups. Nobody could explain biology to anybody. His people, Kaz said, knew little about where they came from; a great deal of their history had been lost during the time they spent as actual cows.

There were fruit on the island, which nobody was touching. The clerics had bread and cheese; Kyrah handed round sandwiches and dried berries. Kaz's hide, the white cat-skin he'd slept on, somehow had food inside it: the moldy orange, and some dried fish.

Nobody had forgotten Darius. His body lay in its armor with flies around it, and it was starting to smell. There was a bet over whether his snakes had survived his death. Kaz went and had a close look: they were still there, not moving.

### One Scroll Left

Then Kyrah sat down by the body.

*"You went to look at fruit. You did not come back. The carpet ate you. That's a fact. I have one scroll left. Just one. You know? Just one. And I don't know when I'll get to use it."* A pause. *"And I'll get to use it now. So wherever you are, however far — hold on. I'm still not ready. Please hold on. I need to get my tea."*

She went out and came back. And Darius took his first breath.

The first thing he understood was that someone was shoving something into his hand. *"Shower, please. Just shower."* The second was a terrifying monster standing over him made of water. It was his shower. Darius stripped and washed inside the water weird, in full view, holding eye contact, twenty or thirty snakes out from his head. *"At least turn away." "Why is it supposed to be covered?"* Then Proteus, the priest they'd seen before, walked into the grotto, stopped in his tracks, and looked at that too.

Kaz, trying to decide whether to swim for it through the boiling water, tried a jump instead (13). He flapped his arms violently at the air and was suddenly off the ground. It was the water weird holding him up. *"I'm immortal, and apparently I can fly."* *"The day minotaurs start flying, I'll go back into the ground."*

What had death been like? Darius remembered something like a vacation. *"Under the weight of your bad decisions. So while we were fighting for our lives, you were having a vacation."* Why had he felt the urge to snuggle the carpet? *"I just ate a grape. One little piece of grape."* *"So you died for a grape."* He asked Kyrah to spin it into something that sounded better. *"He didn't say it needs to be good. He said more epic."*

Then Kyrah sent him a whisper by *message*. In here, she said, he could call Sydon whatever he liked, and nothing would happen to him. She demonstrated: *Sydon is a pathetic little wretch who tried to kidnap his own daughter, and that's wrong.* Outside, thunder rolled. *"Get it out of yourself here, now. All of it. All the resentment you have."* Anyone he wanted to call a bastard, he could do it here, where it was better than out there. Darius wouldn't tempt fate.

Kyrah took the white fur back off the floor and draped it over Versi. *"I'm not getting back my cat, am I?"*

Meanwhile Kaz swore blind he had not thrown the javelin that hit Ellion. *"Could have been anyone."* Everyone else in there had spears.

### Proteus and Kyrah

Athalea sat at a respectable distance, eating her sandwich very slowly, and listened to Kyrah and Proteus argue. There were two disciples left besides him. He was supposed to go out into the world and preach, to get the temple running again for anyone devoted to the Oracle, and he didn't think that was wise: there would be no one to protect her. That put Kyrah in a tight spot. Versi had ordered her to follow the party, and she didn't want to leave Versi, least of all without Proteus there.

Kaz, meanwhile, turned out to be a Large creature, which made him hard to hide behind a sandwich. He had always wanted to be one.

Somewhere in someone's pocket was [[NPCs/Mr. Flufster (Master Flamster)|Mr. Flufster]]. They found him crushed.

### The Prophecy

Versi calmed the argument with a gesture that meant *light the braziers*, and came out with a basket of incense. The party helped light all six; the braziers had never been lit so fast. Then she held up a small crystal, closed her eyes, breathed in the fumes, and stared into each of their eyes in turn, as if probing their souls. Some time passed before she spoke.

> *"You warriors who stand here gathered will be tested. The Fates have revealed three great tasks that must be accomplished before you are ready to sail into the three seas and face the Titans. You must shape the silver fires of the lost forge in order to craft the tools that you will need. You must claim the mighty weapons wielded against the Titans by the first Dragonlords. And you must drink deep from the bottomless dragon horn, for it will reveal a vision that I cannot see. But woe unto thee, for I have seen the end of all things. My father's anger cannot be quelled, and his sister schemes even now. Your quest may yet fail and, if it should, the sky will rain black fire, and the doom of Thylea will come."*

(Someone heard *weapons* and nothing else, and it had to be repeated.) Then she withdrew to her alcove and received them one at a time.

**Athalea** went first.

> *"My condolences for your loss, Haunted One. While I cannot see the fates that befell your loved ones, I can see their memory clinging to you like so many ghosts. I know now the silver light of dawn shielded you from the wrath that so terribly fell upon your family. Yet they need not haunt you forever. You may yet save your dearly beloved family. But to do so, you must retrieve the artifacts of your house. Seek out my sister, Demetria, where she dwells in the darkest parts of the forest, for her servants have delivered to her a relic stolen from your fallen family."*

And, unprompted: she did not see their faces, but Athalea's family still may be saved. *"So I'm not crazy."* Should she tell Versi's sister anything? Versi's lips stayed shut.

**Darius** was next.

> *"You are the cursed one whose veins run black with the foulest of hexes, and punishments disproportionate to the crimes committed ages ago. I see that you must travel to the City of the Dead, where you must speak with the silent guardian that watches over all of Telamok. He shall reveal the true nature of your curse, for he was there when the curse was placed upon your blood."*

He came out confused. All his life he'd believed two things: that he would never be rid of the curse, and that it was earned by something very, very bad, and everyone, Kyrah included, had told him so. The Oracle had just told him otherwise. It was the first time anyone had said the punishment didn't fit the crime, and with the confusion came a moment of hope. Then he and Kyrah went a round on trust. *"If you don't trust someone without a reason, they'll give you a reason."*

**Kaz** went in next. Versi hadn't expected more people.

> *"A flower cannot bloom when its stem wilts. To see your destiny through, go to your divine parent, now King of Estoria. He wallows in his wine and his grief, long having lost the luster of his glory days. You shall forge the weapon he could never wield, defeat the ancient foe he could never face, and save the love he lost. In doing so, you shall walk a path that will allow you to outshine your father in heroism, and perhaps even join him in godhood."*

He spent a very short time in there and came out entirely unbothered. It was the exact prophecy he had heard as a child.

**Ellion** went last.

> *"I am pleased to have one with such a lofty destiny come before me. I see you are meant for great things — to ride at the forefront of a flight of reborn dragons. But have you ever wondered the source of your great gifts? Have you pondered whence they came? I shall reveal it to you. You and I, you see, are kin. My father, Lord of All, once loved a mortal sorceress. She was your grandmother. My father's sister-wife, Lutheria, convinced him to murder his mortal lover. You must avenge her, and in doing so, her spirit shall guide you to your great destiny. Now allow me to give you something of hers — a memento I know she would wish for you to have far more than I."*

She gave him a tiny locket on a chain. He asked her to repeat it from the beginning. He looked at Versi and recognized her for what she was: a naiad, a water nymph. His cousin. He was to avenge his grandmother on everyone involved. No name was given, only *a mortal sorceress*.

Of all of them, Ellion came out looking the most disturbed.

### What Was in the Locket

*"Did you guys also get jewelry, or is it only him?"* Just a memento from his grandmother, he said. Silver locket, silver chain. It felt colder to the touch than when he'd first taken it (Wisdom save 24), and nothing more.

Later, he turned his back to the room and opened it. It clicked open easily. Inside was a single dried mountain flower, **the same flower he had seen before**.

As soon as he opened it, the voice in his head spoke, with its same words: *"I cannot decide whether they should live or die."* He held against it (22), and this time it did not take him. Then came a whisper he had never heard before:

> *"We shall resurrect the dragons, my grandson. We shall avenge, and we shall defeat Sydon. But keep me close."*

He closed the locket and put it around his neck. Nobody noticed. When someone asked what had been inside, a picture of the woman or what, he said: a flower. Dried. So many years.

### The Three Labors

Then Proteus spoke up, and gave them the practical side.

**The weapons of the Dragonlords** were forged by a god and were capable of wounding the Titans themselves. No one goes where they lie now: the place is guarded by an undead gatekeeper known as **Damon**, who must be paid in a special kind of coin before he will let anyone in. (That was where Kyrah had heard the name.) Proteus reached into his pocket and brought out one coin with dragons on both sides. *"Please keep it safe."*

**The Mithral Forge** was used by the dwarves to make the weapons of the gods in the first war, and can still make powerful magic items or repair ancient ones. Kyrah went very thoughtful. The dwarves don't go to the forge any more, but they mine copper near Estoria.

**The Horn of Balmytria** should be in Estoria. It is the horn of Balmytria, a famed silver dragon lost in battle against the gigantes, later recovered by the priests of Mytros and consecrated by the Five, and it has many magical powers, including granting visions to those who drink from it. Would anyone let a band of more-or-less vagabonds near it? The Oracle's chosen are supposed to be given aid by anyone who wants Thylea to survive. *"It's not written on us."* *"Kyrah's songs will take care of that."* Proteus grinned, very forcedly.

Kyrah had one ready:

> *The axe was sharp, the axe was true. It broke a door because it had to. It's not an axe — it is a club. Its name is Betsy, and it's earned a verse for every fight it's turned. Betsy, if you're listening: you are an excellent soul.*

Proteus brought out **four more coins**. *"I do not have much trust that you keep this one safe."* Each of them took one, and Athalea two. He told them they were welcome to stay as long as they needed, bowed toward Versi's alcove, and went.

That made two tasks in Estoria, or starting there, said Kaz, and his father rules it. Pythor was not taking visitors in his current state; Kaz had never once spoken to him. They'd need fame first, the kind earned by labors. He'd need time at the forge too: part of his prophecy was to craft a weapon, and apart from Betsy, which was just two pieces of wood hit together, he'd never made one. He asked Kyrah if she knew where a green dragon named **Hexia** laired; it would kill two birds with one stone. She didn't. *"Somewhere green and scaled. Not quite here."* In short, said Kaz: *"I need to craft a weapon my father couldn't, kill a dragon, save my mother. Pretty straightforward."* Pythor, someone added, has a favorite daughter, and made her a princess of Estoria. Time for a favorite son.

For Athalea, the prophecy settled something. People had asked her more than once whether she had killed her family; where she came from, those rumors were about the only thing anyone talked about. *"So it's pretty safe to say you didn't."* And her family was probably alive, so they needed to move fast.

Ellion wanted to know where to look for the story of Sydon and a powerful sorceress. It might have been a hundred years ago. Try a big city, said Athalea; the academy in Mytros had a ton of books, and there was an archive in Estoria.

And how do you speak to a silent guardian? Sign language? Writing? Could undead even see? Kaz decided that was a question for the road.

### Terms of Leaving

Proteus was waiting for them on the way out. They'd asked for food; that had been taken care of. From his robes he brought out a small pouch of coin. *"I don't have much, but this is what we have."*

> *"The future of Thylea may be bleak, but we have hope. And this hope is you. The people of this land will look to your actions as a sign of what is to come. Seek neither fame nor fortune, but show them what remains of heroism. Make yourselves a beacon of light as we enter the time of darkness. And you will do this with the blessing of this temple."*

And potions of healing: *"We do not have much, but what we have goes with you."* They pressed him to keep the money; he had more problems than they did. He looked at Kyrah with some blame for the news that she was leaving, and the picture on the wall suddenly became very interesting to her.

Athalea made a speech of thanks, and offered to help another way. They were going to Estoria. There was a wine festival, and a great many taverns, and she was a bard: she'd sing a lot of songs, and a couple of them could be about this place and its need for willing hands and minds. She put her siren's voice behind it (Persuasion 24). Something battled in the old man's face; he didn't want to leave, and he didn't want to abandon his duties. Her voice has a way of making people stop thinking about hardship while they listen. His face went smooth. Right. Right. That would make sense, and she had the temple's blessing. Anyone willing to serve the Oracle and build the future of Thylea.

How would they get in without diving through a hole into boiling water and being attacked by mephits? By announcing themselves: better send a letter. And how does the mail get down here? *"Usually by the siren couriers. They drop the mail."* Athalea almost smirked.

If they wanted to keep in touch, Proteus said, and walked out to the boiling stream. Where he stepped, puddles of water formed above it, chilled stepping stones that didn't boil him. He sat down by the steam, made a motion, and the steam coiled into a very familiar shape, a mephit. He took out a bottle, sucked it in, corked it, and gave it to them: a **steam mephit in a bottle**. Steam is everywhere; if he needed to send a message, it would come through. Let it out to send one, and the same motion draws it back. What if the bottle broke? *"Then you would need to kill it."* Nobody gave it to Kyrah. Athalea kept it, alongside the bottle of weird blood and the healing potions.

Two reminders for the road. Darius had come back at one hit point with a **-4 penalty to all attack rolls, saving throws and ability checks**, easing by one each long rest. Kaz still carried **-5 feet** of speed from his burned backside.

Steam mephits formed a staircase out of the chasm, whistling like an old kettle on every step. Kaz, being Large, carried Athalea out on his shoulder like a sack of potatoes. Kyrah hopped up after them, apologizing to every step.

### Sir Ferdinand

Proteus pointed them to a very small cart, with blankets and some provisions in it, and one donkey in front of it. The old man went a little red at their thanks. *"To your travels, heroes."*

Kaz suspected the donkey at once. Why would anybody become a donkey? *"You wouldn't suspect the donkey. Exactly. For that reason."* The donkey's eyes narrowed.

Ellion spoke to it in beast tongue, and it answered like a minor noble. *"Why is your cow staring at me?"* *"I'm not your cow. I am my own minotaur."* Its name is **Sir Ferdinand**. It was educated at the University of Mytros, where it carried the bathwater, and has grown too old for that work, so it was sent to the temple as a gift. Athalea had given lectures on poetry there for a while. That, said Sir Ferdinand, was why she seemed familiar; he might have attended a few. On an Animal Handling check (19), Ellion established that it was just a quite old donkey, about twenty. As for Athalea's current line of work, she keeps people company: conversation, cards, dice, and people donate generously.

Kaz stayed unconvinced. It spoke too well and knew too much. *"If I pretend to be French, I'm going to speak French."* Sir Ferdinand put it down to common jealousy: *"Look at my shining fur, and look at his."* *"I'm a demigod. Why would I be jealous of a donkey?"* Somewhere along the road Athalea pierced Kaz's nose, and he ended up with a bleeding nose ring. Sir Ferdinand approved: they could tell him apart from other cows now, though there weren't many left, since most were sacrificed to Sydon these days.

Sir Ferdinand asked Lady Athalea to read something to suit the journey, since he remembered her verses. Kyrah read instead:

> *Today it is not the worst day. It is not the best. It just sort of happens, like all the rest. And I don't have a strong opinion on it, truly. It just needed something to rhyme with "duly." As in: Tuesday arrived, duly unremarkable, and left the same way.*

*"That is truly terrible. Lady Athalea, please, read something to cleanse my ears."*

Ellion asked Sir Ferdinand where he might learn about an old affair of Sydon's with a sorceress. *"It seems like a private affair of sorts."*

### The Blood in the Bottle

Ellion still owed Mr. Flufster a promise: a bath in bird blood.

The party still had a bottle of unidentified blood with a bird skull on it, picked up ages ago because the bottle looked cool. Ellion listened for birds (Perception 23) and heard mockingbirds, a whole nest of them, with nestlings. He refused to kill them, or to leave chicks to starve. He stepped on some very loud branches, and two birds flew off; the nest stayed.

So they looked at the bottle instead. Kaz put his nose to it (advantage on smell): copper and a bit of ash. Not humanoid, at least. Airborne. At least five years old, maybe more. So it was something that takes to the skies: a siren, a bird, or a dragon. Ellion looked closer (Arcana 20): it was magical, carrying evocation, abjuration, transmutation and conjuration. From the ash, they assumed dragon. They decided very quickly not to pour it on a dead squirrel.

### The Quail

Half a day out, Ellion found quail in a field sixty feet away: a chicken, but smaller and fancier. He crept closer (Stealth 7) and it bolted. He threw a magic stone (21 to hit, six damage on a creature with one hit point), and it fell, bleeding. Its mate came out of the grass furious at him for killing its spouse and bit him for one damage. *"I'm so sorry. I just need to fulfill my promise."* A second stone (16).

He laid Mr. Flufster's body under them, and let their blood cover it. *"I'm sorry, my friend, that I did it too late. But at least I fulfilled my promise. I hope your soul is treated well in the other world."*

The body twisted and grew, and its bones broke. What sat up was still tiny, and mechanically it was a **homunculus**. And it spoke Common.

*"I'm finally free."* *"The death is nothing. The victory is everything."* It flew up into the sky. Where was he going? *"I shall seek my destiny and make it burn. I'll be in Estoria. Find me."* Then it came back, took Ellion by the cheeks, kissed him, and went.

*"He went to burn down my father's city."* Estoria is built of marble, which is hard to burn. The people in it aren't.

Kaz spent an hour plucking and cleaning the quail — he was raised in a village.

### Master Flamster

He came back after an hour and sat in the cart. *"Did you know stone doesn't burn?"* *"Yes."* *"But hair does."* He wanted to be touched, and it didn't matter much by whom, and most of all by Darius, whose touch he wanted as divine intervention. He burned because he needed to burn. *"When I started to feel this urge — when you touched me — it grew and grew, and through the fire I saw the way of things. The way of things is fire."* And: *"You can call me Master Flamster now."*

Athalea recognized it (Religion 20), and recognized it twice over. It looked very much like her own people. This is what Lutheria does: she finds creatures that are struggling and gives them a new purpose, and it drives them mad; they have one purpose, and they change because of it. And homunculi she knew from somewhere else: they are made from the remains of the creatures in the caves during the parties, to serve their makers for a short while, during blood rituals. She told them the legend. A titan was furious because some feathered ladies built a tower taller than his, so he took away their ability to feel joy. A female titan took pity and fixed it by giving the joy back, and nothing but joy remained. So now there are harpies, who do whatever they want, including killing and eating people and kidnapping children, for fun. The table granted her inspiration.

Master Flamster tried to set fire to Darius and kept blowing flame at his armor, since he was the one who had made him. Told to stop burning people: *"Very well then. I shall find my next sacrifice."* And he was gone. Ellion tried to call him back (Performance 1).

The only thing that would calm him down, it was agreed, was to end him, peacefully, and Athalea whispered as much to Ellion by *message*. Ellion refused: it was a blessing from Lutheria, and they shouldn't bargain away a blessing from a living creature. She would take offense. *"That's not might. They will."*

A few hours further down the road, they found charred animals: a deer, a few foxes and a rabbit. Then, with the words *"Your master returns,"* Master Flamster jumped back into the cart.

Ellion and he argued for the rest of the evening. Stop burning things. *"You want me to go against my nature? Don't you love me the way I am?"* It was his soul, his purpose. Ellion said he had seen a great fire too, a great sacrifice, in his dream. *"You see? We understand each other. We see the same things."* But they were trying to save Thylea, not burn it. *"We just burned two birds."* They'd killed those birds to do the ritual for him. *"You do not know the price of a good sacrifice. You never knew the price."* Mr. Flufster? *"Master Flamster."* *"No one will kill me. Everyone is immortal in the Furious domain."* And he had only come back for Ellion — hadn't Ellion cried over his grave, all those birds? *"Only two birds."* *"Two are plenty. I was but a squirrel."*

Ellion set him down off his shoulders and walked away. *"I don't need this energy in my life right now."* *"I'm heartbroken. You don't want this relationship any more? I know this breakup is hard on you, but come on, man."*

Kaz proposed a contest: whoever won would be the other's master, and that might be a way to contain him. Master Flamster didn't want Ellion as a servant; he'd rather have Darius. *"Very nice hair, and the magic touch."* The contests proposed were turned down one by one: first to retrieve an ancient text from the secluded part of the archive in Estoria, which might have recently been nearly burned down; most creatures burned in the forest; most ladies seduced. *"Well then, no one wants anything from the old master."* He jumped back in the cart. They tried tying him down with a rope, and it burned. (*"I'm all for experiences. You cannot experience much when you're a squirrel."*)

*"Hey, twiggy boy. Did you take any of my offerings?"* Nobody had. Within about fifteen minutes, most of the cart's food was gone.

Someone handed Ellion an apple on a dagger. *"Read between the lines, brother."* Ellion told Master Flamster that if he kept burning everything, he would stop him. *"You burned the boar. You burn animals for food; I burn them for joy. We're not so different, you and I."* *"And who do you burn for?"* *"For Lutheria."* *"She doesn't require five sacrifices in a day."* *"The requirements are one thing. Personal wishes are another."*

*"It is the first breakup I have had."* *"Meet you in Estoria, then."*

It was evening. Estoria was a day and a half, maybe two, down the road, and they were heading there as two groups, with two different intentions.
