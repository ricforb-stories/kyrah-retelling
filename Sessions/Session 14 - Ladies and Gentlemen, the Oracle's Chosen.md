# Session 14 — October 30, 2026 — "Ladies and Gentlemen, the Oracle's Chosen"

**Level:** 2
**Party Members in Attendance:** [[The Party/Ellion|Ellion]], [[The Party/Darius|Darius]], [[The Party/Xhazag (Kaz)|Xhazag (Kaz)]], [[The Party/Athalea|Athalea]]

## Our Adventure

### Not on the List

[[The Party/Xhazag (Kaz)|Kaz]] sprinted up toward the palace, which sits tucked in among the hills. Behind him, the people who had watched the mountain walk over their city were slowly drifting back to the wine barrels still standing in the streets. Heavily. The actors from the amphitheater were among them.

At the palace steps, a guard stopped him. *"What do you want here?"*

Kaz did not know this one. *"Come to see my father."* He showed his old soldier's insignia. The man was not impressed: Kaz wasn't palace guard. *"I think I know you. That kid who keeps telling everyone he's Pythor's son, right?"* *"You might have heard the name Kazak."* *"Yeah. I've been warned about you."* By whom? By [[NPCs/Stivon|Stivon]], who had told him Kaz might try something like this, and *infiltrate the palace*.

Then he would see his sister, [[NPCs/Anora|Anora]]. The guard was not sure that was knowledge Kaz needed to have. Didn't he have a list he could check? *"You're not on the list."* *"You haven't checked."* The guard turned around, did not check anything, and turned back. *"You're not on the list."* He had heard of Kaz, he said, and he'd heard not to let him in anywhere. *"The palace is closed. No one is coming in. Especially not you."*

Kaz asked his name, so he could tell his father who had kept him out. The guard didn't think that would be necessary. The throne room was about seventy-five feet away, and Kaz weighed running for it, and thought better of it. He settled for committing the man's face to memory, very carefully, and walked back the way he came, shouting for Stivon.

### Code Black

Stivon was already near the wall, shouting for him: *"Horny bastard, where the hell are you?"*

*"Let's go, so you can get me into the palace. I need to check on Anora. I'm afraid the centaurs might have got to her."*

Stivon jumped for his horn, didn't bring him down, and hung off it. Then the horn came off in his hand. It was Kaz's spare, and it unscrews. *"You're holding my horn in your hand. I never knew you were interested."* *"I didn't know it unscrews."*

Stivon had no intention of taking him anywhere near the palace. *"I don't believe I ever let you near my sister, you half-horny bastard."* It was code black, and code black meant the guards' quarters. *"Or do you want to be under a tribunal for not coming? I am covering your ass."* Anora? *"Who do you think you are?"* *"Son of Pythor."* *"Who cares? She's a princess."* The palace guard were guarding the palace, Stivon said, and his mother was in there too; everyone was fine. What they needed was to discuss city security. *"We had something walk into the city today with no precaution whatsoever."* *"You can't really do precautions against that."*

At some point Stivon made a go for the other horn, and it went about the way you'd expect. Then he offered, with great sarcasm, to confess his long secret crush and marry Kaz on the spot, and pinned him to the wall to prove it. Kaz picked him up and carried him the rest of the way to the guards' quarters. *"I hate you."* *"I missed this."* He did not put him down.

### Helena's House

Meanwhile [[The Party/Athalea|Athalea]] carried [[NPCs/Kyrah|Kyrah]] to [[NPCs/Helena|Helena]]'s house, with everything she could grab. There were no lights on, and nobody home. They were still invited, so she let herself in, laid Kyrah on the bed she remembered Stivona's things being kept under, and checked her again.

Nothing much had changed in ten minutes, but Athalea knows medicine. Kyrah's pulse and breathing were slowly getting worse. It was as if the life was being siphoned out of her. It was hard to track at all, it was so gradual, but at this rate she still had a few days.

Athalea took out the bottle [[NPCs/Proteus|Proteus]] had given them, with the **steam mephit** inside. She uncorked it, and the mephit appeared, looked around, and stared at her. It spoke Primordial, in a dialect so thick with accent it barely sounded like any she knew, and she had to dictate her message one word at a time while it repeated each one back: *Versi. Kyrah. Is. Unconscious. After. A giant. Rock. Titan. Walked. Over. Estoria. Help. Question mark.* Then it turned into a thin line of mist and went out of the window.

She cooked while she waited: the chilled venison she had kept back from the deer, made into a stew with what was left of the leg.

### Stivon, at Home for a Minute

The front door opened. Someone came in, went into the other room, clanked about, and was on the way out again when Athalea leaned out and caught him. It was Stivon, picking up his gear. She apologized for putting her friend in his bed. He didn't think anyone would be home tonight anyway.

What had that been? He didn't want to know. He had ideas, and they weren't nice: something carrying a scythe and putting the world in shadow, at the end of the oath of peace. Did it do anything, Athalea asked, except scare everyone? *"Scaring people is also an act of war. Psychological warfare."* *"Then it means the oath is fading faster than we speak."*

Were they going to do anything about it? Had the Oracle given them anything? They needed to drink from the horn to learn what to do next, Athalea said, and people go mad from it. Stivon had no idea the city kept any part of a dragon. Then he had to go: code black, report for duty. Had she seen the horny one? At the gates, last time. And his sister, she told him, had been drinking on the wall fifteen minutes ago.

Did the city have a doctor? *"We had a healer, but she disappeared a week ago."* People kept disappearing in the old woods. Something was nesting there. The guard had sent out a search party and found no traces. Most of the missing were young, kids and apprentices, and the healer had been young too. Her grandmother still lived in the city, but she could barely see or hear; she had been training the girl to be the next *Whisperer*, which is what the city's healers have called themselves for years. Otherwise there were bandages. Or maybe a god could help, and there was one close by: Pythor.

Somebody would be home around noon, he said. She could take any room. On the bright side, the play hadn't gone on tonight, so it would be performed tomorrow.

### The Pit, Again

[[The Party/Ellion|Ellion]] stopped at the edge of the forest, and stood there for a while thinking about his life, and what he had done, and what he should do next, and whether to tell anyone.

Then he went back to the pit.

There was no more sound from it. There was no sign of [[NPCs/Mr. Flufster (Master Flamster)|Master Flamster]]. As far as Ellion could see, the centaur at the bottom was dead. He called quietly for Flamster, and nobody answered.

He did not really know what to do for the dead. He had seen the rites performed, but never asked about them. The trees were quiet, as trees usually are. So he climbed one near the pit, the way nymphs know how to sleep in trees, and before he slept he prayed, for himself and for his soul. Did he have one? What happens to him when he dies? He was supposed to go back to his Mother Tree. Only now he knew she was not his mother. How do you tell good from evil?

Then he fell asleep (Wisdom 20), and slept like a baby, in the smell of burnt flesh.

### The Rock Fell on Him

[[The Party/Darius|Darius]] came back to his senses in the black. He was in immense pain. He had very obviously just been killed: there was a wet, bleeding hole in his chest, and poison still in his blood. He was lying on something rough. His ears were buzzing. And, for the first time in his life, he had something like a hangover.

His face was covered by something, and his fingers would not work. His body felt like cotton; he couldn't tell whether he was touching himself or somebody else was. He smelled mostly a lot of piss, and blood (Perception 17). It took him twenty minutes to get his senses together enough to grab whatever was on his face, and he grabbed one of his own snakes instead, and it bit him.

He came close to dying again, there on the ground, and did not. Then he was out again, for another two hours.

### A Body in a Costume

Around two in the morning, Athalea went out for one small flight over the city, to see how people were doing before she slept on the floor beside Kyrah. Something near the alleys by the northern wall caught her eye. She didn't know why at first. Then she came down and saw it: the costume Darius had been wearing, lying on the ground, with Darius in it, and a bag beside him.

He had a pulse, steady. There was soft hissing from under the turban. He smelled terrible. The wound in his chest wasn't bleeding much, though his hand was. She found a barrel, and since she can bend water and not wine, she pulled the water out of it and washed him, and then she healed him.

He still couldn't see much. Everything was a blur. He tried to get the turban off his face (Sleight of Hand: 3), and instead got it around his neck and started strangling himself, and Athalea sliced it with her claw. *"Why are you trying to kill yourself? Wasn't one time enough?"* *"Why is it so dark here?"* She told him not to hurt the snakes; they'd be kind if he was. He tried to calm them (Animal Handling: 3) and got bitten again.

What was the last thing he remembered? *"The huge rock falling on me."* In front of which city? He got there in the end. She asked him her name, to check.

He tried to stand (Constitution save, failed). He felt the way you feel when you're very, very drunk, lie down, and feel even worse. *"I like ground."* Where was the entrance to the city? They were in the city, an alley or two behind the wall. Athalea wrapped the turban back over his head, but the snakes kept writhing out through the gaps in the cloth. She told him what had happened: he had been taken by a huge rock formation on two legs with a scythe. *"The last thing I saw is a huge rock flying at me. Its hand."* She tossed him the bag she had found beside him. And he had smelled of piss. *"That's not me."*

*"I need my helmet back."* It was at the theater. She told him to stay where he was. He tried to come with her, on his elbows, then tried standing again, and threw up. Great news, she told him: there were no doctors in this city, and he was fighting in a day and a half. *"Ground is actually nice. So cool."*

### The Wardrobe

At the amphitheater a few people who hadn't gone home were still gathered around a barrel, talking about what had happened. One of them was wearing a very familiar armor. When the stars went out, they had all dropped their things and run.

Athalea tried to get Darius's armor back, but the actor had it on and wasn't giving it up. So she slipped past them (a good roll) to the changing rooms and went through the costumes. She came back with the only armor left, which was much too small for Darius; a shirt so long it would come down to his knees; and, for his head, a very old rusty bucket with holes cut for the eyes, the helmet they had prepared for the play. She packed everything into the bucket and snuck out the way she came.

Darius changed, there in the alley. The armor sat like a crop top and showed his belly button, the long shirt went over it, and the bucket went on his head. Did it make him feel better? Yes.

He would not go to a tavern, or to Helena's house. *"You can find me here."* So Athalea went home and came back about five minutes later with a bowl of her soup, no spoon, and a blanket from the house.

### The Oracle's Chosen Thing

At the guards' quarters, the code black meeting was underway. For a very brief moment that night, Kaz had not been the strongest thing in the city. *"So we need bigger weapons. Huge crossbows. Put them on the walls."* Kaz could probably fire one alone; it would take two or three of the others. Stivon suggested firing Kaz out of it instead.

*"You guys start on the crossbows, and I'll do the whole Oracle's chosen thing."* *"What thing? What thing do you have at two in the morning?"* They had done what they could tonight, Kaz said: the city hadn't been overrun. *"Didn't you guys cause it?"* Athalea had told Stivon they had prophecies. This was part of it, right? *"Because otherwise it would be very stupid."* *"Yes. That's the only logical thing."*

Was the mountain real, or some kind of voodoo? *"Athalea is really good at what she does."* Stivon was impressed. He'd mostly thought Athalea couldn't do anything. Could she do it again, if the herd came back? Kaz explained that magic of that size takes finite resources, the kind you keep for a rainy day, and glanced at the empty bottle at his side. *"I have to admit you're not such a useless bastard as you were before."* *"If you keep talking like that, I might think you consider me a friend."* He would not go that far.

Stivon counted five Oracle's chosen: two at his house, one who mostly stood still, one who shook like a tree, and Kaz. Kyrah, Kaz said, was more of a guide. A historian. Stivon wrapped up: they would think about bigger weapons, try the old smithy, figure out something with the artifacts, and meet tomorrow during the day, when Kaz could tell them how they did it, so they could replicate it. *"Dismissed."*

Most of them left. Stivon, Stivona and a few others stayed at the table over maps and plans. Betsy was propped against the wall. Stivona looked at Stivon. Stivon looked at Kaz. She shrugged, and walked out of the room.

### Stivona

Kaz followed her into a long hallway. Was she feeling better? She had been shaken on the wall. She hadn't known it was them doing it, she said; it had seemed very real. But now that she knew it was part of their performance…

Then she told him. She had never said how amazing he was: the strongest and kindest and prettiest person she had ever met, the whole life she had known him, since they were kids. She was really happy to have him in her life, and grateful for it.

He felt the same. About her, he added. It would be very egotistical to say it about himself. *"I see you the same way. You're the only person—"*

*"Oh, shut up."* And she kissed him.

Behind them, a door creaked, and Kaz felt a stare on the back of his head.

He slept on the floor of the guards' quarters that night.

### Morning

Athalea woke in tears. Nobody was in the house. Kyrah was getting worse at exactly the same rate as before. The soup beside the bed was cold and untouched, and so was the note she had left in case Kyrah woke up frightened in a strange room. It was the very brink of dawn, and people were starting out on their business. She went to find Darius.

Darius woke with a torch in his face. It was the sun, through the holes in his bucket, flickering as the bucket moved. The alley was empty. He had slept the night through and had his strength back, all three hit points of it. While he slept, some children had drawn a funny face on his bucket.

### You Kissed My Sister

Kaz woke in the guards' quarters beside Stivona. He had to run, so he left her a very well-structured letter, because he can write. Then he went room to room through the barracks until he found Stivon, and woke him up.

Stivon took something out from under his pillow. It was a shield. Natural 20: **14 bludgeoning** to the face (Strength save 23, so Kaz stayed on his feet). *"You kissed my sister."* Kaz's nose was broken and pushed off to one side, one eye swelled shut at once, and his jaw hung a little loose. *"I think you broke my nose."* *"I will break something else."*

*"Look, this is going to sound weird, but it's very important that I check on my sister, and you're about the only person I trust with this. I know we usually joke around about everything, but this is one of those times where it's really important. Please. I'm begging you."* *"You idiot, you're an Oracle's chosen. You can just say so and get into the palace."* It didn't seem like that worked, Kaz said; he'd only been an Oracle's chosen for a few days. *"Where did you sleep last night?"* *"I need to go see my sister."*

He ran, leaving a trail of blood from his nose, and Stivon came after him through the city in his underwear, screaming. Kaz was faster. Every head in the street turned. Kaz grabbed a bottle on the way, and then another, and ran drinking from both at once.

*"Ladies and gentlemen, the Oracle's chosen!"* People started to join in behind him. *"What's that?" "I don't know, but they're the Oracle's chosen!"* Athalea, flying over to find Darius, saw the whole thing and came down alongside. Why were they running? *"Stivon didn't like me being a gentleman."* Gentlemen don't talk about these things. From somewhere behind came Stivon's voice: his spear would reach all the places Kaz had reached last night.

And Kaz had had a weird dream. Someone had said something they shouldn't have, and now the herd knew which girl to look for. He was *hoping*, he explained, that his sister was still in the dungeon.

At the palace he told the guards he was one of the Oracle's chosen and needed to see the king, and ran inside, with Athalea on his shoulder.

Behind them a crowd had gathered at the palace gates. A man in his underwear was screaming at the guards to let him in. Someone else was screaming that he was the Oracle's chosen. And Darius, who had followed the noise, announced that he was also one of the Oracle's chosen, and that he usually wore the armor, and that they had never seen his face before. The crowd took it for a flash mob. Darius began, very slowly, to back away into it, and went to the theater.

### The Spider and the Fountain

Ellion woke on his branch, almost forgetting where he was, and nearly fell. It hadn't been only a dream. He could still smell burned flesh.

He was more confused than before. Had anyone in his circle ever had a Titan, or a god, speak to them in dreams? Not that he had heard. The forest was calm, the animals as usual. He was the only startled creature in it. The Oracle's temple was days away.

At the edge of the forest he turned into a spider, which took about half an hour to cross to the city, and climbed the wall without anyone noticing (and nobody on it hated spiders). People were buzzing about a performance in the city before the evening's wine festival. He found an alley to change back in, and on his way through the streets he passed something walking along in a bucket. He did not recognize it.

At the palace there was a crowd, a man in his underwear shouting at the guards, someone else shouting that he was the Oracle's chosen, and guards forming a cordon to push everyone back. Ellion asked what was happening. A bystander had no idea, but it was exciting: whatever they put in the wine at these festivals, it was amazing. There had been performers, just like in the poems, and they had called everyone to join in. A minotaur had run through drinking from two bottles at once, and they'd all run after him into the palace.

Ellion tried to slip round the edge of the cordon. A guard caught him by the shoulder. *"King Pythor welcomes me."* *"Right. King Pythor welcomes everyone. Shush. Go."* He tried the charm nymphs have on people (16). It didn't work.

So he went and sat by a fountain, and threw a coin in, and hoped the place would still be standing when he came back. People going to work looked at him a little strangely, a fairy creature stretched out lazily beside the fountain, but nobody stopped. Every so often he looked up at the sky.

### Camille

Kaz ran into the palace, stopped at the statue, and opened it the way he had seen it done (History). The stair appeared. Down in the cellars, Anora was sitting with her back to them, paying no attention to footsteps.

The whole left side of Kaz's face had swollen up, and his nose was dripping blood. He stuffed something into it.

*"Good morning, my lady,"* said Athalea, from his shoulder. *"Was the fight atrocious?"* asked Anora. It wasn't from a fight. *"My friend Athalea. She's also an Oracle's chosen."* *"Wonderful meeting you. What brings you on the visit, Camille?"* Athalea looked at him. Camille? *"The father-given name is the true one."*

Kaz told her about his dream: one of the Titans, or something else, had said that someone ran their mouth out in the field, and now the centaurs knew who to look for. They'd known who to look for all along, Anora said; she was the sacrifice. But maybe now they knew *where*. *"So people know I'm here."* Athalea repeated the line from the dream for her. Kaz had only needed to know she was still safe. *"One would argue this is not safe."* Their father had not been much help. *"Surprising."* Kaz was starting to realize he might not be the most competent. *"How could you say that of our king?"* *"Hence the word* might*."* And Kaz couldn't free her: he had sworn to their father. He might turn into a frog. Or a cow.

Anora had heard the story going around the barracks: a Titan had appeared last night, and it had been Athalea's illusion. *"Brother, I sing songs sometimes, do some nonsense. Not this."*

So Athalea told her what she could. Ellion had come to them saying the herd would attack unless the city brought out a champion of the Lady, and the cursed one. They had staged a ruse, and the herd believed it. Then a giant stone figure with a scythe had crossed the city, picked up their champion, Darius, and vanished toward the sea, and half an hour later she had found him in the streets. *"Darius is my champion?"* He had died, and come back, and was in very rough shape. Ellion had done most of the talking, in Sylvan, and gone off with the herd, so she couldn't say where things stood with the cursed one. And Kyrah was getting worse. They had maybe a couple of days before her pulse stopped. The city's healer was missing in the woods. Anora knew nothing about the missing people.

Could their father heal? *"I am not aware of those capabilities."* How long had Kaz known their father? *"Since my birth,"* Anora answered for herself. *"He was by my side from the very beginning."*

They had promised to bring Darius to her, and they would, as soon as they found him. And the horn they had to drink from? *"The horn, of course. It is part of our relic hoard."* Whether they could reach it depended on how things ended for the Oracle's chosen up above. *"Not that I have much choice in the matter,"* said Anora, *"of the person fighting for my chance not to leave home too soon."* *"We'll do our best."* *"I know you will."*

On the way out Athalea realized her tie was gone. It had been her favorite, and her only one.

### At the Amphitheater

Darius reached the amphitheater. It was closed; the performance was in the evening. He sat down by the door to wait.

### The Smell of Desperation

Kaz and Athalea came up into the palace in the morning hours, among people of the court, where Kaz looked a little out of place in his tuxedo with things stuffed up his nose. The throne room had almost the same crowd as the day before: beautifully dressed people, food, wine, mingling. Statues of the Five Gods stood all around. The throne was empty, and nobody seemed to be taking petitions.

Kaz went looking for his father by smell (Perception 18, with disadvantage for the nose). There is a lot of wine in a palace. The strongest smell, of wine, blood, sweat and no will to live, led away from the throne room, across the gardens, to the living quarters, and to a door with guards in front of it. Royal guards (History 18): not allowed to move, not allowed to talk.

*"We are the Oracle's chosen, and are on official business with the king. We seek passage."* Nothing. Athalea watched their faces (13), and there was nothing to see. Years of rigorous training.

They decided to find Helena. Kaz tried again, but all he could smell was blood, as if some animal were dying nearby, until Athalea pointed out it was his own nose and had him take the stuffing out. *"That's better."* (22.) The trouble was that everyone in that family smells alike, and the scent came from several directions. The strongest one smelled of unyielding rage. They took the other.

It led round through the gardens and corridors to a part of the palace they'd never seen, and a room with ordinary guards. From behind the door Helena shouted that she was in a war council and could not talk. She'd be free in about half an hour.

Over some dried venison, they made a plan. Kaz would wait by the door. Athalea, who is very good at finding people, would go and find Darius and Ellion, and bring them back if she could get them in, or meet Kaz at the amphitheater if not. Someone should talk to Pythor about Kyrah. He might know something. They might have something in common, Kaz thought, and Athalea thought the same thing, and neither of them had to say it. *"Just don't stay alone in the same room with him."*

When Kaz realized the man in the bucket and the crop top was Darius, he was surprised. He'd thought it was some pervert.

### The Necklace

By the fountain, a thin, sickly man came up to Ellion. His clothes weren't bad, but he was starving; you could see his bones through his skin. In his hands was an **emerald necklace**, and it looked very out of place there. *"Would you consider buying this necklace? I only ask one gold piece for it."* He had found it, he said, in the old city of Telamok, in the Necropolis. Why so cheap? *"I really need to eat."*

Ellion gave him the food from his pouch: dried berries, dried meat, apples. The man thanked him, but it wouldn't sustain him; he needed the money. Ellion offered him gold for nothing, but the man would not just take money. He needed to *sell* this. So Ellion bought it, and paid him two gold pieces for it, double what he'd asked. Did the man have anyone to take care of him? He did.

*"Thank you. And I'm so sorry."* He walked off, and as he went he ate the whole pile of food in one go, and it was not a nice thing to watch.

Ellion set the necklace down beside him on the edge of the fountain. It was very beautiful, and it shone in the light.
