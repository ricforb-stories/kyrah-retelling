# Session 10 — September 19, 2026 — "By Not Being There"

**Level:** 2
**Party Members in Attendance:** [[The Party/Ellion|Ellion]], [[The Party/Darius|Darius]], [[The Party/Xhazag (Kaz)|Xhazag (Kaz)]], [[The Party/Athalea|Athalea]]

## Our Adventure

### The Road to Estoria

They still had most of Athalea's stag, and nobody wanted to bury it. There were farms on the way to Estoria. [[The Party/Xhazag (Kaz)|Kaz]], as Kazak, was certain of it. So they would give it to whoever needed it.

How old had Kaz been when he went to Estoria? Fifteen. He's thirty now. And he never saw his father once, even living there; they don't just let you in. Only the statues.

The deer hide Athalea had given him was still wet and very sticky, and needed tanning. There wasn't time to stretch it on a board, but you could speed the process up with raw eggs and wine. In his tribe, they used seagull eggs.

Sir Ferdinand couldn't pull the cart with a whole carcass in it. Kaz tried to hoist the donkey onto his shoulders (Athletics 9) and ended up with a hoof in the face. In the end Sir Ferdinand walked on his own, and Kaz pulled the cart.

Athalea flew ahead to scout, so they wouldn't walk into another clearing like the last one, and to look for somebody to give the deer to. They were out of the forest now, in hills, closer to the mountains. Animals are the best gossips, and she kept hearing the same two words: *bulls gone. Bulls gone.*

### Unplowed Fields

Closer to Estoria, there were farms. There are always farms near a city, and by this time of year near a city they should have been bursting. These were mostly untouched. Only small patches had been worked, and the people working them were pushing the plows themselves, trying really, really hard. The ground was almost as dry as rock.

Athalea came back to report. There was a drought, no draft animals, and people plowing by hand in the middle of autumn, which was stupid and sad. Then someone screamed on the farms, and the sound cut off.

Athalea flew closer. The farmers were running for their houses. From up in the clouds she almost missed it: small figures walking near the riverbank, which looked like people until she noticed the proportions were wrong. The river was big, and they looked tiny next to it, so they weren't people. There were at least **six cyclopes**, crossing the river.

Kaz read their path (Survival 23): they were heading south, toward Mytros. Athalea saw something else too. On the far side of the city, there were **groups of centaurs** camped, too far off to tell which tribe or what they meant. Centaurs don't usually come near cities.

*"Should we go or should we fight?"* *"I don't want to shy away from a fight, but a tactical retreat might be in order."* The cyclopes were giants with one eye, not much intellect in it, and a lot of determination to turn you into paste. If they needed a weapon, they'd pull up a tree. Nobody in this party had seen one fight. *"If it's bigger than me, it's dangerous."*

Then the cyclopes turned and started across the river toward the farms. The party did what the farmers had done: got out of the way. Estoria stands between a cliff and a rocky gorge, surrounded by farms and plains. They went wide around the fields to the gate. Behind them, the cyclopes stopped at the farms, and there was a huge creak as a house wall fell.

### At the Gate

The gates were shut and there were guards on the wall. The outer wall had houses built into it: inns, Kaz knew, for the wine-festival crowds who couldn't find room inside. Ahead of them in the queue, a tired merchant with a cart of stock animals and a herd of bony oxen was arguing with the wall. The wall was on alert, and nobody was coming in until it was over.

Kaz used to be a soldier here. He knew the guards, and one of them, **[[NPCs/Stivon|Stivon]]**, knew him. *"WHERE'S MY TEN GOLD, YOU BASTARD?"* In a drill once, Kaz had been knocked out and landed on him, Betsy and all. The ten gold was for medical bills. Kaz maintained he'd paid it and the guard just didn't remember, what with Betsy landing on his head. *"Betsy did not fall on me. You fell on me, with Betsy."*

Athalea flew up to smooth things over. They were here to provide services in the name of saving Thylea, she said, and some relief from the drought, with free food for anyone who needed it. And he was aiming, Kaz explained helpfully, at a demigod and one of the Oracle's chosen. *"Don't worry, he would never shoot me. He was born without balls."* The guard shot him in the balls with a longbow (21 to hit, three damage, the left one). *"There was a bigger target there, but you still can't pay for shit."* Could Darius heal him without touching him?

The guard had heard things. Travelling hunters had said good things about the party, and travelling farmers had said they'd seen a big scary monster that almost killed them in a tavern. And he had eyes for Athalea. Did she have plans for the evening? He was going to see a play in the main arena for the wine festival. And where would they sleep? *"You can stay at my place."* He lived in the barracks, it turned out, so the address he gave was his mother's, with a kitchen for the deer. *"Please be careful. He's a degenerate gambler, a womanizer, and lives with his mom."* *"She's an amazing lady. You'll love her."* *"Wait until you meet his sister. He's hairier than me."* And were they still doing dice nights? Tonight. *"No money, no honey."* Athalea kissed her fingertips and pressed them to his cheek, and the gates started to rise.

### Summoned

Inside, the streets grew more and more crowded: people from everywhere, trading goods and gossip. Guards lined the streets, most of them at the gates, trying to look stoic and visibly restless. Then a detachment of hoplites came toward them from the north, and an officer stepped forward and respectfully hailed the party. *"I kindly ask you to follow me."*

Athalea, quietly and very clearly, asked on what grounds. What had they done to deserve such attention? *"There is no politely declining this invitation. I'm very sorry."* Where were they going? The palace. *"Questions later. Now come."* They still had a cart of meat to see to. Later. Kaz settled it by picking up anyone who didn't move and carrying them, Athalea over his shoulder like a sack of potatoes, shouting back at the officer that they needed somewhere to keep the meat cold. Sir Ferdinand and the cart stayed behind.

On every corner there was a barrel, and people were drinking and gossiping and smiling. Athalea looked closer (Insight): they were strained smiles, the kind you wear to keep your mind off something. The gossip:

- The drought is because **Sydon is withholding the rain** from this region
- **King Pythor has gone mad with grief** for his daughter
- **Sydon has demanded that the king's daughter be sacrificed** on the night of the next full moon
- **Centaurs are gathering in record numbers**, obviously to assault the city
- The farms around the city have **very few livestock left**, used up by the daily sacrifices Sydon demands

Kaz heard none of it. He was about to meet his father for the first time. *"What should I say?"* He was lightheaded (a Constitution save), but held on.

The officer wouldn't say why they'd been summoned. *"The king's orders are not to be discussed."*

### The Throne Room

The great palace of Estoria loomed over the city. Its windows let in a great deal of sunlight, which did nothing for the gloom inside. There were precious relics from the first war, and tapestries of Estoria's history and of King Pythor's great adventures. Nothing recent was on them. In the old ones, he fought with a hammer.

In the throne room was a man who looked a lot like a thunder god, very drunk, swaying where he stood, and fighting the statues of the Five Gods one by one with a spear. It was hard to say who was winning. The statue of Mytros might have had him. There were nobles watching silently from the sides.

*"The Oracle's Chosen have arrived, my lord."* Everyone else filed out, the doors shut, and **[[NPCs/Pythor|Pythor]]** tossed the spear aside, sat down heavily on his throne, and put his head in his hands. He beckoned them closer. *"You are the Oracle's chosen. Yes. Let me tell you of my woes."*

[[NPCs/Kyrah|Kyrah]] cut him off. *"Whatever your woes may be, drowning yourself in wine will not solve them. You have a duty to serve your people in dark times. The wallowing is disgusting, and does no one good."*

He blinked at her a few times. That might be true, but what could be done? The Oath of Peace bound his hands. *"And now the Titans conspire to steal away my only precious child, [[NPCs/Anora|Anora]]."*

*"Don't you recognize this one?"* *"…My son, right?"*

### Anora

His daughter, the future of Estoria, was to die in two weeks. **Sydon had demanded her as a sacrifice.** Were there any statues of Sydon in this room? *"Fuck, no."* That little shit puts his dirty meddling hand in every situation.

He had killed almost every ox he had for Sydon, bought more at the highest price he could, and there still weren't enough to satisfy the bastard. So now he wanted Pythor's blood. Anora was to be taken to the **Rock of Sacrifice**, outside the city, and devoured by the beasts of the river: **basilisks**. If Sydon's will wasn't done by the next full moon, the centaur tribe camped outside would descend on Estoria and raze it to the ground. Sydon had already starved the city with this drought, and he always makes good on his threats. (*"Yeah, it wasn't pleasant being struck by lightning."*) The drought had been going for about a month, since the Order of Sydon came.

The cyclopes? He didn't know whether they had anything to do with it. They'd been pillaging the farms. The city lived mostly on merchants now.

Athalea knew the legends of basilisks (History): huge, sea-snake-like creatures drawn to shipwrecks where they hunt treasure-seekers, known to hunt ashore along island waters too. In some legends they turn people to stone. They were scarier than cyclopes.

A plan took shape at once. If the victim is put on the rock and the basilisks eat her, Sydon is satisfied. But if the basilisks are dead before then, nothing claims the sacrifice. They could kill the basilisks beforehand, quietly. Pissing off Sydon, someone pointed out, is basically the whole campaign. And Kaz: *"I will gladly die for my crew, as one should."*

Couldn't someone else be offered? No. The word was set in stone: Anora must be chained to the rock. Why her? *"Maybe because I loved her mother. Maybe because she's the only child who brings me joy these days."*

### Father and Son

*"My son, I love you so much. You know that, right? I love all my children. I'm proud of all of them. I just don't know them much."* He was the spitting image of his mother, apparently. *"Do you think I do not recognize my own blood? He looks just like me."* He looks literally nothing like him.

Pythor asked for Kaz's mother's name (Ophea), and then asked again, about five times in ten minutes. He named his son **Camille**, he said. *"You are that son."* *"Kazak is my name."* *"It's not what I named you."* He went on calling him Camille for the rest of the day.

*"I tried, you know. Three times,"* he said, to find her, and failed. *"You need to forge your own path."* *"Yes. And your weapon."* And then: **if Kaz saved Anora, Pythor would give him his hammer**, so that he could make something better out of it, a god's weapon forged and mastered by his own hand. It had not served Pythor. It might still serve him. *"I was going to do it anyways."* *"You do that, boy. I'm very proud of you. You've grown up to be big. I don't know anything else about you, but I'm proud of you."*

Somewhere in the middle of it Pythor pulled Kaz into a full hug and lifted him clean off the ground, feet dangling. Kaz, who had wanted this all his life, didn't really register it. He was thinking about how to poison a basilisk.

### Kyrah and the King

Pythor also asked Athalea what she was doing tonight. She was supposed to be seeing a play and playing dice, but she could reconsider. The charity kitchen for the poor outside the walls? *"Those are not mine."* But would it make her heart happy? Then yes, they'd do it at sundown.

Kyrah squirmed through all of this, very unhappy with the man. Aside to Athalea, she was scathing. He sires tons of children, then drinks his sorrows away and locks himself up. He'd marry Athalea, she said, and some evil wyrm would steal her and she'd die somewhere because the man can't keep it in his pants or stay sober. Kyrah allowed that he has a heart of gold, pure gold, and the intelligence of a goatling, the stamina to drink all night without sobering up, and godly powers. Athalea heard that she was quoting someone. And it had been going on for years, Kyrah said. Five hundred years, about, since all his women started disappearing and dying.

Athalea's own verdict: *"Too much beard and not enough boobs."*

Anora, Kyrah said, is smart, kind and good, and it runs in the family: she would sacrifice herself in a blink. Pythor keeps her very close. She was, in fact, **in the dungeons**, where she couldn't run out to the centaur camp and surrender herself. *"I thought you loved her very much."* *"I love all of my children."*

The Order of Sydon has **seven captains**. They were coming to the palace in two days to speak with his general, **[[NPCs/Gallios|Gallios]]**. Pythor doesn't speak to terrorists; his general does. The general was the cold officer who had brought them in, and he didn't speak to them either.

### Whose Villain

Pythor asked their names at last. Kazak. Ellion. Athalea. Darius. The helmet stayed on. *"They have the spirit for battle. I adore that."* *"You terrorize my heart already, my dear Athalea."* To Darius: *"My boy, what is your skin condition? Is it treatable?"*

Then Athalea asked the god of battle a question. Every child loves their parents, Pythor had said, unless the parents did something heinous. What is a battlefield, she asked, but people with pointy sticks making holes in each other? Isn't it heinous to kill a great many people so as to push the locals out and make room for the people you came in with? *"Depending on whose perspective you're looking at it from."* She is the one meant to hold the memory of her tribe, she said, and her tribe's history long predates the Five. Some of those stories are very bloody, and not glamorous for the gods. People like her, cast out of the places they were born, and slaughtered in the process.

*"Are you saying my father did that to your people?"* Pythor confirmed it: they came here after the Dragonlords; the rebellion was already under way, and the people only needed someone to lead them again once the Dragonlords had all perished. People didn't like being enslaved to gods they did not choose. *"I am very sorry that this happened to your people."*

So why love him? Kaz, when she pressed him: *"Up until you asked me a minute ago, I never had to think about it. I love him because I love him."*

### The Five, Remembered

Someone mentioned the **Oath of Fellowship**. Pythor laughed. *"That's a marvelous idea!"* The greatest things start with an oath of fellowship. That was how the five of them had been bound together.

He didn't remember a lot about his old fellows until, for a moment, he did. They were bastards, but they were family. **Volkan** was not much of a people person, spent days stuck in his tiny forge inventing things that never worked, immensely proud of every one of them. He made Pythor's hammer. **Kyrah** was a pain in the ass: she wrote terrifyingly stupid verses about every step they took and tortured them with it. And he looked at Kyrah, and looked away. **Vallus** fought with her mind more than her powers. He never won against her at dice, or cards, or chess, or anything, and his pockets were always empty. **Mytros** was the only other one who wanted a city, and she was very much like him, and very annoying. She was all about change, and meeting the most interesting people, and she was the first to open the gates of Mytros to Athalea's kind. She named the city after herself. Very self-absorbed.

They were the most powerful creatures there were, and they never had a final battle, which was probably a good thing. And now he's a god of battle who is prohibited from fighting. Five hundred years, and it feels like the blink of an eye. He sees Volkan from time to time, and Kyrah whenever she travels. A reunion of the five is not possible, with Mytros ascended.

This, drinking with strange people off the street who happen to be his own blood, was actually nice, he said. It made him feel surrounded by family. One of them said they would trade the world to be surrounded by their family. Were they gone? *"Don't know. They just vanished."*

Pythor proposed to Athalea. The green dragon might come for her afterwards, he offered, and Kaz could kill it. *"Honey, I won't be marrying you."* *"Why not?"* *"Because you don't have boobs."* *"Those are man boobs. But I do have them. It's more of a wine belly, and I'm proud of it. As proud as I am of you,"* he said to Kaz. (*"Somehow the arrow to the balls didn't hurt as much."*) If they ever needed bait to lure the dragon out, Athalea offered, she could marry him. *"Please don't."* So instead he tried to set her up with his daughter, and went to fetch a portrait of Anora. *"My pride and joy."* A very beautiful lady. *"I'd like to meet my sister."*

### Plans

Darius wasn't convinced that killing basilisks would end the drought; people would still starve. Who else could they ask? Not Sydon's people, said Athalea. Talk to Anora? She'd only say *please kill me in a very fashionable way.*

Athalea had another idea: go to the rock with everyone watching, fight whatever came, demand trial by combat, and proclaim that they'd overcome fate. Kaz preferred killing the basilisks beforehand, quietly, without an audience. Then, if nothing came to take the sacrifice, they could proclaim it divine providence. Sydon didn't want her anymore. If Sydon specified the basilisks, it would be losing face to do it some other way, so better not to do it at all. Fight the basilisks, rest a few days, then fight an angry mob if they have to, rather than both at once.

Darius asked Pythor whether sacrifices had always been made this way. They hadn't. The Rock of Sacrifice is for the most terrible sacrifices. It was used a few times, hundreds of years ago.

*"Camille, what's 'elaborate'?"* *"I think I raised you better than that."* *"You never raised me. Today is the first day we met."* *"I think I raised you better than this."* *"Then how did you raise me, if you've never met me?"* *"By not being there."*

Then he started to tell the story of how he met Kaz's mother, in the steppes. She hit him like a blast to the head. He knew he had to do something, and he knew he couldn't just recite poetry; it would be terrible. Did she ever tell Kaz about it? *"She got abducted by the dragon before she could."* *"I tried, you know."* *"Three times."*

That was where Athalea and Darius left them. Kaz and Ellion stayed with the king.

### Helena's Kitchen

On the way out, a guard stopped them: they had been summoned by the king, and now they were leaving? There was a warm family reunion going on, and they weren't interrupting it. Their cart was in the royal stables; she would escort them.

Her name was **[[NPCs/Helena|Helena]]**, and she was Stivon's mother. Athalea told her they would be using her kitchen, since her son had invited them. *"Really."* She was playing dice tonight. Did Darius have a date to the play? *"You're going with my daughter."* Why was Darius wearing a helmet indoors? His brotherhood: *the Children of the Sacred Serpents*, from Mytros. Never heard of them.

The royal stables were the emptiest stables you could imagine, except for one very happy donkey who had all the hay to himself. They left Sir Ferdinand there. Darius hauled the cart himself (Athletics 12), slower than the donkey.

On the way, Helena told them more. The city had been under something she wasn't supposed to call a siege for almost half a month. The southern gate, from Mytros, still let a few merchants through. Three months ago they'd been preparing for the wine festival, so there was an abundance of wine. There was no food to go with it. The cyclopes came when the siege started, destroying houses; people hid underground when they came. The cyclopes didn't seem too smart, but they were taking supplies. So either someone was directing them, or something bigger had pushed them out of their homes. Helena had met a cyclops once, on her travels, and it killed almost her whole group. *"How did you deal with it?"* *"We ran."*

Inside her house, with the door shut, she said more. Months ago the **Order of Sydon** came to Estoria and went to the palace. She doesn't claim to know what happened there, but King Pythor and Sydon's demands did not go well together. Estoria hadn't been making enough sacrifices for a city its size. It doesn't even have a temple of Sydon, only a temple of the Five and a temple of the dragons. You can't please the Titans with sacrifices to just one of them; you're meant to be devoted to both twins. Estoria isn't much devoted to either, and especially not to Sydon. Now they're catching up, and the demands keep growing. Nobody talks about it on the streets. The walls have eyes and ears.

Darius wondered whether he could slip out through the countryside and talk to the centaurs. They're friendly to the native and fey races, and they usually worship the Titans. Kyrah reminded everyone of what they had actually come to Estoria for: feed the poor, find the dwarves from the copper mines, the wine festival, recruits for the temple, and the **Horn of Balmytria**, which is here, in the city's reliquary. Balmytria, she said, was the all-seeing dragon. She could see even more than Versi, which is why Versi sent them here. People believe the horn still shows things, but no one who drinks from it stays sane enough to say what. Could they swear their oath with it? It would be poetic.

Athalea gave Helena the deer's skull, antlers and all, as a guest-gift. Helena was delighted that her son had found someone who respects the rules of hospitality. Not everyone who comes to Estoria does, she said, and they don't always leave blessed by the land. Athalea and Darius would stay with her.

Then the long couple of hours. Small portions, because the pillaging never stops. Athalea flew the food out, came back for more, and flew it out again, drinking wine the whole time, because everyone looked so miserable. She was a food raven.

The door opened. *"Oh, Stivona, you're here! I found you a date for the play tonight."* Stivon's sister: a half-dwarf, very tall, very muscular, a whole family of bodybuilders.
