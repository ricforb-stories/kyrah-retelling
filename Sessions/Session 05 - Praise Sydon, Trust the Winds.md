# Session 5 — July 26, 2026 — "Praise Sydon, Trust the Winds"

**Level:** 2
**Party Members in Attendance:** [[The Party/Ellion|Ellion]], [[The Party/Darius|Darius]], [[The Party/Xhazag (Kaz)|Xhazag (Kaz)]], [[The Party/Athalea|Athalea]]

## Our Adventure

### The First Draft

Three days after the boar, on the road to the Oracle, [[NPCs/Kyrah|Kyrah]] read the party the first draft of the poem about them — the epic of the four, from the tavern to the boar's cave, sung in the voice of the Muse. They had notes. [[NPCs/Mr. Flufster (Master Flamster)|Mr. Flufster]] did not wander into their camp; they abducted him. And Darius's oath of servitude was sworn during the resurrection, not before it — her call on it came later. Kyrah allowed that these were creative decisions; she could hardly tell the world they'd kidnapped a squirrel.

Nothing much had happened in the three days, by general agreement. Mr. Flufster was still with them, though nobody had touched him with *guidance* since the cave, and he had gone back to being an ordinary animal. Kaz was still bandaged across half his face and most of his torso, with one finger still bent the wrong way.

### How Does It Feel to Be a Hero?

So Kyrah interviewed them for the record. They were already being recognized in the towns they passed through — the Heroes of the Heartlands — and she wanted to know how that felt, in more detail than "I'm okay."

**Kaz** went first, and rose to it: *"I feel the glory coursing through my veins. But it also saddens me to know that the eight other parties before us have failed. Although I'm happy that where they did, we have succeeded — as we are just at the beginning of our journey, accompanied by the glorious bard, the sage of the forests, the queen of the skies."* Kyrah coached him toward something with more ring to it for the history books: *the one who changed the fate.*

**Ellion**'s answer was plainer: they had done the right thing — the creature was dangerous, they'd protected people, and they'd given the sacrifice to the right god. The name "hero" didn't quite fit yet.

**Athalea** gave the version meant for posterity: relief at finally having a purpose, and a place where people no longer called her lost in the mind; how heartwarming it was to watch her reputation turn from the local crazy with a couple of wings into a hero of the Heartlands with her brethren in arms. *"I see why you're being paid,"* said Kyrah. *"If I didn't know you were bullshitting me, I couldn't have told."* Athalea insisted it was honest.

**Darius**, the changer of fate, was allowed to lie. He didn't: *"I feel good to be part of friends, and to reveal myself for the first time."* It had been four days. Was killing them murder or manslaughter? *"I never wanted to murder any of you."* Somebody gave him a week.

Then the questions turned to his hair. The snakes don't have spines, as far as he knows; they don't need haircuts; they shed their skins; he washes them with soap. Athalea floated a business in snakeskin purses, asked for any snake that ever fell out so she could dissect it, and suggested he get a pet snake to blame things on. Neither she nor one of the others likes snakes — *"I am too much of a bird for liking snakes."* Some birds eat snakes; some snakes eat eggs. Athalea lays eggs. Pretty big ones.

### Courtship

That led, inevitably, to a long seminar on love.

**Kaz** explained that minotaur customs vary by tribe. His was pacifist — one of the ways they atone for their people's original sin — so courting with gifts of cattle, or anything that had ever lived, was out of the question. You court with songs and poems, or with labor: plowing a field for someone, building them a place to sleep. (He'd built the others a tent. That hadn't been his intention.) His father, a free spirit, wrote his mother ten poems, which is considered a very high amount of effort. And he speaks so eloquently because his mother told him that out in the world people would look down on their kind, so they had to try all the harder to be part of society.

**Athalea**'s people are about six hundred women to a hundred men, so most courting happens between the women, with underwater dances; the men, too delicate for that, dance around campfires. She drew one for scale — she was twice his size. When she was young, a minotaur woman courted her; her real name was a lot of sounds, but she called herself Mal. Athalea turned her down and kept her feather. Her people don't build in stone, so as not to be smitten by a god again; most of their songs are sad. *"Depression is our second nature."*

**Ellion** turned out to be the most sheltered person any of them had met. He grew inside his birth tree for about a hundred years and came out twenty years ago. In all that time he has left his small part of the forest around his druid circle only twice: once, a few years ago, to go to a shop with the other druids, and now. He has never met another dryad. The elders told him Kyrah's summons might be important, and he had the same feeling, so he came. Romance, marriage, family: nothing in his life maps onto them. Trees pollinate; sometimes, with a bird's help, there's a new tree. Does he feel anything for a nice curvy tree? No. A bush that sings? A lot of bushes sing.

**Darius** said his people live in free relationships. His order takes in orphans off the streets, of any age, and especially any who turn out to be cursed, to give them time and a chance to find a way. Athalea listened, and judged, and did not agree it was help.

Ages came out too: Darius is twenty-one, Athalea is twenty-eight, and Kaz turns thirty in exactly two weeks — which is a reason for a party, and for him to bring his flaming water, which he makes from rocks, water and a few more ingredients. Kyrah doesn't know when she was born. She was raised with [[NPCs/Versi|Versi]].

And under all of it, a real worry. Versi hadn't contacted Kyrah in two weeks, which was unusual; she normally checks in on how things are going. Kyrah had sent a letter saying they were coming, and nothing came back.

They were half an hour away. Could they go faster? Athalea could fly, and could sit on Kaz's shoulder; a carriage, if one happened to be lying about in the forest, had about a 5% chance, and there wasn't one. So they walked, with haste, Kyrah singing, and Mr. Flufster murmuring *fire.*

### The Crack

The forest didn't quite end. If they hadn't been looking for it, they'd have missed it: a crack in the ground, a canyon full of smoke and steam so thick they couldn't see the bottom.

How did Kyrah usually get in? She usually brought rope. Kaz's had burned. Athalea — the one person who didn't need a rope — had one, fifty feet of it. They would need about five of those.

Kaz did what none of them had yet done and looked around for a way down. Instead he found a stone tablet, half fallen out of its slot, carved with the words:

> *Praise Sydon and trust the winds.*

In the end [[The Party/Ellion|Ellion]] turned into a giant spider — without warning, mid-conversation — and climbed down the wall on his own; the chasm was bigger on the inside, and he went down it upside down. Athalea took the others: there was a great deal of discussion about who would tie whom, whether a man should tie up another man unless he was arresting him (Kaz used to arrest people, but isn't in the military anymore and no longer has the authority), and a gentle arrest, on suspicion of being the greatest bard, to which Athalea pleaded guilty. Then she cast *feather fall*, held the rest of them on the rope like balloons, and they stepped off. *"I believe I can fly."* Ellion raced down the wall ahead to guide them away from the boiling pits below.

Beneath the mist lay a river and a temple.

### The Gate

The temple was built straight into the canyon wall: towering columns holding up a pediment carved from the cliff face, and above the central columns a marble frieze of Sydon seated on his throne, black glaive in hand. The air was so thick with the geysers' mist that they were sweating before they landed.

They didn't land cleanly. Darius came down right on top of a geyser as it erupted — 26 fire damage before the fight had even begun. Four small creatures of mist and steam burst out of the vapor at them, and from inside the temple a guard in full armor came running, looked around, locked eyes with Kyrah, and shouted: ***"They're here. Kill them."***

More guards followed. One tried to shove Kaz into the boiling river with his shield. Another threw a javelin at Athalea; she caught it (Acrobatics 19), threw it back, dropped the rope and flew straight up out of reach. The steam creatures breathed scalding vapor, clawed and burned, and exploded into fire when they died.

The guards were strange. None of them seemed sure of what they were doing — except one, who was very locked in. Darius wondered whether they were under some kind of control. They were bleeding perfectly normal red blood.

Ellion killed one of the steam creatures and offered it as a sacrifice: instead of fire, it burst into snowflakes, and a blessing fell on everyone within sixty feet. *"Mother, please accept this sacrifice in your name, and give us an easy victory."* Kaz, breathing slow and focused rather than raging, brought another down with his shoulder, and called out to leave one of the guards alive for questioning — Darius's choice which. Then Athalea, from above, asked Kaz if he could take a hit. He could. Her *thunderwave* caught him, three guards and a steam creature, and pushed the guards toward the river. One dropped dead before he even got there. Another landed in a geyser, and his skin peeled away in the steam. Athalea gave Kaz a bardic inspiration by way of apology.

One guard drank a healing potion and retreated into the temple, shouting *"They're here! To arms!"* Kaz split a steam creature's head with a thrown handaxe that sailed on across the river, and knelt by a dead guard looking for potions. Kyrah looked into the temple, shouted *"Versi!"*, and ran in. Darius went after her.

### The Net

Inside, Kyrah had stopped dead, staring. Darius caught up to her just as a net came down on them both; he shoved her clear and took it himself. There were eight guards inside — mercenaries in mismatched armor — and in the center of the temple, looking right at him, stood an enormous **golden statue of Sydon**, holding a black glaive. Darius tried hard not to say *bastard.*

Bodies lay scattered across the floor, unmoving and covered in blood. Near the altar stood someone in priest's robes, and another priest was tied to a chair; three more clerics were bound with rope behind them.

Kaz fetched his handaxe and came in. Ellion, stuck across the river, became a giant toad and jumped the lot. *"The tank has arrived."*

Darius got out of the net spectacularly: he rolled with emphasis — double or nothing — and got an 18 and a 17. So he took the edge of the net, leapt onto one guard's belt, then his shoulder, then the next guard's shoulder, threw the net over both of them and pulled them to the ground.

Among the enemy was a woman in robes who called out *"Sydon blesses you, my dear"* and healed a mercenary, sending him back into the fight. Sydon's own healing, turned against Sydon's own temple. From the far side of the room one of the bound priests shouted at her: ***"Bria, don't! We can still fix this. Let me free!"*** Kyrah knew him: **[[NPCs/Proteus|Proteus]]**. Athalea asked if anyone there was named Versi, since they'd come to see her. They had taken her, came the answer. *Who are they? Where did they take her?*

### The Dragon

Kaz was badly hurt, a javelin sticking out of his chest. Darius healed him with divine power, and first had to decide which of his two goddesses to ask. He flipped a coin for it. The healing was enormous: Kaz came back to full health, the javelin pushed out as the wound closed, every injury from the boar sealed over and his old bandages fell away.

Then he heard a lady's laughter in his head, as if someone had called to him from one side, and a **green dragon** flew through the mist before his eyes. It was the same one he's hunting, and it was everywhere. For the next minute every creature around him looked like the enemy, and he attacked whoever was nearest. He crushed one guard with a single swing from top to bottom, frothing at the mouth, the whites of his eyes gone red, and dropped Betsy to headbutt the next target full force.

The next target was the withered old priest tied in ropes, [[NPCs/Proteus|Proteus]]. Darius threw himself in front of him and took it — thirteen damage, straight to the floor. Proteus was untouched. Kaz's vision cleared on a 23. He saw Darius on the ground with blood seeping from under his helmet, and a terrified old man looking at him.

Meanwhile Kyrah cut the prisoners free. She moved something behind the statue, and **the whole golden Sydon slid aside**, opening a hidden passage. She led the priests out through it.

### Bria

It fell apart for the mercenaries after that. Athalea put an arrow between the eyes of the guard whose mother she'd been insulting all fight — *"Bria, you're not that bright"* — and flew high into a dark corner. She promised the mercenaries they would drown in their own blood in the realm of Lutheria if they didn't surrender. Darius, just back on his feet and a little wobbly, tried intimidating them too. One threw down his sword with *"Fuck this, it doesn't pay this much,"* ran straight across the boiling river, came out bright red and started climbing the wall. Ellion hit another with a blast of frost as he ran. One of the last threw a javelin at Bria before he fled. Kaz, clear-headed now, knocked out the guard beside him instead of killing him. He's a pacifist, he explained: he only kills when his mind is clouded. Ellion threw *magic stones* at the ones climbing out of the chasm.

Bria was left alone. She looked at all of them, and then at the statue of Sydon. She took a vial from her pocket, drank it, and fell.

It was poison. One of the party got to her in time (Medicine 11): she threw up everything, and she lived, unconscious. They tied her up with rope.

There were more questions than answers, starting with where on earth Versi was.
