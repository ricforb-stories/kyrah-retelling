# Session 15 — November 6, 2026 — "A Lot of Beating for One Kiss"

**Level:** 2
**Party Members in Attendance:** [[The Party/Ellion|Ellion]], [[The Party/Darius|Darius]], [[The Party/Xhazag (Kaz)|Xhazag (Kaz)]], [[The Party/Athalea|Athalea]]

## Our Adventure

### The Throne Room

[[The Party/Athalea|Athalea]] had half an hour to fill before [[NPCs/Helena|Helena]]'s war council let out, so she went to the throne room, where the nobles were still mingling among the food and wine and the statues of the Five.

The mood was chill on the surface (Insight). Underneath, people kept starting sentences like *"I can't believe that happened again"* and being stopped, almost in fear, before they finished them. They glanced at the empty throne a lot, and at the statues. Scared, and irritated.

As soon as people noticed her, they stopped talking, so she changed tactics (Stealth 17). She slipped into the shadows and up near the high ceilings, and listened. The nobles were irritated by the king's absence. They were irritated by the state of the altars to the Five: those statues were the gods themselves, and they had tiny cracks in them now, since [[NPCs/Pythor|Pythor]] went at them with his spear. They hadn't seen the princess in a long time, and it was starting to look as if she didn't want to see her court anymore. And there were rumors going round that the Oracle's chosen were connected with the thing everyone saw, though nobody yet knew how. About the court they were irritated; about the Oracle's chosen, scared.

So Athalea came down looking like an ordinary messenger siren, and went round spreading rumors of her own, in a more down-to-earth way: *I've heard they saved a family on the road. I heard they handed out food to people outside the walls. They were doing very important things so the farms would be safer. Nice people.* Everything was true; she only made sure it was seen in the right light.

### The Fountain Everyone Avoids

Meanwhile [[The Party/Xhazag (Kaz)|Kaz]] set out to gather the others by smell. Ellion he knew: he had a little of his sap on him from some old wound. He caught a very faint smell of tree, mixed in with spice and leather and a hundred other things, and the only place in the city where that many smells mix is the **Agora**.

The market was crowded, but nobody went near the fountain at its center. The merchants kept glancing at it, with a mix of interest and pity (Insight 19), and leaning in to say something to their customers, who then glanced at it the same way. Kaz picked the most befuddled-looking merchant and asked what had happened over there.

*"You would not believe it. Someone actually fell for [[NPCs/Claus|Claus]]."* Claus had been trying to sell everyone that necklace from Telamok for a week. Who would buy something from the **Necropolis**? He pointed. By the fountain sat [[The Party/Ellion|Ellion]], looking at the sky, and something green gleamed on the fountain's edge beside him.

Kaz knew why that was bad. The Necropolis is where many ordinary people rest, and many heroes chosen by the gods too, and whatever you find lying there, you never know whose it was. Anything from there is probably not worth taking. What had it done to Claus? *"He withered away. Thin as a broom. Almost dead in a few days. You know how it works."* Kaz bought two loaves of bread, and the merchant wished him luck.

Then a shadow fell over Ellion, and the sun was gone. *"So you fell for Claus."*

### Walk and Talk

People were staring, so they moved. Ellion, it turned out, knew nothing at all about what a necropolis was. Kaz looked him over. He didn't look like an Oracle's chosen; that was why the guards hadn't let him in. *"You lack pizzazz. A certain je ne sais quoi."* Ellion asked about Kaz's own clothes, the same as yesterday's. *"Traditional minotaur celebratory garments. I know it's missing the tie. It was bringing the whole outfit together, but it got stolen at the palace."*

And where was Darius? Hadn't the giant taken him away? He was in the city, Kaz said, and wore a bucket on his head now, and needed a doctor, and there were none, because the healer had gone missing in the forest. *"Look, there are three things I'm good at. Fighting, architecture, and fashion."* He would make Ellion look like the Oracle's chosen. Ellion did not trust his sense of style.

On the way Kaz asked how it had gone with the herd. Ellion had told them the fight would not be necessary, and they had agreed; most probably they would wait for the sacrifice now. Then Kaz asked the real question. The big stone thing: had that been him and Athalea? *"It is definitely not me."*

### The Bucket Sings

The amphitheater was closed at that hour, the gates down. In front of one of the side entrances sat a man in a rusty bucket, singing.

[[The Party/Darius|Darius]] had come back for his armor and the rest of his things, left behind the stage when he changed for the Vigil, and the theater would not open until the evening. While he waited, he sang his way through Kyrah's verses, one after another, all of them, very badly:

> *Oh Kaz, oh Kaz, you lost your horn*
> *to a lady and a drink and a very long dawn…*

They took him for one of the city's drunks. Then, just before the end of the song (Intelligence save), Kaz recognized his own adventure in those terrible, terrible lyrics, the verse about his horn. Kyrah had told that one to him alone. *"Sir, where did you hear those things from? You stole work from our bard! How dare you? Should I call the guards?"* *"No, no. I just retell your beautiful works."* The stories hadn't been published yet. Ellion offered the man a walk in the forest, to clear his head; he knew a good spot. The man said he would rather wait for the theater to open. *"Are you an actor?"* *"Kind of."*

*"Sir, where did you hear the story?"* *"From the source."* *"And the name of the source is?"* *"Kyrah."* *"From where?"* *"For fuck's sake. From the very start."* And he lifted the bucket a little, and small snakes peeked out, and he put it back down.

*"How the fuck did you get back into the city? And why didn't you say who you are?"* Why a *rusty* bucket? He could catch a disease from it. Athalea had given it to him. *"What did you do to offend her this much?"* *"Nothing."*

The last thing he remembered, Darius told them, was a huge rock falling on him; then darkness, then falling, then everything spinning. He had been in the underworld for the second time, and had been given a choice, to rest or to go back, and he had chosen to go back.

Kaz told him the news. Ellion had bought a cursed amulet. Why cursed? Because it came from the Necropolis, the city of the dead, and when a whole place is cursed it seeps into everything in it: *"If you put something in a bowl of water, it's going to get wet."* At the word *Necropolis* Darius sat up. *"There is a guy in the city who knows how to get to the Necropolis? You have to show me this guy. I need to go to the Necropolis."* Ellion described the man: tired, exhausted, skin and bones. Most of the city looks like that right now, Kaz pointed out. Could Ellion recognize him? Yes.

His things were locked inside, and he would not break in. So they put off the makeover and set off to find Claus. On the way, Kaz broke a loaf in half and handed Darius the half.

*"It's a rusty bucket with holes in it. It doesn't function as armor. It doesn't function as a bucket. It only functions as a warning."* *"A warning?"* *"Against drugs."*

### Round Two

First the barracks, the long stone building against the inside of the north wall, a stone's throw from the gate, to get Darius something better to wear. As soon as Kaz stepped in:

*"There you are, coward. Round two."* [[NPCs/Stivon|Stivon]] was in full armor, a spear on his back and a bow.

*"Please don't stab, punch, pierce or bludgeon me."* Kaz's face was still swollen from the morning. *"It's down to only two of you, not three, but the vision hasn't come back in this eye yet. If you punch, do this side."* Stivon told him to wait until mother came back from her palace shift. Then, very calmly, the spear tip came up to Kaz's throat, close enough to press without drawing blood. Was Kaz talking about his sister? Did he even ask consent? *"It was more of a question,"* said Kaz. *"Did* she *receive consent?"* *"She's very assertive. Knows what she wants. Takes what she wants."* *"Good for whom?"* *"For her."*

Why had he brought a man with a bucket on his head? He was an Oracle's chosen, Kaz explained, and needed armor; something from the lost and found would do, anything was better than this. And he was marrying Stivona tonight. Darius and Ellion were hereby, officially and very graciously, invited to the wedding. *"Tonight? Why so fast?"* *"Do you know the term shotgun wedding?"* Nobody did. A short-spear wedding, then. A heavy-crossbow wedding.

Darius, who had backed well away from the spear, turned out to be the Darius Helena had tried to set Stivona up with. Stivon turned to Ellion: who was he? From the Old Wood; Oracle's chosen. *"You're the one who created the Titan."* *"Yes. One of them, probably."* Then how had they done it? Kaz had told them everything already, so there was no need to hide it. Ellion had been speaking Sylvan with the centaurs, that was all. He'd received the plan two minutes before they started. Kaz had brought twenty barrels of wine; maybe the wine had something to do with it. *"So you're telling me this was not your group's doing?"* By then Kaz and Darius were already slipping out of the back door. Ellion went back to his fountain. He liked his fountain.

### Four Pieces, Two Matching

They had done the armor in a hurry (four d4s: 4, 4, 3, 1), and it showed. Two pieces matched and two were from somewhere else entirely. A ceremonial horned helmet, Viking-looking, with big eye holes and no face guard, so Darius's mouth showed. Full plate on the legs. One gladiator's leather shoulder. A chest of riveted leather and chain. Heavy fur-lined boots. Marginally better than the bucket.

Darius led Kaz to a narrow gap between two buildings near the north wall that he seemed to know very well, with clothes lying around in it, and said, *"Come with me."* Kaz asked whether he could roll to find out if he was about to be murdered. He stood twenty steps back, looking away, listening, ready to run. When footsteps came up behind him, he ran, and only turned round at the end of the alley. It was Darius, in the new armor. *"I stand by me running. That is terrifying."* But he did look more like an Oracle's chosen than before. Darius checked himself in a shard of mirror.

### The Dragon's Tooth

The **Dragon's Tooth**, the only and best tavern in the city: if Claus couldn't eat for a week, the first thing he'd do now was eat. Darius realized Kaz was taking him to one of his favorite places in Estoria, the place where he had made friends. He asked Kaz for a silver, to clear his throat. *"What do you think I'm made of, money? I can give you bread."* There would be drinking at the wedding tonight, and Kaz did not want him showing up drunk and embarrassing him; he could get drunk there. Darius had never been drunk, he said, except the one time. *"So you've never been drunk except for the one time you were drunk."* Kaz suggested a meeting for people like him.

The tavern was packed. Kaz was scanning the room for the man Ellion had described when someone stopped dead, stared at Darius, and hurried off to the nearest guard. Two guards came over.

*"Sir, please remove your helmet. This person claims you are cursed."*

Darius tried the truth, of a kind. Yes, he was cursed: he had bought a necklace from a man, and it was from the Necropolis, as he understood now. Everyone knew about Claus, so it was worth a try (Deception, with advantage, and an inspiration on top: a 9, a 5, and another 9). It did not work. Kaz stepped in: *"Oh, hey guys. He's with me. We're looking for the guy who sold the cursed amulet. Claus."* *"Yeah, that's what we were hearing. But the story doesn't track. Remove your helmet, please."* Was the tip from a valid source? *"Any source that tells us there's a cursed person in the city, after what happened near the wall, is a source worth checking."* Kaz tried: being an Oracle's chosen was a kind of curse. A curse of responsibility. The guards were not going to let it go.

Then, as if the moment had been waiting for him, a heartbeat, like war drums: **[[NPCs/Stivona|Stivona]]** came out of the tower and stopped dead.

*"Hello there. Sorry I had to run this morning. You found my letter?"* *"Yes. And are you with this guy?"* *"Oh, he's getting arrested. Want a drink? Of course I'm paying."* She asked if Kaz had seen her brother; he seemed to be avoiding her. Then Kaz asked for help. The idiot had said he was cursed, and now they were arresting him. His name was Darius, an Oracle's chosen even if he didn't look like it, and Kaz thought he might be the one who did the tree at her house. They really needed him. Stivona thought he did need to sit somewhere for a while, for everything he had done. Couldn't she just take him away until they'd talked to the man they were looking for? *"Can I shoot him already?"* Lightly, Kaz said; he's very weak. *"Athalea said I could shoot him later."*

*"I'm taking the prisoner. Come."* She tied his arms, with a loop round his neck, and the guards do not argue with higher command. She walked him off toward the palace, and a few blocks on, turned into an alleyway.

### Claus

Kaz found the man (Investigation 15): in the far corner, very thin, cheeks fallen in, looking terrible, with a king's feast in front of him. *"Good day, sir. May I join you?"* He pushed two chairs together and sat on both, carefully.

Claus was not one to turn down free food, and the way he ate was a gory thing to watch. *"You got rid of the thing?"* *"The thing?"* *"Yes. The thing."* How does one come by such a horrid but interesting object? He had joined an archaeology group going to **Telamok**, hired by historians. None of the others returned. There is a mad thing in there, the keeper of the place; he barely got out. He didn't even know why he had taken anything. *"You do not know what a blessing it is, to taste, and to eat."*

The road, he could never forget. About **three days away**, to the east, but tricky to find across the plains; the historians had a map. First across the plains; then, uncannily, a very good paved road through a field of **ginormous stone arms**, each as tall as a temple, reaching up as if frozen. Getting that far was strangely easy. After that, a thin mountain trail the historians called the **Pass of Death**, with snow underfoot and a long drop on one side, though a goat and a donkey had managed it. Then something started **singing**, a harpy or something worse. He stuffed his ears with wax at once. The others walked toward the edge, toward the song.

Then the **bridges**: three stone ones over a canyon full of mist, and only one still standing, and on the third stood a **robed, silent figure with its hand held out**. He didn't know what it was, so he didn't cross. He climbed down into the canyon and up the other side. He was the only one who climbed, and none of the others came back. All he heard were screams; he hears them every time he closes his eyes. On the far side were tombs, with statues of the dead outside them. He went into only one, and had been starving ever since. *"They said the place is forbidden to all but the Dragonlords."* He had never met one.

His advice: *don't take anything from there. Cover your ears. Do not interact with the hands. Bring food, travel light, watch for falling rocks on the mountain, and tie yourselves together, the way the historians did.*

He was out of work, and if they really wanted to see the place, he could lead them to the start of the pathway, no further, for a price. Kaz didn't bargain; he offered **one gold**, a fortune to him, and the rations for the road, and gave him an advance. They would meet here **tomorrow at sundown**, though it might be a few days before they left; Claus lived in the city and his address changed often. Kaz asked him to eat slowly: after a long starvation, too much too fast can kill you. *"It would be bad to die after all this."*

Being cursed in Estoria, Claus said, was like being sick even after it's gone. Nobody would touch his food or come near him. He was a little weirded out that Kaz had, knowing his story. *"I don't believe a man should be judged his whole life for one bad decision."* *"Same to you, Master Kazak."* *"Please. Just Kazak."* As Kaz left, Claus began to laugh, a little crazily, very quietly.

Outside there was no Stivona and no Darius. In the alley there was only a sword lying on the ground, with Stivona's initials on it, the way the guard marks their weapons. Kaz picked it up, with two drinks in the other hand and a loaf and a half of bread.

### Your Brother Said

In the alley Stivona had looked Darius up and down. He congratulated her on her wedding. *"Do you want those to be your last words?"* Her brother had said she was marrying Kaz. *"What do you mean, I'm marrying Kaz?"* *"We are marrying,"* he explained. *"We are great."*

She went fully white. The only thing keeping him from running was that she was still holding the rope, so tight her knuckles had gone white too.

There was a little slack, and he worked his head free of the loop. His hands were another matter (Sleight of Hand 10): the neck had been for show, but the hands were tied properly. So he kept talking, since it was the only thing that worked. Would the dress be white, or would she wear a toga? Did she have a makeup artist? What flowers was she thinking? And the feast, in a famine? (Persuasion with advantage: a natural 20.)

*"Shut the fuck up. You're coming with me."* And she marched off, dragging him behind her. She had left her sword in the alley.

### A Bag Over the Head

She took him straight through the palace guards who had kept Kaz out the day before. In a hallway she stopped, took something from her bag and put it over his head. Then a long walk, and the sound of the statue opening, and *"Stairs. Careful,"* and down, and a shove.

When he got the bag off he was in a small cell in a natural cave underground, behind bars, with nothing beyond them but more cave wall. Inside were a hide and a metal pot. *"Hey, is anybody here?"* Somewhere, someone told him to stop shouting. He spent a while working the rope off. Water dripped.

### The Map

Athalea's group was not good with time. After half an hour she went to Helena without them, because she had promised.

The door opened on a long room with shuttered windows. A great map of the Heartlands covered the table, held flat by bronze cups and two drawn daggers, with little clay markers pushed across it toward the city. Oil lamps burned at either end. At the head of the table stood an empty high-backed chair with a red cushion, with two bronze shields on the wall behind it. Officers filed out past her without a word. Helena, for once out of armor, was the last one at the map. *"Close the door behind you."*

What had the meeting been about? The siege, the first code black in the city's history: what to do if King Pythor did not allow his daughter to be sacrificed, which was the most likely case. Athalea offered the Oracle's chosen's help. Helena's face went to stone, even more than usual (Insight 19): she was thinking something she didn't want to say out loud.

*"How much do you know of the Order of Sydon?"* Estoria had good reason to think the Order had spies in the city, so the city had sent spies back. That isn't hard when you can play a lunatic praying to a Titan. The Order had received signs from their god. One was the great sacrifice. Another was to get rid of the Oracle's chosen.

Athalea had already got there. The only way to get Anora out of the sacrifice was to sacrifice *them*; she had told the others so, and they had partly agreed. Sydon didn't like them; he would be pleased if they ceased to exist. Helena took that in, and **officially invited the Oracle's chosen to the meeting with the Order of Sydon's delegates in two days.**

Then Athalea asked for the king. One of their friends was in dire need of his wisdom. *"The king is not receiving. Not today, and probably not tomorrow. Wait a few days. He's tired. Kings get tired."* In his present state, Helena said, he cared for a very short list of things: his daughter, his drink.

*"Can we keep this between us?"* Athalea said. She knew the king's sister, and she was dying.

Helena didn't follow. King Pythor's sister was on Mount Mytros, or at the bottom of some cup, wherever gods are. No, said Athalea: the goddess of music, Kyrah, and she was dying, and that was why she needed him. They got tangled in who was who among the Five, and Helena was firm: Kyrah was on the mountain with the other gods. *"Gods are known to walk among mortals,"* Athalea said. *"Sometimes."* There were at least a hundred girls calling themselves goddesses around Estoria during the wine festival, Helena said; she wasn't letting anyone into the king's rooms for gossip. *"I understand that the person in my house might have been good at making you believe something. But I know for sure Kyrah is on Mount Mytros. I trust my sources."*

Then would she at least take him the message, exactly as Athalea said it? That his sister was the girl at her house, and she was dying. Athalea had kept the rules of her house, and that counted (Persuasion with advantage: 25). *"Very well. Wait here. Don't let anyone come in."*

Alone, Athalea read the map. There was a tiny bird-like figurine on it, and figures for each of them, all at the sacrificial stone in the river. Around the centaurs were many human figures that didn't look like militia, and some big cyclops-like ones. More human figures stood out beyond the city on the far side, away from the herd. In Estoria itself there were only two small figures, one more masculine, lying down, and one more feminine, standing. While the Oracle's chosen did their part, the council was planning to **evacuate the city.**

She spent the rest of the wait writing verses.

### The Nose Knows

Kaz went to the palace to meet Athalea. The guards at the front gate looked confused. *"You seem to stick your nose into things that are none of your business."* *"Fair enough. I do have a big nose."* Had they seen a captain go by with a man on a leash, looking furious? Into the palace. *"How did you know which captain I meant?"* *"Oracle's chosen. It's my job to know this."*

Nobody was on the way to the dungeon. So he used his secret weapon (Perception 18): a very strong trail, heavy right where he stood, as if the person had just been there, and it led toward the royal quarters. In front of the king's door stood the same two royal guards. Kaz offered them bread, and grape juice. From behind the door came a loud crash. The guards did not flinch. *"Seen any good plays lately? The big thing outside yesterday, that was crazy. I was scared."* Nothing. *"That's okay. I'll find her myself."*

At the war room door, a guard inside had been told to let no one in. *"Is my bride in there?"* No. So he talked through the door to Athalea. He'd found the others. *"Ellion is cursed now, and Darius is in prison."* Ellion had bought a cursed amulet, and now Kaz knew where the Necropolis was and how to get there; Darius had said he was cursed in a tavern full of people, and Stivona had arrested him and might have beaten him up. Did Athalea want to come to the dungeon? She was waiting for Helena.

Then a huge hand came down on his shoulder. *"Why are you still wearing the same clothes as yesterday?"* It was ten in the morning. *"I will rephrase. Why are you not in your uniform, soldier? Get yourself in order. Never come to the palace like this again. A disgrace to the guards."* *"Yes, ma'am."* He went. He had put in for leave to be an Oracle's chosen, and apparently it hadn't gone through.

### He Heard You

Helena's face, so hard a minute before, softened a little. *"He heard your message very well."* It had not changed anything.

*"He's condemning his own sister,"* said Athalea, and then, *"I'm sorry. You're higher up. I shouldn't have said that."*

*"Try the Whisperer."* The young one had disappeared, along with a lot of other young people: the vineyard man's daughter, and other youngsters from the city. Gods do walk among the people sometimes, Helena allowed. And if what Athalea said about the missing was true, could her merry group look into it? The old Whisperer was very old, but she still sometimes saw patients. As for Helena, she wouldn't be leaving the palace until the delegation had come and gone safely.

Before she went, Athalea asked about the dates: why had Helena been trying to set Stivona up? *"Just to get her out of the house."* She only chose the promising ones, Oracle's chosen or guards. *"I think she got what she wanted,"* said Athalea. *"I hardly think so. What she wants is to leave Estoria and build her own greatness."* *"Maybe she will."* *"Not until she's old enough to know that."* How old was she? The same as Stivon.

And if one of the Oracle's chosen was in the dungeon, could they get him out? *"Wait. Why is one of you in the dungeon?"* *"Ask your daughter."* Helena knew nothing about it. *"How grave must the crime have been,"* she said, and went for some questioning.

Kaz came back about ten minutes later, marginally cleaner, in a guard's uniform: the metal skirt and the shoulder guard, and otherwise mostly as naked as before. *"It needs a tie."* He told Athalea about Claus and the meeting tomorrow at sundown. Healer first, Athalea said. And the council wanted them to sacrifice themselves instead of Anora. *"Solid move. Killing basilisks first, then agreeing to sacrifice ourselves."*

And Kaz had questions of his own. Apparently the Titan had not been Athalea's doing, which was a surprise to him. *"Brother, I sing. I don't do that."* Gods do walk among the people sometimes, Helena had said. So she flew off over the gardens to see one, at Volkan's Hall.

### Ellion at the Steps

Ellion went back to his fountain. The necklace he had left on its edge was nowhere to be found. He spent an hour there, looking at the sky. Whenever he went near a merchant, they walked away. He went to the palace, where the guards wouldn't let him in either, and asked them about the huge minotaur who'd walked into the palace that day, just as Kaz came out. *"And who is this?"* *"Camille."* *"If you say my name, I'll punch you."* *"It is your name. Your given name."*

Kaz went to the stables first, to check on the donkey. [[NPCs/Sir Ferdinand|Freddy]] was the only animal left there and very excited to see him, and kept telling him something, probably about his day. Kaz couldn't speak donkey, but if Freddy had had a good day, could he tap a hoof once? Freddy tapped for about five minutes. There was only hay. Kaz promised carrots.

Then he came back for Ellion on the steps. *"I'm going to see my grandfather. You want to come?"*

On the way Ellion admitted he couldn't eat. Every thought of food, grilled meat above all, made him sick. That would be the curse, Kaz said. They settled on code words: the curse was *green*, and when it came to Darius, *medusa* was *Bruno*. And Ellion had something else: last night, in the forest, he had seen [[NPCs/Mr. Flufster (Master Flamster)|Mr. Flufster]]. He was somewhere nearby, and kind of okay, actually. And once, out in the fields, a mouse had told him its master was gathering an army. Kaz, who knew what mice bring into a city, found that utterly terrifying.

### State Your Crime

Darius heard heavy steps coming, stopping, and then felt a stare. He pretended to be drunk (Performance 2).

*"State your crime."* *"Being alive."* *"Stand up and state your crime."* Public disturbance. Pretending to be drunk. *"You don't want me to come in there, Oracle's chosen."* Then hands pulled him up off the hide, and he was in the air.

*"I don't have time for your shit, Oracle's chosen. What is your crime? I know it must have been grave. And why are you wearing my guards' uniforms? I count to five, and then I start beating the confession out of you."*

He told her the truth: that Stivona was marrying Kaz.

She slammed him against the wall, the air went out of his lungs, and she began to choke him (Constitution save, failed). Then he didn't see or hear anything at all.

### Bring His Best Weapon

Athalea was flying toward Volkan's Hall when she saw Helena, who had said she wouldn't leave the palace, come out of it to the palace guard at the front and tell them something (Perception): *bring* and *here* was all she caught. The guards are posted outside and aren't supposed to leave, and they looked very weirded out, but Helena's face was not one to argue with. Athalea had never seen it show so much emotion. She was furious. Then she turned back up the steps.

Athalea came down a little, staying well out of reach. *"Are you all right? Does hospitality still apply?"* It did. *"But I need your help. I need a meeting with Camille."* *"Oh. So you do know."* *"In the war room, in thirty minutes. Make him bring his best weapon."* *"Oh, so you'll kill him."* *"Most probably."* She would cut off tiny parts of him, and then the sacrificing would be easier. *"For the sacrifice he needs to be alive."* *"He will be alive."*

And Darius, Athalea asked: once Helena was done with him, could they take him out of the city? *"Once I'm done with a particular someone, we can exchange hostages."* The palace guard watched all this with a pale face. Athalea thought about it, then flew in, patted Helena on the head, *"It'll be all right,"* and flew away. Helena looked back at the guards and marched up into the palace.

### Outside Volkan's Hall

They met in front of the forge. Kaz was talking about his grandfather. *"These have been a very good two days. I'm getting married, I met my father, turns out I have a sister, and now I'm meeting my grandfather."*

*"How much do you value your junk?"* asked Athalea. A certain lady in charge of him wanted it separated from his body. *"She wants to take my arms off?"* *"Lower."* War room, thirty minutes, bring his best weapon. She doubted he would come out with his private parts still attached. *"I'm very confused. But an order is an order."* And bring Stivona, she suggested. He hadn't been able to find her; he wanted to talk to her about last night. Somebody had told Helena he was marrying her daughter, Athalea said, and Helena didn't think her daughter was old enough for that sort of thing, and wanted him six feet under, or parts of him. *"Because you slept with her daughter,"* said Ellion.

Stivon, Kaz said, wasn't a problem at all. *"He hit you in the face with a shield. You were bleeding out of your nostrils for an hour."* *"That's just friends being stupid. We used to do that all the time."* *"If this is what friends do,"* said Ellion, *"I don't have any friends."* Helena had ordered the gate guards to find him and drag him in, Athalea said, so it would be better to go himself. And they weren't going anywhere with Claus in two days: in two days the Order's delegation was coming.

*"Please don't die."* *"Don't plan to."* *"Tell my grandpa I said hi."*

### Volkan's Hall

Near the center of town stood a large building with fortified stone walls and a shingle roof. A bronze sign hung from its beams: **Volkan's Hall**. Inside something rattled, something fell, and a young man poked his head out of the door. Did they have an appointment? No, but it was very urgent. *"Well, okay. Come on in."*

Inside was the warm glow of embers and blasts of hot air, and bronze armor, weapons and farming tools all over the walls. At the anvil by the blistering forge worked a withered-looking dwarf. He wiped his brow. *"Greetings, travelers. Do you need something forged?"*

They needed advice, in private. The boy was his apprentice, the dwarf said; but it was very hot in there, and might burn her feathers, so: *"[[NPCs/Theo|Theo]], go get the delivery."* *"Yes, Master [[NPCs/Volkan|Volkan]]."* And he ran out.

*"I have one very weird question, and I hope you won't take me for a madwoman."* *"Speak your mind, kid."* *"Are you a god?"* *"Yes."* *"Great. Another god is dying, and we need some advice."* *"Which one?"* *"Kyrah."* *"Kyrah. Lovely name. My daughter's name."* He went on hammering. *"How many girls are called that right now?"*

Not many, Athalea said, who talk to the Oracle regularly and do magic every day, and songs. The best bad songs. Then she recited one, and it was hard to forget:

> *The ocean took your flock away*
> *and you have not had a nice day since,*
> *not one, not once, not any day,*
> *the sea is big and also wet*
> *and I am sorry, and also this rhymed with "regret"*
> *somewhere around line three, I promise, trust the process.*

On his face: instant recognition, and then the look of someone hearing a very badly tuned instrument. He put the hammer down, which he hadn't done once, not even when they came in. He looked at Athalea, then at the forge, then back at her.

*"If that's my girl… Gods don't catch cold, and they don't get sick. So if it is her, it means she broke the Oath."* The Oath binds them, and takes its due from whoever breaks it. *"That's the law. I helped forge it. She knew the price."* He picked the hammer back up. *"No god will help, child. None of us will break the Oath, not even for one another. We are not allowed to guide you, or help you."*

Then how could a giant thing walk around like that, Athalea asked, if they're all so bound? *"If you get far enough, you will learn."* Was there anything at all, then, that they could do for her, not with his help, just so they understood? *"This is not the first time."*

Could he forge things? *"Anything, given the proper instruments."* Athalea was sorry about his children, both of them. And his grandson had asked her to say he would be excited to meet him. *"Which one?"* The chosen one. Camille. *"Who's Camille?"* Kazak, from the city. Volkan asked his mother's name, and Athalea, who is bad with names, couldn't quite bring it up. *"Good. He wants something forged? He may come."*

Out in the street, Theo stood in front of the forge, not knowing what to do. Athalea took out her notes and changed a few things. Her plan was a mass prayer session that evening to the goddess of music: perhaps they could change tonight's festival play so the audience joined in.

### Blood of Her Blood

Right there in the street, a puff of steam appeared in front of Athalea's face: the **mephit**, back from the Oracle's temple. It began at once, in its thick Primordial, repeating after someone, one word at a time:

*"[[NPCs/Versi|Versi]]. Says. Blood. Of. Her. Blood. Then. Sing. Then. Hurry. Question mark? No. No question mark."*

Blood of her blood. Whose? Stivona's? Helena's? Kaz, as a nephew? Pythor, as a brother? Prayer would not hurt either way. Ellion wanted to go home to the Old Wood. *"No. We have a world to save."* *"We are not saving the world. He's going to be castrated soon, and he's in prison, and he's a Bruno."* Athalea, who has only one body, put things in order: gather everyone, see how badly beaten they all were, then the missing people, then the horn. The wedding was presumably off, so there would be time for prayers in the evening, and she could write on the road.

There were no guards at the palace door at all now. Ellion had tried to get in twice that day, past a ton of guards.

### A Lot of Beating for One Kiss

Kaz stepped into the war room. The table was broken in half. Betsy immediately took a sword to the side.

*"Hey, boss, what's happening?"* The attacks continued. He did not understand at all; as far as he could tell his commander had simply started laying into him. After the first blow he put Betsy down, stood at attention, and took it like a man.

When the others knocked, Kaz was on his last hit point and barely standing, bleeding from everywhere. One shoulder guard hung off him; the other lay in a corner, broken. He was every color of the rainbow. Part of his ear was gone, and his spare horn was half broken again. Helena sat in the chair, looking at him. She had not said a word through the whole thing. *"Come in."*

*"Listen to me closely, son of Pythor. I know your blood. I have watched your blood for many years. You are your father's son, and I shall not give you my blessing. If you come anywhere near my daughter again, you are not coming back into this city, no matter whose son you are or who chose you. You are dismissed from your duties until the end of your crusade. Then we can talk. Permission to speak granted."*

He opened his mouth and about half a liter of blood came out onto the floor. *"Why have I been reprimanded?"* *"No personal relationships in the army. And I will not let my daughter end up like your mother."*

The medical kits were in the barracks; then they were to take him to the Whisperer. And she went out, toward the dungeon.

*"That was a lot of beating for one kiss,"* said Kaz. *"It was worth it, though."* *"Just one kiss?"* And it came out that, as far as Kaz was concerned, nothing else had happened. A man and a woman lie together, he explained, and things enter other things, and that's how babies are made, and they had done no such thing. She had kissed him, and then they slept in the same room, the way people do in the barracks all the time. Everybody, Athalea told him, including Stivon and Helena, had assumed things entered other things. *"I would never. You told me to be a gentleman."* She suggested he save the city first, so he would be an eligible bachelor, and then talk to Stivona. *"At least I'm on leave now."*

The door creaked open, and Helena came back and put something on the floor: a hide, rolled up. *"Do you want to tell me something before I find out for myself? I'll give you ten seconds."* Kaz offered, *"He's green now."* *"Thank you for sharing that. Anything else?"* Ten seconds passed. She pulled the hide, and it unrolled across the floor, and out of it rolled Darius, **fully naked**: no armor, no helmet, nothing.

*"Is he dead?"* *"It's Darius."* *"Pleased to meet you."* Helena was unmoved. If it had been her choice, she would never have chosen them. *"As of now, the Oracle's chosen's invitation to the palace is revoked. If you come to the meeting in two days, the guards will bring you in. Think about your story. After the meeting I want explanations from each and every one of you: why I, who was gracious to the Oracle's chosen and am in charge of protecting this city, do not know very important things."* She looked at Darius. *"Don't answer now. I don't want to hear it now."* They were still welcome at her house if they needed somewhere to stay. But there were two guards outside to escort them out of the palace.

Athalea flew up, took Kaz by the one horn he still had attached, and towed him toward the exit. Darius, without his armor, was light enough to carry, and dragging him wasn't good for him; Kaz put him over his shoulder. *"Thank you for going easy on me,"* he told Helena on his way out.

Outside they met the two front-gate guards, still running around the city looking for a Kaz who had already gone to the war room, and very confused about what they were supposed to do with the Oracle's chosen now. Would they be so kind as to escort them to the Whisperer? *"That we can do."*

*"Care for some bread?"*
