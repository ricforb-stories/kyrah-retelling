# Session 9 — September 5, 2026 — "Those Are Mine"

**Level:** 2
**Party Members in Attendance:** [[The Party/Ellion|Ellion]], [[The Party/Darius|Darius]], [[The Party/Xhazag (Kaz)|Xhazag (Kaz)]], [[The Party/Athalea|Athalea]]

## Our Adventure

### A Perfect Place to Camp

Still a day and a half out from **Estoria**, it was time to make camp. They had the temple's blankets and cloaks, and the makeshift tent from the burnt field, which fits three-ish people, or two people and one minotaur. They needed to hunt. [[NPCs/Sir Ferdinand|Sir Ferdinand]] raised one hoof: he was no help in that regard.

They wanted somewhere off the road, deep enough that passers-by wouldn't wander in, and close enough to run back to civilization if wolves came. About seventy feet into the woods, [[The Party/Darius|Darius]] found an almost perfect clearing, roomy enough for the cart, and pronounced it perfect. *"Nothing suspicious."* [[The Party/Xhazag (Kaz)|Kaz]] wanted it checked: a man in a full helmet is not the party's best pair of eyes, and Darius hadn't given him any reason to trust him. *"He died to a rug."*

Kaz, the party's self-appointed architect, checked it himself (Investigation 19). The clearing was used regularly by hunters, but there were no lodges anywhere nearby and no villages. Who would hunt this far out? Not the temple, which had lived on bread and cheese. Someone suggested they keep moving, on nothing but a hunch. *"It's a perfect place. There are no people living anywhere close by." "And there's probably a reason for that."* They agreed to move on.

They were about a minute too late.

### The Maenad

Something rustled through the bushes. Kaz readied himself to grapple. Athalea hid in the trees with her bow and readied herself to shoot anyone who touched the party, or the donkey. The donkey came first.

Into the clearing came a very unkempt woman with the feet and horns of a goat, singing raucously and swigging from a filthy cup of wine. Merrymaking with her came three squat, bipedal goat-things. The sweet smell of roast pork wafted from the corpse of a dwarf they were carrying with them, and cooking as they went.

[[The Party/Athalea|Athalea]] had spent five years among creatures like these, and she knew what she was looking at. You are in a terrible place at a terrible time, she understood. She might personally have talked her way out of it. Not her friends.

The woman locked eyes with them, laughed hysterically, and began to dance. Everyone had to resist it (a Charisma save). [[The Party/Ellion|Ellion]] failed, laughed exactly as she did, and began repeating every move she made, dancing crazily around the clearing. The little ones laughed too. Their song had no words in it, only a mixture of sounds, something like Athalea's burial rites.

### The Dance

The woman danced over to Ellion, who danced with her, and clawed at his eyes, then at his wrists, trying to pull him deeper into the dance, leaving claw marks on them. Kaz's readied attack and Darius's *sacred flame* (with his -4) landed at the same time.

Athalea, still unseen in the tree, dropped a whisper into the woman's head: ***"Those are mine."*** It was the most terrifying thing she had ever heard (14 psychic), and she ran. Darius rang a bell that wasn't there, *toll the dead*, with a soothing *"Calm yourself, child,"* for eight necrotic. Ellion snapped out of the trance (16) to find himself covered in scratches, very disappointed in himself and very angry. Kaz raged and brought Betsy down at her head. She twisted at the last moment, and it took her on the collarbone (12).

The goatlings didn't bother with weapons. *"Did your mother feed you dragon dung?"* (Kaz, save 18: *"No, but I'll feed you to a dragon."*) *"You're sapping out!"* One started singing to Ellion about how they'd suck him dry, and the song drew real blood. *"Need an opener? Come right here."* Their insults hurt.

The next round, others started dancing too. Kaz caught the woman across the back as she ran (15). Darius went down under her claws, still dancing where he lay. Athalea landed *healing word* on him from her tree: *"If I see you dance in the next couple of minutes, I will kill myself."* Ellion, still under the spell, turned into a **giant badger** and burrowed three feet into the ground for full cover. He wasn't leaving; he wasn't going to leave his dear friend, the donkey. **Sir Ferdinand** was dancing on his hind legs. Kaz kept tenderizing the back of her.

The party had put about seventy damage into her by then, and she looked exactly the same: crazy eyes, blazing, laughing.

Then a goatling shouted, *"I hear you growling, mother!"* The woman rippled apart, and a big brown bear stood where she had been.

### The Struggle Cuddle

Darius used his Channel Divinity to heal himself (eight, after a four doubled), and as the magic went out, Sir Ferdinand, still dancing on his back legs, began to glow pink with faerie fire. *"Fabulous."*

Ellion as a badger bit one goatling to death (17, seven piercing) and savaged another (24), soaking himself in goat blood until it looked very much like a sacrificial rite. He sacrificed both to Thylea on the spot, and the whole party was blessed for a minute. When it went through him, he made a Wisdom save (25) and heard something chuckle in his head.

Kaz decided that the bear was getting off his dying friends. He grappled it from behind (a natural 20, 24 total), hoisted it above his head, and carried it away. *"Run. I'll hold them off and join you later."*

The bear fought back and bit him in places he'd rather it hadn't (a slash for seven). Athalea told a goatling that its mother had lain with a goat on two legs because she couldn't pull a goat on four (six psychic). That one was out.

Darius's plan was to save the donkey. Sir Ferdinand was still harnessed to the cart and moved at fifteen feet. Darius's spear doesn't cut, so he fished out a dagger he'd never used, since he'd carried a spear all his life, and started sawing at the harness under the donkey's belly (*"be very, very careful"*). A harness, it turned out, is harder to cut than a commoner. Kaz, meanwhile, dragged the bear thirty feet with him. He put Betsy away and headbutted it (seven). *"Are you finally flirting with it?"* It was, the table agreed, a struggle cuddle.

Then the bear looked him right in the eyes, and spoke into his mind:

> *"I might have had some fun with you and let you go, once I had had my food. But now that my children are dead, you all will be too. Unless…"*

It tried to break his grip (26 against 24). His hug was stronger.

Athalea's mockery missed. *"I said those were mine."* And to Kaz: *"Do what you want, but don't sleep with it."*

Ellion clawed and bit it (19, eight slashing), and the bear came apart and was a woman again, still in Kaz's arms. Kaz headbutted her twice. *"You attacked us first. We can stop fighting if you want. Unless what?"*

Her answer came as she danced in his arms and began, very deliberately, to undress. *"Unless I receive a good substitute instead."* A substitute for the children. Athalea to the maenad: *"My little honey, you won't find any more kids here and won't get anything from these guys. I'm saying it for the third time. You don't understand. Those are mine."*

Kaz answered with his handaxe (11 slashing) and his other arm, trying to put her clothes back on while he stabbed her. *"I'm sorry, you're not my type. I like my women not trying to kill me. But I'm sure you'll find someone."* *"That's all right. I can play with all of you after you die."*

She slashed the badger (10 and 12). Ellion came back out of the form, and when he did, the others saw that his whole skin had turned blue.

### The Thing That Would Not Die

Nothing seemed to stick. An arrow grazed her hand, and the wound closed back up. Darius kept ringing the bell. Ellion threw an *ice knife* (nine). Kaz threw her ten feet (Athletics) and ran, Ellion over his shoulder, like a baby, or a very hairy shoulder-mounted accessory. At the start of each round she was visibly no worse than before. More psychic, more magic stones. Nothing.

Ellion had been closer to her than anyone, and had seen what hit her and what didn't. He studied her (Investigation) and was willing to bet everything he had: everything harmed her. She simply *walked a thin line between life and death*.

Athalea knew why (History 21). These creatures value one thing in life: pleasure, of any kind. The woman didn't even feel aggression toward them. She was having fun. And Lutheria had given them a gift: whenever they are at death's door, she gives them a chance. It was a run of luck, and one that came very easily to them. It reminded Athalea of how they had survived the last fight, on one hit point after another, against Helica, when Kaz broke off his horn for the goddess Kyrah. And even if they killed her, it was the blessing of the fey. Who knew whether she'd come back? Athalea had also noticed that the woman talked to every one of them except her. She advised them to give her something more fun to do, like a set of keys.

### The Helmet

Darius was down, and she was clawing at him. Kaz went to him, put himself between them so that all she could see was his backside, and, without looking, lifted the flap of Darius's helmet, poured a healing potion in (eight), and closed it again.

He never looked at Darius's face. *"I'm very appreciative."* *"I didn't want to look at it."* *"Because of your oath."* *"Exactly."*

Then Kaz grappled her again (26) and hauled her as far away from Darius as he could. Darius was stable. She raked Kaz again (six, then seven), and he threw a rope around her.

Darius, still ringing the same bell, rang it once more. For once, the bell worked. She dropped, failed the save, and stayed down.

Kaz tied her up anyway. *"She could have been so much more."* Nobody trusted it.

### Kyrah

In all of this, where was Kyrah? Not there. While Kaz began to dig, Athalea went looking. Half an hour of retracing their steps, and she'd lost all idea where Kyrah could be, until she looked up and found her asleep on a high branch, a big ball of fur. She grabbed the burrito, took off with the burrito, and flew back with the burrito. Delivery.

### Other People's Territory

Darius kept the lookout (Perception 16, even with his -4). The longer he looked into the trees, the more uncomfortable he became. There were strange patterns of plants and mushrooms on the ground. There were tiny trinkets tied high in the treetops. Some of them looked like humanoid dolls. Some of them, on a longer look, looked like body parts. *"Guys, we should go."* Athalea: *"I told you so."*

Athalea had found a better spot while searching for Kyrah: cramped, between trees, but with none of the signs. That was probably where somebody else's territory ended. They loaded the bodies onto the cart. Kaz put the donkey on his back and pulled the cart himself, Athalea carried Kyrah, and they went.

As they left, behind them came bursts of bleating laughter, and six little humanoid goats came out of the trees. The party ran for three rounds straight and didn't look back.

The new spot checked out (Perception 15 with *guidance* and help). Strategically it was worse ground, but it had none of the signs, so it was much safer.

### Burying Them

Did those things have souls? Athalea explained what they were. The maenad and her goatlings had been people, killed, then cursed, or blessed, whichever you called it, against their will, until everything they knew in life was this. And Lutheria would probably be angry if they killed her creations and didn't send them back to her. So they buried everyone: the maenad, her three goatlings, and the dwarf, who had no clothes left and was almost well done.

Kaz dug on all fours with his handaxe, since it was better for digging than a club. It was the third time in a weekend. (*"We really need to get a shovel next time we stop anywhere."*) Ellion laid a branch of mistletoe on the two goatlings he had sacrificed, just in case.

Athalea performed the rite. She scraped out a smaller hole, put the offal in, and set it alight. It smelled terrible, and somehow fit. Then she fed a handful of her smoking-herbs into the fire until it smelled really, really good. With coins from a bowl she'd taken from Sydon's temple, she laid coins on the eyes of everyone who had eyes, and in the eye sockets of the burnt one. She breathed in a lungful of smoke from her pipe, untied the rope, and said something in Celestial that nobody could follow. She kissed each of the children on the forehead, went to the dwarf, spoke again, and laid a single feather on the burnt child's forehead. Then she flew up and stood behind the fire, which had somehow grown as tall as she was, and sang in a very high bird's voice. It felt shamanic, and for a brief moment the air felt just like the place they had fled. Then the flame dropped, the feeling went, and she sat down hard, smoking and breathing raggedly. The table granted everyone inspiration, and one more for the rite.

Athalea had also gone foraging (19): herbs, mushrooms, berries, enough for a small dinner. Then she spent forty minutes hunting and came back with a stag. *"You said you're not a hunter."* *"I never hunt."* She skinned and butchered it with her knife, very fast, covered to the elbows in blood, for someone who never fights, awfully skilled with a knife. Kaz straightened his back from the digging. *"Remind me not to anger her, like, ever."* She offered him the hide.

Ellion asked the trees what they knew. Trees are slow, and the message came down the line from tree to tree: *rituals, burnings, dancing.* When he asked about predators, the answer took a while, and turned out to be *beaver, beaver, beaver.*

### What They Took

On the maenad were a small carved drinking horn, decorated with grapes; a very pretty goblet tied to her belt; and a little vial of something. Kaz was playing a hero character and could not loot her, so Athalea did. She knew exactly what the vial was, and she wasn't sharing. *"If you ask, I'll answer, but I'm not starting this conversation."* She strongly recommended that nobody touch it.

### What Kyrah Said

Kyrah woke up to a campsite full of bodies. *"Why bodies?"* *"Because we're burying them. We are good people."* *"You missed a good fight. Would have made a good song. I wrestled with a bear who was a woman. She wanted to have sex with me."*

What was it? *"A human."* *"Not anymore."* *"As human as you are."* *"I'm a minotaur."* *"A cursed human."* Athalea told Kaz he'd have known what the creature was if he'd listened to what she said to it the whole fight. *"You kept saying we are yours, which is a little concerning."* It was the only way they could understand anything, she said.

*"It wanted me to give her children. Because we killed hers."* Kyrah thought the goatlings joked around at most. *"And it hurts."* She looked at Ellion: she hadn't taken him for a killer. He had sacrificed them. Why did they kill a dwarf? They hadn't. *"I propose this whole part stays out of the song."* But the wrestling with the bear was good; that could stay. Those were cursed kids, and that was their fate from birth. At least, someone said, they'd be happier in the realm they'd gone to.

Then Kyrah made her points. First: if anyone ever found out about this, every scrap of fame they had been building would evaporate. And second: fame is a tricky thing in a group; one can outshine the others. She thought they should swear the **Oath of Fellowship** at some point. They'd share fame, and who knew, maybe there would be other benefits along the way. The party liked it in principle. *"Sometime in the future. Not now."* *"Maybe tomorrow."*

They moved on another half hour. After a while the sudden bursts of laughter in the night stopped, the signs in the trees stopped, and the woods seemed mostly normal.

### The Dream

As they were falling asleep, a woman's voice came out of the darkness around them.

> *"So… your great quest begins. Off to become heroes, are you? Mortals are so very proud."*

A light shone in the dark on the sad, pathetic figure of a withered old man, hogtied to a stake.

> *"Look at this one. Falling to pieces, joints stiff, skin slack, teeth gone. The life is practically dripping out of him."*

Inky black shadows fell across him. He flinched and squirmed, panicking as something drew closer, something they couldn't see.

> *"Yes, his sad mortal life is leaking away. But I wonder what will happen to his soul?"*

The old man thrashed against his restraints, screaming, as the darkness smothered him.

> *"He bleeds light and fades away…"*

All four of them woke at once in the middle of the night, soaked in sweat. None of them knew the man. Darius recognized the voice (Insight 15): it was **Lutheria**, and he had heard it before. But something about it was not right. It was as if, before, he had only ever listened to a recording, and only now, in the dream, was he hearing the whole of it.

Kyrah didn't wake. Only the four of them had seen it.
