# Session 11 — September 25, 2026 — "With Friends Like These"

**Level:** 2
**Party Members in Attendance:** [[The Party/Ellion|Ellion]], [[The Party/Darius|Darius]], [[The Party/Xhazag (Kaz)|Xhazag (Kaz)]], [[The Party/Athalea|Athalea]]

## Our Adventure

### The Kitchen

[[The Party/Darius|Darius]] was in [[NPCs/Helena|Helena]]'s kitchen, and he had a problem. He had come, he explained, because her son [[NPCs/Stivon|Stivon]] had invited him. *"No, your mom actually invited him."* To be Stivona's date for the play. And the first thing he said when he saw Stivona was that he wasn't her date. Did he have a date to the play, then? No. Then a lie (Deception, which failed). *"You come into my house and you don't even oblige the rules of hospitality. The boy is being rude and lying to me. Your helmet is twitching. I see condensation below it."*

[[The Party/Athalea|Athalea]] looked at the window, considered it, and flew out of it. She had food to deliver.

Darius thanked Helena for her hospitality and made for the door. A hand landed on his shoulder. *"Where do you think you're going?"* Helena turned him round and pushed him back into the kitchen.

### Food Runs

Athalea flew over the walls; nobody minds a siren going over them. She took small portions she could carry, flew them out to the three farms, and came back for more. The cyclopes were still out there, so she went quietly (Stealth 21). She picked houses with no cyclopes near them, and knocked softly, so nobody would take her for a cyclops trying to lure them out.

A little crack opened. *"Hello. I brought some food. Want some?"* *"Are you an angel?"* *"I have been. But I'm not."* Did they want songs, too? After the whole cyclops business, she could give a concert. Did they have a kettle or a bucket?

Then someone asked her which deity had sent her. It was an important moment for somebody's reputation. Athalea froze, and processed. *"The Oracle has sent me and my comrades to prevent all mortal races from extinction. This is my contribution."* From inside the dark house, all they could see was her outline against the sun, which looked, frankly, angelic. They stared, and then somebody shouted at somebody else, and a bucket was lowered for the stew. *"We shall pray for you."*

### Round Two

Back in the kitchen, Darius tried again. He'd promised his friends in the palace. *"You're a bad liar. You, a rankless merc, have friends in the palace?"* He was one of the heroes of the prophecy, he said. The Oracle's. *"Can you f— me any harder? Just leave. Why are you still here?"* Helena filled the doorway. The only other exit was the window, and he wasn't getting through it in that armor.

Then he saw [[NPCs/Kyrah|Kyrah]], who had finished her tour of the house (they had a pretty cool hunting collection), wander into the living room. He waved. He started whispering to her by *message*: they were holding him, they wanted him to go on a date with Stivona, could she help him get out of this house?

Kyrah's first reply: *"You do see the irony, with your curse? You are being forced into a relationship you don't want."*

And then an oak tree burst up out of the kitchen floor, straight through the roof. Stivona leapt aside. Helena went into full battle mode. And Kyrah's second message: ***"Run."***

He ran.

When Athalea came back for another load, the house had an oak tree growing out of it and Darius was sprinting out of the door. Kyrah, strolling out after him: *"Hostage situation, I think."* She'd been admiring the hunting collection, he'd started waving at her, then started talking in her head, then the tree, then he ran. It was, she agreed, also kind of ironic. They set a meeting point, the amphitheater, in about an hour.

### Where Darius Ran

Darius ran in the opposite direction from the women, and kept going. North, through a bazaar, past the houses, to a round plaza with a great pool where the oxen had drunk, until he hit the northern wall and there was nowhere left to run. He worked out his way from there (Survival 19): turn right at the circle and follow the hills; the palace is almost built into them.

### Father and Son, Continued

In the palace, [[NPCs/Pythor|Pythor]] was still telling [[The Party/Xhazag (Kaz)|Kaz]] about his mother, *almost thirty years ago now*. How was Camille's life going? *"First of all, I go by Kazak."* *"You go where?"* Not particularly hard, not particularly easy. He'd been in the guards for a while. Now he was summoned by the Oracle.

[[The Party/Ellion|Ellion]] quietly used *purify food and drink* on the king's wine. Then he asked, in his own language, for permission to come and go from the city freely. Pythor gave it: nobody pays to enter Estoria. But going out during the red alert? That was because of the cyclopes. *"What did they teach you in the guards, Camille?"* And why stay at an inn when the palace had plenty of rooms?

Then Kaz asked to see his sister. *"Camille, you need to learn to speak normally,"* said Pythor. *"Practice on me."* So Kaz rehearsed with his father, who played Anora: *free me from this dungeon, brother; I know the tunnels under the palace, I know where to find the keys; father will never know.* Kaz would not. *"Cross my heart, hope to die."* Pythor wanted an oath on his mother, with her name, which led to a long standoff over which of them actually remembered it, until Kaz swore:

> *"I, Camille, swear on my mother Ophea that when I go down now to talk to my sister in the dungeons, I will not attempt to free her."*

*"Very well."* Pythor put down his goblet and stood up, and in a second his face was completely sober. *"Let's go, then."*

He took a spear with him and walked very fast, right, left, right, forward, left, through corridor after corridor, to a statue of himself. He tapped the spear against it in several places. Kaz tried to memorize the sequence (a natural 2) and couldn't. The statue slid aside onto stairs going down into the dark.

Below, the corridors opened out into a cave system, like the Oracle's temple, with cells dug straight into the rock and bars set into the stone without any doors. The prisoners pressed to the bars and reached out as they passed. Ellion moved everything he valued in front of him where he could see it. It didn't help: somewhere down there, someone stole his druidic focus.

### Anora

At the far end, set apart, was a cell that looked nothing like the others: rugs on the floor, paintings, figurines, vases, a great barrel of wine, a gilded bed. On the bed sat the girl from the portrait, tinkering with some sort of contraption, and she didn't look up. *"If you have not come here to let me go, Father, I'm in no mood to speak. Even to my half-brother."*

Then she looked at Ellion, stood, smoothed her dress, and bowed. *"To what do I owe the pleasure of this company?"*

**[[NPCs/Anora|Anora]]** was formal to the point of comedy. She called Kaz *my lord*, since as her half-brother he was nobility by blood. *"That's going to take more getting used to than the Camille thing."* *"You are the Camille! How interesting."* She had heard the tales of many of her siblings. And how could she not know him, when he'd been in the guard here? *"Could be the numerous concussions."* She identified Ellion as a dryad at a glance, her first; her studies had proven fruitful. Pythor left them to it (the lever to get out is on the far side of the statue).

Kaz told her what they wanted: a way through this that didn't end with her sacrificed and the city razed. She had thought about it for a long time. As far as she could tell, the Lord of Storms has no motive at all. His servants read signs, and act on them. So: find a priest of Sydon who reads the signs differently. Or a substitute: oxen, something dear to the city. Or a mass ritual, the whole city praising the Lord together. She couldn't pursue any of it from a cell. The basilisks not coming might not be enough on its own. But if at the same moment it finally *rained* around Estoria… Could Ellion induce rain? With Thylea's help, perhaps? The Old Wood was a week there and a week back, maybe more.

Human sacrifices are quite common, she said, though there had been none in Estoria in her lifetime. That is part of the predicament.

And then the real worry. In two days the Order's delegation comes into the city and the palace. What did Kaz think would happen? Part of the delegation would demand her. The other part, she was *certain*, would try to take her. Pythor can't harm anyone he invites into his palace, and they can't harm anyone in it, but carrying her off isn't harming. The caves have other entrances. Her father's oversight, she said, needed correcting: get her out of the city.

To where? Kaz ran through everywhere they had been: the Oracle's temple, where the Oracle was nearly abducted; the road, where they were attacked twice; the farms outside, with cyclopes. Nowhere was safe, except perhaps his tribe up north, a long road away. *"You sound so much like your father."* *"I am his son."* *"That's no excuse."*

She was not running away to be sacrificed, she said. She was staying away until the day. If nothing worked, she would go there herself, one way or another. Did she already have an escape plan? *"I might."* Would Kaz run straight to their father with it? He'd hardly remember by tomorrow, the state he's in. *"You very much underestimate him."* Their father remembers everything and will use it; the whole drunk, sluggish king works for him. He is deep in his sorrows and trying to drink them out. *"Can you imagine how much alcohol a god needs to drown his sorrows?"*

Then Kaz had an idea. What if they swore an oath to deliver her to the sacrificial rock on the day, not to sacrifice her, just to deliver her? Then the delegation would have no reason to take her, or to pillage the city. Anora knew how that worked with centaurs. To swear such an oath and have it believed, a champion is appointed to fight *their* champion, and the gods show the truth through whoever wins. She didn't mean Kaz. Who else did he have? A bard, who is better at killing them than anyone else. A priest. Darius. *"That might be the best option. If the priest wins, that's a great sign."* Was he any good with a weapon? He had a spear, and a shield.

So Ellion, who was going out to the centaurs anyway, would carry the message: the city had a champion, a **priest of Lutheria**, ready to fight in two days' time to settle the terms. Once invited into their camp, by the laws of hospitality they would do him no harm. And if he brought a gift, better still. *"In return for this favor,"* Kaz offered on the side, *"Ellion will go on a date with you."*

She wanted to meet Darius before the fight, and see her hero face to face. He wears a helmet, Kaz warned, which he refuses to take off, due to an old family matter. *"Camille, do not be so literal. We really are family."*

One more request. Could Kaz tell her the wine festival play, in detail, acting out every part? They had a bard for that. Then bring everyone after the play. And the **Horn of Balmytria**, in the reliquary: drink from it *after* the priest's battle. No one who has drunk from it has told the tale. They all probably lived, but no one is saying.

Why, she asked, had he never come to meet her before? He'd been a city guard; he could have asked for an audience. He had, many times, and was denied every time by the bureaucrats. Names? He didn't wish them any harm; they were doing their jobs. *"That is not for them to decide. If you want to be the ruler of a city — which I plan to be — you shall not let people do wrong by you. And I feel I have been wronged."* Their other siblings had come to the palace while he stood outside. None of them live in Estoria. She wondered whether someone keeping a son of Pythor out, and someone selling Estoria out to Sydon's followers, might be the same someone. Kaz had had a prophecy since he was young that he'd be the one to reforge his father. *"It will be beneficial for you to remember those names, then."*

Had he met any of their other siblings? She was the first. *"Interesting. Because I did."*

*"Stay courageous. All will be well."* *"You sound just like Father."* He gave her arm a light, reassuring squeeze through the bars, and they left her standing there.

Kaz and Ellion talked on the way up. Kaz had slept over at Stivon and Stivona's place a few times; Athalea would be all right with them. He was better friends with the sister than with Stivon.

### The Vineyard

Athalea, on her last run, went looking for the vineyards the farmers had mentioned. They were the only thing still growing. East of the city, on the outskirts, she found one: wooden trellises in neat, symmetrical rows, healthy green vines, heavy with brilliant purple grapes, and no rain at all. A fit middle-aged man was pruning with great care. His face was friendly, and deeply furrowed with worry. These vines, he said, were blessed by Thylea herself when the city was founded.

It was his daughter. **Karina**, fourteen, long dark curly hair, olive skin, a birthmark on her back, in her foraging greens and browns with her herb bag, had gone into the **Old Wood** about a week ago on some errand and not come back. The Old Wood's heart is a week or two away, but its edge starts close by. The guard saw her last going out the southern gate, south-southeast, across the river. That was where the cyclopes had come from.

Something clicked for Athalea (History 22). Her prophecy: *the darkest parts of the forest.* She said she had a feeling she'd be in those parts, and promised to keep an eye out. His name was **[[NPCs/Thericles and Karina|Thericles]]**. He gave her a bunch of grapes for the road.

### The Dragon's Tooth

Darius, walking back toward the palace, went looking for gossip, and for the wine. There were barrels on every corner, all open, each with a cup on a string. He tried a few. Wine usually has no effect on him at all. But the barrel by the tavern called **the Dragon's Tooth** made him feel funny, suddenly tired and sleepy, yawning.

Inside, the tavern was packed: locals, travelers, gossip. On a Perception check (19) he picked up:

- A band of warriors went to the **Necropolis** last month and hasn't been heard from since. Entry is forbidden, which doesn't stop fortune-hunters, or archaeologists
- A **massive hurricane** is brewing on the coast of **Mytros**. It'll be weeks before it's safe to travel to the city; the nearby roads are closed. Weirdly, the hurricane seems to be *fiery*
- **Teenagers in the region have been disappearing** lately, Thericles's daughter among them
- Openly, at the next table: **Princess Anora would make a much better ruler** than her father. *"We love Pythor, but he's a terrible king."*

In the evening a few dwarves came in, dirty, with pickaxes, and went straight to the bar. Darius asked the bartender for something in small glasses. *"Are you sure?"* It was something very white called **Dragon's Breath**. He ordered a double, then another round for the gentlemen. It tasted like fire, and did nothing to him at all.

*"Mighty body you've got there, drinking like that. Never get drunk? That's a curse if I know one."* They were **[[NPCs/Brolin and Graham|Brolin]]** and **Graham**, Estoria born and raised, copper miners. Darius asked about the Mithral Forge. The mines are cursed, they said, and wouldn't advise going. Their people ran from there a very long time ago, and now they break their backs in the copper mines for nothing, but it's all they have. For anything more, go to **Volkan's Forge**, the city's weaponsmith: *they know everything*. They'd show him the copper mines themselves. Meet at the gate, four in the morning, sharp.

And one piece of advice from Brolin, with an elbow: *"Do not toast like that in this city."* A few people in the corner were already staring at him.

Eight shots of Dragon's Breath later, the dwarves were barely walking, and Darius had overdrunk two dwarves. Being gentlemen, they insisted on escorting him home, and he insisted on escorting them (Persuasion 2), so they went round the square in circles arguing about it. Kyrah went past on her way to the palace.

That was how Kaz and Ellion found him. *"Hey Darius, you want to meet the princess?"* The dwarves wanted to know which son of Pythor Kaz was. Kazak? Never heard of him. *"Camille."* *"Ah! Camille!"* There is, it seems, a list. Kaz wanted to steal a bowl of Athalea's stew from Stivon and Stivona's before the play, and asked Kyrah whether there was anyone else on her list who hadn't died to the boar. She knew of one other, chosen personally by the Oracle and raised by her. *"Raised from the dead?"* *"You are grim."* *"We are in a city under siege."*

In the end the dwarves won, and Darius went home with them.

### The Roof

Back at Helena's there was a lot of screaming, at the kids, and at the ungrateful guest who just ran out and did *that*. The roof was historic marble. Her grandfather built the house. Trees don't grow from nowhere, and that man was standing right there, and he's not into nature whatsoever.

Athalea talked her down (Persuasion 25): they'd collect all the marble scraps and fix it in time, and on the bright side, that was a lot of good-quality oak planks; new furniture is expensive, and this could make a table and a couple of chairs. There was plenty of stew. At which point the pot on the kitchen table started sliding toward the window. Athalea grabbed the handle and pulled it back. The hand outside belonged to Kaz. He was poor, he maintained, and hungry. He got one plate.

Stivon arrived. Kaz, the architect, cut the top of the tree down (you don't cut from the bottom; it would just fall), took off most of the branches, and built a wooden frame to stretch a tarp over, a temporary roof until the marble could be done.

Stivona came in, saw Kaz, went bright red, and ran out of the room.

### Stivona

It was obvious to everyone except Kaz. Kaz thought she was scared of him.

Athalea asked her what she saw in him. *"Everything. He's the kindest, most unselfish person I've ever met. He's taller than me. He's handsome. He trains like crazy. My brother hates him."* They'd grown up together, trained together, slept in the same barracks. She didn't want to ruin it. And why did he call her *beard lady*? Her own drunken idea, it turned out, to annoy her brother. Athalea took Kaz aside and told him not to do that any more, and to be gentle with her. *"Be a gentleman."* (In Kaz's experience, Stivona was the most ruthless person he'd ever met, and in sparring he had half-suspected she was a demigod.)

Then Kaz asked Stivon for a favor on Anora's behalf: extra guards in the dungeons, and a check of every other way in. As far as Stivon knew, all the other entrances had been covered when they made the dungeon. *"Probably a good idea to double-check."*

Athalea had less than half an hour to get Stivona ready. She braided Stivona's hair, fast and ornate, and tucked a few of her own feathers into it. Jewelry from a box under the bed: copper pieces for the shoulders and hair, something for the neck. Stivona had outgrown her parade toga, so Athalea made a new one from a bedsheet, rough in a charming way, *like her*, flowing over one shoulder, pinned with one of Athalea's own brooches. For a mirror, she made a cube of ice out of a bucket of water. A little color on the lashes. It took less than ten minutes. *"Is that what people like?"* *"Depends on the people. Do you like it?"* *"I usually just wear my uniform."*

Meanwhile Athalea asked Helena, quietly, about sexual education. *"Nature will take its course,"* said Helena, and Stivona heard.

Kaz arrived at the door in a full suit with flowers. Stivon stood in the doorway staring at it. *"What are your intentions?"* *"To be a gentleman."* *"To whom?"* *"Everyone. But very specifically to your sister."* Kaz leaned in and whispered a threat of his own: one wrong step, and an arrow, no bow required, would go through each of Stivon's balls. Stivon was taking Athalea.

*"Stivona. You look… wow. Beautiful. Not that you aren't always beautiful. These are for you."* Kaz offered her his arm, and her instincts nearly threw him to the ground (Constitution 23). Everyone could see what was in his trousers: Betsy, just in case. He felt naked without her. Stivona was bringing poison daggers.

### The Steppe

Ellion, after the dungeon, had a maid show him a room in the palace, took three bottles of the world's best wine, and went looking for herbs. The shop was closed for the festival, so he walked out of the northern gate to forage. Beyond it the grassland stretched to the horizon under a perfectly cloudless sky, with the sun going down: beautiful, and full of danger.

There was no centaur camp to be seen. There were figures moving far away (Survival 14). So Ellion found the only local he could, a very fat and very anxious mouse with enormous cheeks, and turned into a mouse himself to talk to it.

It was full of warnings: rocks falling from the sky. *"Brother says it shall be fire. We shall gather at the fire of Thylea once more. She shall awaken. We shall have food in abundance. One of us, one of us, one of us."* Where were the horse-people? Everywhere. (The party had a guess who the fire-preaching brother might be: Master Flamster.)

Back on two legs, he walked on and came across a patrol of centaurs galloping about a hundred feet off. He called out in Sylvan, asking them to take him to their settlement. A rain of arrows thunked into the ground at his feet. Seven centaurs, bows drawn for a second volley. He raised his open hands. He was a dryad.

A [[NPCs/Hylonome|centaur woman]] stepped forward, carrying a sheaf of spears and hung with the skulls of many animals. *"Prove you are."* He spoke the words of his order. She murmured something and turned into a giant scorpion; it hissed at him, and turned back. So he turned into a giant wolf spider and waved at her. She gestured, and the bows came down.

*"What do you do far from home, child of Thylea?"* The steppe was dangerous, their tribes and the cyclopes roamed it, and there were rocks falling from the sky. He offered wine as a gift. She didn't accept it yet. Why would a dryad rest in a city instead of the woods? Because he travelled with four companions who don't like forests: a siren, a medusa…

*"You travel with a cursed one."* They would purge him from the land. *"Bring the cursed one to us. Once you do, we shall see it as a gift."*

Ellion dug. Was the cursed one in the city? He had parted from him about three hours ago. He told her about the champion and the terms, and more besides. She whistled, and whistles answered her from all across the steppe. They were not interested in Estoria's champions; Estoria is blasphemous and has none. Then bring him. *"My patience is running thin, child of Thylea."*

Their terms: **tonight, before sundown**, he was to bring them the cursed one and the city's champion, the one he claimed was chosen. If not, he should be out of the city by sundown, for his own safety. *"We will find him ourselves."*

### The Amphitheater

The open-air theater was built into the bottom of a hill, with stone rows climbing the slope. Ellion was waiting at the meeting point. Once he'd dragged them into an alley, it all came out, including, at a very carrying volume, the part about the centaurs storming the city.

Stivon and Stivona both stood up very straight. *"Are we at war?"* No, they just had to show the centaurs that the city really had a champion, before sundown. Which champion? Darius, apparently. *"He's not a champion."* Who knew the address of the two dwarves? Stivon wrote it down. Did they need to raise the alarm? In twenty minutes most of the guard would be drunk. The highest code was black, and black requires telling the king first. Kaz asked Stivon to get everyone on the walls quietly, without an actual alarm; if the centaurs didn't know the city knew, maybe the city could surprise them. Stivon took his sister by the hand and went to the palace, and there went the date. Athalea drank two cups of wine one after the other and flew off to find the dwarves' house.

It was a big building with several floors. Behind the door someone was playing something very slowly, very badly. Kaz lifted the door off its hinges (Athletics), just as a dwarf came to open it. Athalea: *"Good evening, nice to meet you, we're here to take our friend home."* *"Darius, get your ass out here now!"* *"Code red! It's an emergency. He needs to take his medicine."* A roomful of very drunk dwarves watched them go, and the architect put the door back on its hinges.

On the way to the gate they explained the plan to Darius. He would be fighting the centaurs' champion. *"You sold my ass to a bunch of centaurs."* It wasn't selling, they said; it was a charitable donation. *"Friends, you don't need enemies."* The plan was supposed to be put to him first, Kaz said, not done immediately. Ellion had had other ideas. *"They want to f— kill me. What will change in two days? Do you think I'll get significantly stronger?"*

Did the centaurs need the champion to fight tonight, or only to see that he existed? Only to see him, and to believe. Could the champion appoint someone else to do the actual fighting? Kaz would fight; Darius would do the pretending. Kyrah went by with a big piece of meat on the bone, heading for the theater. Had she ever met a champion of the Titans? Nobody has seen one in ages.

What would prove it? Dying and coming back would. Kyrah had no scrolls. She could make another, but it would take her two to three business years. Would Pythor's blood work instead? About three liters of it. Pythor isn't that important, said Kyrah. He has an heir. And Anora would be less inclined to jump the gun if she had the crown.

There were about seven minutes to sundown, so it was time for a rehearsal. *"You need to look regal and ethereal. You are the chosen of death itself. You are not scared of death whatsoever. Death is your friend. You embrace it. Chin up."* What would he say to them? How would he stand? Two minutes to write a script, and one minute to learn it.

Before they went out, Darius took a small vial from a pocket. Something bubbled inside it. He opened it and breathed in all the vapor, and for a moment his eyes turned white, then back.

Tonight, the whole city depended on his performance. No pressure.
