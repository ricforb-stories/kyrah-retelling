# Session 3 — June 26, 2026 — "The Wisdom of Mr. Flufster"

**Level:** 1
**Party Members in Attendance:** [[The Party/Ellion|Ellion]], [[The Party/Darius|Darius]], [[The Party/Xhazag (Kaz)|Xhazag (Kaz)]], [[The Party/Athalea|Athalea]]

## Our Adventure

### Shelter in the Storm

The rain and thunder came back as the party gathered at the ruined farmhouse. The family had gone, leaving a good many sacks of seed behind, and it was far too late — and far too wet — to go tracking a boar. [[The Party/Athalea|Athalea]], still furious, dragged the abandoned sacks under what was left of the roof, dried them with *shape water*, and cursed people who leave their belongings lying around.

The problem was the house. [[The Party/Xhazag (Kaz)|Kaz]]'s keen eye for architecture had already established that if he set foot in it, it would come down; the cellar could only be reached across floor that Athalea had put an axe through the day before. So Kaz stood in the rain holding the fur of his cloak — the pelt of a huge white cat-like beast nobody could name, its head still hanging at his side — up on a stick like an umbrella, and waited for someone to decide something. [[NPCs/Kyrah|Kyrah]] shoved her bag of scrolls into his hands to keep them dry.

Walking back to the tavern would take an hour or more in the dark and the rain, and they had, as someone pointed out, just angered Sydon. So Kaz built a tent: pieces of unburned fence for a frame, his own bedroll cut open along a seam for the roof, another bedroll for a floor, and Athalea's *shape water* to dry the whole thing out. It fit about two people. He meant it for the others — *"It would be weird if I made myself a tent and left others hanging. Especially considering you saved our lives"* — and planned to sleep under a tree himself; he's been a soldier, he can sleep anywhere. [[The Party/Ellion|Ellion]] pointed out that lightning strikes trees, and he lives in the forest, so he'd know. In the end Kaz wore the tent, and Ellion and [[The Party/Darius|Darius]] slept piled on top of him.

Darius had to take his armor off, lying down, in a tent, on top of a minotaur. It went badly (Acrobatics 4): everyone got stepped on, and as he reached down to remove his breastplate, for one short moment, one of the others saw two little eyes looking back at them. Under the plate he wears soft, well-made clothes of good fabric — white, embroidered with blue ribbons — and his skin is very, very white. He kept the helmet on. It was very stylish, Athalea said, peering in; she couldn't really see. Kaz couldn't have told the colors either: he sees in shades of gray, except for red. (So the arrow Kyrah had promised each of them for their help, he learned, was made of gold. He had agreed to help to save people anyway.)

Kaz, it turned out, names his things. His club is Betsy. And the white pelt he wears is **Tsirmi**, who was once a companion: he freed her from a trap, befriended her, and then she died. He didn't want to talk about it.

### Goodberries and Bacon

Ellion conjured goodberries — magic food that tastes of nothing but will keep you going — and Athalea, the only one who could get in and out of the cellar without bringing the house down, ferried them around and stored the spares in an empty seed sack. Kaz paid her a coin for her courier services, and then produced from one of his pockets a strip of bacon, already cooked and dried. *"Nothing puts into perspective the little niceties in life like dying."*

Athalea went through his pockets and found they went on forever: a tinderbox, three burned-out torches, a full waterskin, a small metal military insignia of an axe and a sword crossed, four javelins he had been looking for for ages, and eventually a whole backpack.

Asked about his father, Kaz said Pythor was not in the best of moods lately, but he aimed to fix that. It's a three-step plan: kill a dragon, rescue the damsel, and make something magnificent. Which damsel? He would tell them tomorrow — he was tuckered out from being struck by lightning. It wasn't a happy memory. Darius, asked his age, said twenty-one; the others thought that was a lot of sad moments for so few years.

Kaz fell asleep almost instantly, and in his sleep he wrapped his arms around whoever was on top of him, like a teddy bear.

### The Upside-Down Tree

Before bed, Ellion went down into the cellar to look at the tree growing from its ceiling. It was an apple tree, upside down, nearly touching the floor, with a few small brownish leaves — the brown of a plant that has never seen the sun. He cut into the bark with a dagger to see whether it was an ordinary tree underneath.

That was a bad idea. There was a murmur from inside it, like a whisper, and Ellion leaned in close — and failed his Wisdom save. The voice came through his mind in his own voice:

***"I can't decide whether they should live or die."***

His hand began to rise with a tremor in it. Then there was a crumbling sound: the tree tore its roots out of the ceiling and fell, crumbling almost to ash, leaving holes behind. The whisper came again, and his other hand rose too.

Ellion tried to tell whether this might be Thylea — he is a druid of the Mother. But he had never actually heard her voice; the Mother had abandoned this place. He couldn't say. *"I also cannot decide whether you should live or die."* He tried to force his hands down (Wisdom check: 2). Something that wasn't quite control, but was forcing him to do what he didn't want to, turned his thoughts first to Darius — and what it wanted was simple. *Go up. Remove the helmet.*

He climbed out of the cellar, walked through the rain to the tent, and cast *entangle*. Roots burst out of the ground and grabbed everyone in the tent, and every Strength save failed; they were all very much entangled, and at least it was warmer. Athalea, awake, asked what exactly he thought he was doing. He didn't know. Then he pulled Darius's helmet off like a band-aid.

Nobody else in the tent could see a thing. The tremor in his hands eased as he stood there in the rain holding the helmet, and his mind cleared. He didn't understand what had just happened. He dropped the *entangle*.

What was under the helmet began to move. The snakes slithered out, and while the helmet was off they bit the nearest thing — Kaz, fast asleep underneath, who never felt it and took no harm. Putting the helmet back on went worse for Ellion: one of them bit him as he shoved it down, three points of piercing damage. *"The tree started to speak with me."* The tree had said something about killing them, and in his mind taking the helmet off had meant stopping that. Athalea decided she didn't want to think about it — Darius was a good boy, and that was that.

Ellion went back down to the tree's remains. There was no crystal, no gem, no seed, nothing: just ashes. Athalea built herself a nest of empty sacks in the cellar and slept with only her nose and a couple of feathers showing. The night was full of noises — bird noises, barn noises, and whatever sounds a sleeping minotaur makes.

### Breakfast

Athalea woke first and cooked. She made a fire in the cellar under the hatch she'd cut, filled the pots with rainwater and rye, scoured the ruined house for salt, bowls and spoons, and asked Kaz whether he had any meat in his pockets. He did — a chunk of raw meat, still good — and she shredded it with her talons with alarming skill, *"I know my way around meat,"* and made a berry drink to go with it. She served it like a line cook. Kaz had slept with his hooves sticking out of the end of the tent all night.

Kaz woke with small marks on him — bites, from very small teeth. There shouldn't have been any bites on him at all. The party asked Darius, pointedly, whether he had needles for teeth. Kaz, for his part, said Darius had been licking him in the night without asking consent, which is important, and that he hadn't enjoyed it; he prefers a real connection first.

Darius made coffee. Nobody else had ever seen coffee; they were fairly sure he was drinking mud.

### The Morning Sacrifices

Under the Oath of Peace every mortal owes the Titans a sacrifice every day, and they each made theirs.

- **Kaz** went off to the side and, trying and failing to be quiet, dedicated his exercise to Lutheria — out loud, counting to a hundred.
- **Athalea**'s is discreet: before every sip she kisses the rim of her cup, drinks, and sits in contemplation — sometimes for half an hour.
- **Ellion** cut a small piece of his own bark and buried it at the foot of the nearest standing tree, asking for protection in the days ahead. Usually he offers trophies of the hunt, or seeds; but they were going through serious things, and it needed to be bigger.
- **Darius** untied one of the ribbons from his clothing and burned it for Lutheria. Every time he prays, he ties a ribbon; at the sacrifice, he gives one up.

It was still raining. The party suggested that Darius, who had caused it, offer Sydon something that truly mattered to him — a piece of armor, a weapon, something from his past — to stop it. He asked why he should. Since it had to be sincere, and he clearly wasn't, and the last attempt had killed them, they decided to walk in the rain.

### The Flask

Athalea wanted a drink before setting out. Kaz directed her to his back left pocket — three sips of wine, all she could have. What came out was a flask with a bird's skull attached to it, pointed and very deliberate, and what was inside smelled of blood and metal. He swore it hadn't been there when he packed and it wasn't his. Someone might have planted it, like the tree — someone who wanted to sow discord. He had slept in the corner of the tavern the night before the hunt; anyone could have reached his pockets. That was the wrong flask; the right one held something much stronger than wine, and Athalea took a sip and found it very relaxing.

Kaz took down the tent, and carried the bedroll over Kyrah like an umbrella for the rest of the day.

### From the Air

Athalea flew up to scout — not quite in a straight line, with a spin and a spiral and a moment where she forgot to flap. From up there she saw the farmland stretching almost to the end of the island on one side, the ocean on the other, the tavern, hills and plains beyond it and the tiny markings of a city far off, and a sparse forest. The forest was intact: no broken trees, no trails, nothing that looked like a beast the size of a building had passed through.

The party's only clue was the hunters' word that the boar was drawn to rotten fruit. Beyond that, they had very little: no hunter among them, and nothing in anyone's memory (Athalea's history check included) about Titans sending cursed beasts. The Titans are petty, but they are direct — lightning in your face. This had no style to it at all, and they were supposed to be bound by the Oath until its end. Asked whether earlier groups had worked any of this out, Kyrah wouldn't say. *"It is your story. I'm not telling you whatever others thought."* Some had brought traps. One had fallen into its own.

On the road Kaz finally told them about the damsel: his mother, taken by a green dragon twenty-five years ago. He had never met his father. It was a personal thing, he said — but he was sure he could save the world and do this one thing for himself.

### The Rabbit

Ellion found a rabbit and approached slowly, telling it he was a druid and meant no harm (Animal Handling 15). The conversation went: *Big boar. Scary. Danger. Where? Where?* The rabbit knew nothing, and then it saw Athalea overhead and fled screaming. Rabbits, it seems, consider birds the greater danger.

On the ground there were no broken branches, but there was blood mixed into the mud and smeared low on the bark of the trees, near the roots.

### Mr. Flufster

From the air Athalea found the next animal: a **sugar glider**, which had clearly never seen anything like her and simply stared (17, with advantage). It landed on her hand and lay spread out like a tiny blanket. *Warm.* Ellion asked it about the boar. *Boar. Danger. There.* It declined to show them where.

Then Darius touched it with *guidance*, and the little creature's mind lit up.

*"I see the light. I see the meaning of things. What is this, mother? Why do you wish to bring such a dangerous situation upon yourselves, mortals?"*

It knew the craft of a druid and refused goodberries as payment. It wanted **the blood of its enemies** — and its enemy was the boar. It had a name, too, once it had decided they were worthy: **Mr. Flufster** — the Second, the Third, the First. Its terms, relayed by Ellion:

- Darius was to touch it with his divine radiance, over and over, *"so that the light of my life never fails"*
- It was to be kept dry — Athalea's job, with a floating umbrella of water
- It wanted blood: enough to be covered in it. Ellion promised to sacrifice blood of its enemies in its name
- It would also, ideally, like Darius as its slave

Darius agreed to keep touching it until they found the boar. Every time the *guidance* ran out, Mr. Flufster went back to being a small, confused animal — *"our compass is broken"* — and every time it came back, it screamed at them to touch it again.

Mr. Flufster had opinions. The party were simple creatures. Darius was brain-dead. Their questions were stupid, and he had asked them not to ask them. He led them for half an hour through the forest and then turned them almost straight back the way they had come, and when Ellion asked why, he was told to stop asking stupid questions. With some effort (Investigation 17), Ellion worked it out: there was a beast nearby, and the squirrel was walking them *around* it. Athalea, listening hard, had heard it too — about forty feet ahead, the sound of something eating, flesh being pulled apart like strings breaking. It was not the boar. It was a different beast, and Mr. Flufster wanted it avoided.

When Kaz later said he would stop touching him, Mr. Flufster tried to leave in a huff. He demanded to be treated with respect. They called him Mr. Flufster. Kyrah, being used as a pillow by Kaz's umbrella arm, had never been so worshipped in her life.

### Plans

On the way the party tried to plan a fight with a boar the size of a building. Surround it, so that if it tramples someone it only tramples one of them. Look for weak points before engaging — an amulet, an old wound, something glowing, the eye. And if none of that turned up, hit it until it died. Athalea would stay airborne; she's more of a poetry girl than a fighter. Darius has a spear and channels his goddess, Mytros. Athalea can form knives from water. Ellion can throw small fires. Kaz is the muscle. *"It is the size of a building." "So am I."*

Mr. Flufster was issued a small knife, the kind used for cutting food. It was explained that he'd be safest in a pocket during the fight. He did not know what a pocket was.

Kaz covered himself in mud as war paint, to mask his scent. Mr. Flufster's review, translated: *big stinky wet cow.* Kaz let it go, as the creature had been helping them.

### The Boar's Country

Mr. Flufster led them north through the Highlands, past the burned farm and further along the same road, until they reached other farms — not burned, but ravaged. Then a ravaged settlement that looked, Athalea thought, like the aftermath of a meteor shower. Rocks and trees along the road had been gouged deep, probably by tusks, and crows flocked over the fields, picking at the bodies of fallen hunters and unlucky settlers.

Then Mr. Flufster made a sign: *stop.*
