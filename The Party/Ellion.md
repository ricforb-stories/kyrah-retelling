## Summary

A forest nymph druid born from an oak, who paints his skin every day and talks to birds, trees and beasts. Versi foretold he will ride at the head of a flight of reborn dragons, and revealed that he is her kin: his grandmother was a sorceress the storm god loved. He carries her silver locket. Sent home under centaur escort after the Vigil, he came back to Estoria alone, and now carries a cursed emerald necklace from the Necropolis that keeps him from eating.

## Background

A forest nymph — a dryad, as the table calls him — born from an oak: he grew inside his birth tree for about a hundred years and came out some twenty years ago. A druid of the Mother, Thylea. Before Kyrah's letter he had left his small patch of forest around his druid circle only once, to visit a shop with the other druids; he had never met another of his kind. Paints his skin every day (a custom of his people), talks to birds, trees and beasts, and bleeds clear sap that ambers as it dries.

## Epic Path

**Prophecy** (Versi, Session 08 - Keep Me Close): he is meant to ride at the forefront of a flight of reborn dragons. He and Versi are **kin**: Sydon, Lord of All, loved a mortal sorceress — Ellion's grandmother — and Lutheria, Sydon's sister-wife, convinced him to have her murdered. Ellion must avenge her, and her spirit will guide him. Versi gave him his grandmother's silver locket. He recognized Versi as a naiad — his cousin.

- Wants to find where the story of Sydon and the sorceress is kept (an old affair, perhaps a hundred years ago); Athalea suggested the academy in Mytros or the archive in Estoria

## Who He Is (as established at the table)

- **Nature:** everyone and everything is part of nature, and creatures forgot it. Feels dying and burning plant life as his own pain; grass withers around him when he's hurt. Won't help humans casually — "humans are invaders" (Session 06 - The Door That Didn't Ask for Consent); won't kill nesting birds or orphan chicks
- **Daily sacrifice:** usually hunting trophies or seeds; in hard times, a piece of his own bark
- **Magic:** goodberries, *entangle*, *thunderwave*, *magic stones* (his signature), *ice knife*, *stinking cloud*, *purify food and drink*, bonfires; wild shapes so far — giant spider, giant toad, giant badger, mouse, giant wolf spider. Sacrifices creatures to the Mother mid-fight for blessings (a steam mephit, two goatlings). Will gamble his own vigor for spells he doesn't have (three levels of exhaustion, Session 7)
- **Lost:** his druidic focus (a mistletoe branch) was stolen in the Estorian dungeon (Session 11 - With Friends Like These)
- **Blue:** since the maenad fight his skin is solid blue (cause unknown to the party)
- Has free passage in and out of Estoria and a room in the palace (Pythor, Session 11)
