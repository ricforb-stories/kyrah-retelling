## Summary

A seven-foot minotaur barbarian, son of Pythor, God of Battle, who calls him Camille. He hunts the green dragon that carried off his mother, and has sworn to his father not to free his half-sister Anora, the city's promised sacrifice; Pythor's hammer is his if she is saved. He has lost his horn twice, never lets go of his club Betsy, and was kissed by Stivona. He announced their wedding; her mother Helena beat him to his last breath, dismissed him from the guard and forbade him her daughter. His grandfather Volkan will forge for him.

## Background

A mortal son of Pythor, God of Battle. Named "son of the god of battle" in the Session 1 invocation. A seven-foot minotaur, mechanically **Large** (established Session 08 - Keep Me Close). His mother named him **Kazak**; nobody can say "Xhazag"; people call him "Horny" by mistake; he prefers **Kaz**. Pythor calls him **"Camille"**, which he insists is the name he gave him (Session 10 - By Not Being There).

## Epic Path

**Prophecy** (Versi, Session 08 - Keep Me Close; word for word the one he was told as a child, which is why it didn't move him): go to your divine parent, now King of Estoria, drowned in wine and grief; forge the weapon he could never wield; defeat the ancient foe he could never face; save the love he lost; outshine him in heroism, perhaps into godhood.

- **Find his mother, Ophea**, carried off by a green dragon twenty-five years ago. Pythor says he tried three times to find her
- **Defeat Pythor's greatest foe, the green dragon Hexia.** Kaz asked Kyrah where it lairs (Session 08 - Keep Me Close); she didn't know
- **Forge the weapon.** Pythor has promised him **his hammer** (made by Volkan; "it did not serve me") to reforge into Kaz's own weapon **if he saves Anora** (Session 10 - By Not Being There)
- Reward: redeem Pythor from his drunken despair

## Who He Is (as established at the table)

- **Look and kit:** no armor above the waist; a cloak made from **Tsirmi**, a huge white cat-like beast — once his companion, freed from a trap, now dead; he won't talk about it. Club **Betsy** (named in Session 03 - The Wisdom of Mr. Flufster), a handaxe, javelins, bottomless pockets (bacon, a soldier's insignia of crossed axe and sword, a whole backpack). His lost horn grew back in Session 2; he broke it off again in Session 7 and keeps "a spare"; he wears a **nose ring** Athalea pierced (Session 08 - Keep Me Close)
- **Sees in grey, except red** (Session 03 - The Wisdom of Mr. Flufster)
- **Tribe:** pacifist, atoning for their people's original sin; courts with poems and labor, never gifts of living things. His father wrote his mother ten poems. His people's early history was lost "during the time we spent as actual cows" (Session 08 - Keep Me Close). Tans hides with seagull eggs
- **Life:** left the tribe after his mother was taken; came to Estoria at fifteen and served as a soldier/guard; later a mercenary who takes contracts on lives — never children, never sacrifices, never silencing. Asked for an audience with his father for years and was always refused by the palace bureaucrats. His family vanished about five years before the campaign, with no trace
- **Style:** eloquent on purpose (his mother told him the world looks down on their kind); "the party's architect" (a running bit that keeps being useful); names his things; opens doors from five feet away with the far end of Betsy since the door bit him; pacifist when his head is clear — "I only kill when my mind is clouded"
- Makes **"flaming water"** from rocks, water and a few more ingredients
- **Resurrection costs carried:** burned legs from Sydon's lightning — **5 ft speed**, DC 10 Dex save after Dashing or fall prone, until magical healing or ten days' rest (still "someone burned my ass" by Session 10 - By Not Being There); two forks of lightning scar across his back
