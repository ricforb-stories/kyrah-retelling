## Summary

A siren bard whose whole flock vanished while she was away, leaving her suspected of their deaths and half-believed mad. Versi told her they may yet be saved, and pointed her toward Versi's sister Demetria in the darkest part of the forest. She is the party's voice, healer and keeper of rites, and is fighting for the unconscious Kyrah: she has told Helena and Volkan that Kyrah is the goddess of music, and Versi has answered her: blood of her blood, then sing, then hurry.

## Background

A siren bard, "born already mourning" (Session 1 invocation): her flock was destroyed while she was away, and for years she has been half-suspected of killing them and half-believed mad. Flies, fights from above, and does the party's talking, healing and funerals.

## Epic Path

**Prophecy** (Versi, Session 08 - Keep Me Close): the silver light of dawn shielded her from the wrath that fell on her family; their memory clings to her like ghosts; **they may yet be saved** — Versi does not see their faces. She must retrieve the artifacts of her house and seek out Versi's sister **Demetria** in the darkest part of the forest, whose servants brought her a relic stolen from Athalea's family. *"So I'm not crazy."*

- **Lead (Session 11 - With Friends Like These):** Karina, the vintner Thericles's daughter, vanished into the Old Wood a week ago, south-southeast across the river where the cyclopes came from; tavern rumor says teenagers are disappearing across the region. Athalea tied it to her prophecy (History 22)

## Who She Is (as established at the table)

- **Flock:** one day the village was simply empty — nobody hurt, nothing burned, no mess — while she was away (Session 07 - The Ransom of a Horn). Where she came from, the rumor that she killed them is about the only thing people talk about
- **Siren life:** six women to every man in her flock; women court women with dances under the water, the men dance round the fire. A minotaur woman named Mal once courted her; she said no and kept the feather. Her people don't build in stone, so as not to be smitten by a god again; most of their songs are sad. She lays eggs. Harpies were sirens whose joy was taken by one Titan and handed back by Lutheria with nothing else beside it
- **Past:** years among Lutheria's followers (the Raving Ones; "five years" by her count in Session 9) — she knows maenads, homunculi and the parties in the caves; she lectured on poetry at the University of Mytros for a while; now "keeps people company" — cards, dice, conversation, and generous donations (Session 08 - Keep Me Close)
- **Skills:** *shape water* (ice blocks, knives, an ice-cube mirror), *feather fall*, *thunderwave*, *message*, *healing word*, vicious mockery, a bow; advantage on Persuasion with her siren's voice; holds her tribe's memory, which predates the Five (Session 10 - By Not Being There); History proficiency
- **Habits:** her daily sacrifice is kissing her cup's rim before each sip and sitting in contemplation; smokes herbs in a pipe; shreds meat with her talons "like a line cook"; insists she never hunts and never fights, and is alarming with a knife
- **Carries:** the steam mephit bottle, two of the five dragonlord coins, the maenad's vial, the silvery feathered trinket bearing a goddess's sign (Session 1 payment)
