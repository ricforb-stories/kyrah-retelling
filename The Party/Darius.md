## Summary

A cleric of the Children of the Sacred Serpents who never takes off his armor, because he is a medusa, cursed through an elf ancestor. Versi told him the punishment does not fit the crime and sent him to the silent guardian of Telamok to learn the curse's true nature. At the Vigil he played the Lady's champion before a thousand centaurs, died, rose, and was carried off by the walking figure; Athalea found him in an alley. He came back from the underworld a second time and means to go to the Necropolis; Stivona arrested him for saying he was cursed, and Helena has now seen him uncovered.

## Background

A cleric in full armor and helmet who will not be touched or uncover his body before any living creature — because he is a **medusa**, snakes for hair. The curse traces to an elf ancestor who came to Thylea from outside and did something to the goddess Mytros generations ago (he isn't sure exactly what); it runs in his family — himself, a brother and a sister — shows at sixteen, and he has never met anyone with it older than twenty-five, because they are hunted. His order, **the Children of the Sacred Serpents** of Mytros, takes in orphans and the cursed to give them a chance.

## Epic Path

**Prophecy** (Versi, Session 08 - Keep Me Close): the cursed one whose veins run black with the foulest of hexes, *and punishments disproportionate to the crimes committed ages ago*; he must travel to the City of the Dead and speak with the silent guardian who watches over all of **Telamok**, who was there when the curse was placed and can reveal its true nature. What shook him was the phrase in the middle: in a life inside an order built on the curse being earned, no one had ever said the punishment didn't fit.

- The Necropolis is guarded by the undead gatekeeper Damon, paid in dragonlord coins; a band of warriors went there last month and hasn't come back (Session 11 - With Friends Like These)

## Who He Is (as established at the table)
